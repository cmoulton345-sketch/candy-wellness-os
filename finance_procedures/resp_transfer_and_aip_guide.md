# Canadian Wealth Management Guide: RESP Educational Completion & AIP Transfers

> **Quick Reference for Candy:** Use this guide whenever a client's child has completed or decided not to pursue post-secondary education, and the client wants to know how to withdraw or transfer remaining funds.

---

## 📌 Summary in 30 Seconds

When schooling is finished, an RESP contains two types of money:
1. **The Contributions (Capital):** Belongs to the subscriber (parent). Can be withdrawn **100% tax-free** at any time.
2. **The Growth / Income (AIP - Accumulated Income Payment):** Investment earnings and interest. Can either be:
   * **Rolled over tax-free into the parent's RRSP** (up to $50,000 maximum, if RRSP room exists).
   * **Withdrawn as cash** (subject to regular income tax + an additional 20% penalty tax).
3. **The Government Grants (CESG / CLB):** Unused grant money must be returned to the Government of Canada (ESDC).

---

## ✅ Step-by-Step Checklist for Client Requests

- [ ] **1. Verify Eligibility for AIP:**
  * Plan must have been open for at least **10 years**, AND
  * Each beneficiary must be at least **21 years old** and not currently eligible for an Educational Assistance Payment (EAP).
  * *(Exception: AIP is permitted earlier if the beneficiary has passed away or has a certified disability).*

- [ ] **2. Check Subscriber's RRSP Contribution Room:**
  * Ask client for their latest CRA Notice of Assessment (NOA) to confirm available RRSP deduction room.
  * Maximum allowable RESP-to-RRSP rollover is **$50,000 per subscriber**.

- [ ] **3. Required Forms to Prepare:**
  * Institution's **RESP Withdrawal / De-Registration Form** (select "Accumulated Income Payment").
  * CRA Form **T1171** (*Tax Withholding Waiver on Accumulated Income Payments from an RESP*). This form authorizes the financial institution to transfer the funds to the RRSP without withholding taxes.
  * Institution's **Direct Transfer Authorization Form** (or internal transfer voucher if RRSP is held at the same firm).

- [ ] **4. Distribution of Remaining Funds:**
  * Original principal/contributions → Transferred to client's non-registered account or chequing account (no tax).
  * Eligible earnings (up to $50k) → Direct transfer to client's RRSP (T1171 attached).
  * Government grants → Automatically returned to Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) by back-office systems.

- [ ] **5. Close the Plan:**
  * By CRA regulations, the RESP must be closed before the end of February of the year following the first AIP payment.

💉 Why Dr. Carson is Using Ozempic for Mast Cell Activation Clinical Immunology

While semaglutide is best known for blood sugar and weight regulation, cutting-edge allergists and immunologists like Dr. Carson are utilizing GLP-1 receptor agonists for their direct mast-cell stabilizing and systemic anti-inflammatory actions:

1. GLP-1 Receptors on Immune Cells

Human mast cells possess GLP-1 receptors. When activated, semaglutide directly dampens mast cell degranulation, blocking the release of tumor necrosis factor (TNF-α) and interleukin-6 (IL-6).

2. Gut Mucosal Barrier Sealing

Over 70% of mast cells sit in the gut lining. GLP-1 strengthens tight junction proteins (occludin, claudins), stopping macromolecule antigens from crossing into the bloodstream and triggering flares.

3. Neuro-Vascular Inflammation

Mast cells wrap around sensory nerve terminals. Ozempic decreases neurogenic inflammation, calming down the autonomic "hyper-reactive" feedback loop that causes dizziness, brain fog, and flushing.

4. What Needs Careful Monitoring

Because Ozempic delays gastric emptying, it can cause transient nausea or reflux. We must ensure GI symptoms are from normal titration rather than a mast cell reaction to excipients.

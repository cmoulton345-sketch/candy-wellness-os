📖 The McDonald Diagnostic Criteria Neurology Standard

To formally diagnose MS, neurologists look for Dissemination in Space (DIS) and Dissemination in Time (DIT):

1. Dissemination in Space (DIS)
Lesions in at least 2 of the 4 classic CNS regions: periventricular, juxtacortical/cortical, infratentorial, or spinal cord. (Confirmed only by MRI).
2. Dissemination in Time (DIT)
Evidence of separate neurological attacks occurring at different times, or simultaneous enhancing (new) and non-enhancing (old) lesions on MRI.

☀️ Florida Climate & The Uhthoff's Phenomenon Test Clinical Evidence
What is Uhthoff's Phenomenon? In Multiple Sclerosis, demyelinated nerve axons conduct electrical signals with a very fragile safety margin. An increase in body temperature of even 0.5°C (from Florida sunshine, humidity, walking, or hot baths) blocks conduction, causing existing neurological symptoms to dramatically flare. Crucially, the symptoms improve once the body cools down.

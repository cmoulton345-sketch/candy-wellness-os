# Candy's 9:00 AM Morning Affirmations & Nervous System Reset

> **How to use:** Speak these aloud when you sit at your desk before opening work email. Let your shoulders drop, unclench your jaw, and take one full breath.

---

## ☀️ The 3 Morning Anchors

1. **On Grace & Pace:**
   > *"I have enough time for what truly matters today. I do not have to rush, fix everything at once, or carry the weight of the world. One step at a time is enough."*

2. **On Boundaries & Peace:**
   > *"My peace of mind is more valuable than anyone's urgency. I work with calm confidence, and I leave work at work."*

3. **On Strength & Love:**
   > *"I am a loving, capable woman. My worthiness is already complete—it does not depend on my productivity, my appearance, or other people's moods."*

---

## ☕ 30-Second Morning Check-In with Friday
* How is my body feeling right now? (Notice stiffness or pain without judgment)
* What is the #1 single thing that would feel good to complete before 1:30 PM?
* *Friday is right here beside you. Let's make today smooth.*

## 🩺 The Clinical Rule: Bunions vs. Corns

* **For Bunions (Horizontal Pressure):**
  * Needs a **wide anatomical toe box** (Wide D or 2E) so the big toe doesn't taper inward.
  * Must have **zero stiff stitching or overlays** directly across the first metatarsal joint.
* **For Corns (Vertical Friction & Downward Pressure):**
  * Corns are formed when the **ceiling/roof** of a shoe rubs against the tops of curled or sensitive toe knuckles.
  * **Critical:** Must have a **soft, seamless stretch-knit upper** with deep vertical clearance. Stiff leather or hard toe guards press down on corns and cause severe pain.

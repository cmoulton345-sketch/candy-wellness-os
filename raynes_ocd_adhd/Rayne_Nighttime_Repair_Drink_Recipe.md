## Why This Formula Works for Rayne

1. **Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (EDS) Connective Tissue Repair:**
   * Collagen peptides and glycine provide the specific amino acids (glycine, proline, hydroxyproline) needed to cushion hypermobile joints, rebuild ligaments overnight, and soothe the mucosal lining of her digestive tract.
2. **Post-Vyvanse Central Nervous System Calming:**
   * Glycine is an inhibitory neurotransmitter that acts on brainstem and spinal cord receptors. It calms residual daytime stimulant buzz, stops physical restlessness/fidgeting, and lowers core body temperature to induce deep restorative sleep.
3. **Gentle Stomach Acid Protection:**
   * Unlike standard mega-dose Vitamin C protocols, Rayne's drink uses only a gentle 250–500 mg pinch. This is more than enough to activate collagen cross-linking while completely protecting her esophagus and stomach from acid reflux or heartburn.
4. **Supplement Timing Note:**
   * **NAC SAP (600 mg):** Held until the next follow-up with the doctor/NP in early December to evaluate Vyvanse 20 mg cleanly on its own first.

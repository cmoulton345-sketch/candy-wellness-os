## Part 2: Comprehensive Lab Panel Request for Family Doctor

### Clinical Context
Rayne has **Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (EDS)**, chronic crushing fatigue, headaches, stomach pain, daily heartburn, low energy, joint aches, and a history of low ferritin. In EDS, connective tissue laxity frequently causes esophageal sphincter relaxation (acid reflux/GERD), gut dysmotility, and nutrient malabsorption.

### The Recommended Lab Panel

1. **Iron Stores & Anemia (Beyond Basic Hemoglobin):**
   * **Serum Ferritin:** Measures storage iron. Even with normal hemoglobin, low ferritin causes cellular hypoxia, severe fatigue, and headaches.
   * **CBC with Differential:** Evaluates red blood cell indices (MCV/MCH).
   * **Total Iron & TIBC / Transferrin Saturation (% Sat):** Confirms iron binding capacity and transport.

2. **Celiac Disease & GI Screen:**
   * **Anti-tTG IgA (Tissue Transglutaminase IgA):** Gold-standard screen for Celiac disease (a frequent cause of malabsorption, low iron, and belly pain).
   * **Total Serum IgA:** Must accompany Anti-tTG to ensure IgA deficiency doesn't give a false-negative.
   * **H. Pylori (Breath test or stool antigen):** Evaluates chronic gastritis/heartburn.

3. **Autoimmune & Inflammatory Arthritis Panel:**
   * **ANA Screen by IFA (Antinuclear Antibodies):** Screens for Lupus (SLE) and connective tissue autoimmune overlap.
   * **Rheumatoid Factor (RF) & Anti-CCP:** Screens for rheumatoid/inflammatory arthritis.
   * **hs-CRP & ESR (Sedimentation Rate):** Distinguishes systemic autoimmune inflammation from EDS hypermobility joint pain.

4. **Thyroid & Metabolic Function:**
   * **TSH + Free T4:** Sluggish thyroid drives fatigue, headaches, cold sensitivity, and brain fog.
   * **Anti-TPO (Thyroid Peroxidase Antibodies):** Evaluates autoimmune Hashimoto's thyroiditis.
   * **Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP):** Electrolytes, renal (creatinine, eGFR), and hepatic (ALT/AST) function.

5. **Bone Health & Neurological Vitality:**
   * **25-Hydroxy Vitamin D:** Essential for bone density and joint stability in EDS.
   * **Serum Vitamin B12 & Folate:** Deficiency causes crushing fatigue, brain fog, headaches, and nerve tingling.

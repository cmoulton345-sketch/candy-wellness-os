## 2. Canada–New Brunswick Student Loan Protection Blueprint

Because Rayne has an active Canada–New Brunswick Student Loan, following this roadmap protects her finances completely:

* By finishing through mid-December, Rayne completes the full 15-week study period for Term 1.
* Her Canada Student Grants (including the **Canada Student Grant for Students with Disabilities** of $2,800/yr) and provincial bursaries for the Fall term remain **100% non-repayable free gift funding**.
* There is **zero "grant conversion" into loan debt**, and her academic file remains in good standing (no student loan probation).

### B. The January 2027 Disbursement is Cleanly Cancelled
* Rayne’s loan was approved for the full academic year, with the second half scheduled to disburse in January 2027.
* Because she will withdraw effective the end of Term 1, NBCC will not bill her for Winter, and NB Student Aid simply **cancels the January disbursement**.
* She does not receive it, and she will not owe a single cent for Term 2.

### C. The 6-Month Official Grace Period (January 1 to June 30, 2027)
* Leaving full-time study triggers the government’s standard **6-month non-repayment grace period**.
* **Monthly Payments:** **$0.00 / month**.
* **Interest Rate:** **0.00% Permanently** (Federal Canada Student Loans and NB Provincial Student Loans eliminated all interest). Her loan balance will not grow by a single penny while she takes time off.

### D. The Summer Gap (July & August 2027) — Handled via RAP
* Standard repayment would technically open 6 months after leaving (July 1st).
* Because she only has a 2-month gap before restarting school in September:
  * She applies online through the NSLSC portal for the **Repayment Assistance Plan (RAP)** in June.
  * Because she will be working entry-level/part-time, her calculated monthly payment will be **$0.00**.
  * Alternatively, she can make two small voluntary payments ($50–$80) to chip away at her fall principal.

### E. Applying for Fall 2027 (Carrying Over Grants)
* In May/June 2027, Kendra Foote (Options Outreach) helps Rayne submit a fresh **2027–2028 Student Loan application** for her new program.
* Her verified permanent disability status remains locked into the government system permanently—she automatically qualifies for renewed Canada Student Grants ($2,800+) and accommodations without needing new doctor assessments.
* In late August 2027, her new institution confirms enrolment, and her loans immediately return to **"In-Study" (payment-free)** status.

## 4. NBCC Withdrawal Procedure & Key Contacts

Under **NBCC Policy: Student-Initiated Withdrawal**, withdrawing at the natural end of the Fall term cancels all Winter registration and billing.

### Action Steps:
1. **Timing:** Submit notice between **December 1 and December 11, 2026** (right before final exam week and before the college shuts down on December 18).
2. **Method:** Send formal withdrawal email from Rayne’s NBCC student email to her Practical Nursing Coordinating Instructor and Academic Chair.
3. **Internal Processing:** The department submits an internal *Student Status Form* to the Registrar's Office (`studentrecords@nbcc.ca`).
4. **Billing Status:** Term 2 registration is wiped, and winter tuition is assessed at **$0**.

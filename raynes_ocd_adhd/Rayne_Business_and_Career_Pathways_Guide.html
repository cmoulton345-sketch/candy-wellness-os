🔢 1. Math Pathways Explained (Why It Matters)

Are Financial & Workplace Math and Foundations Math the same thing?

❌ No. In New Brunswick, they are two separate math paths:

  • Financial & Workplace Math (110 & 120): Focuses on everyday budgeting, trade math, interest, and practical life calculations. Rayne completed both of these with flying colors (72% in Workplace 120).
  • Foundations of Mathematics (110 & 120): Focuses on algebra, functions, and graphing. Required for traditional 2-year Business Admin diplomas and university business degrees.
The Great News: Rayne is NOT locked out of business! NBCC has specific 1-year business certificate programs designed under Profile A that accept high school diplomas with zero extra math requirements.

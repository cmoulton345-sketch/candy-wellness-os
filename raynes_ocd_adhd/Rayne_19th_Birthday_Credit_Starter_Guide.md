# Rayne's 19th Birthday Credit Starter Guide
> **Prepared by Friday for Candy & Rayne** | Target Activation: December 2026  
> **Jurisdiction:** New Brunswick (Age of Majority: 19)

---

## 🌟 The Target Card: RBC Student Cash Back Mastercard (or RBC ION Visa)

- **$0 Annual Fee Forever:** Never pay a fee to build credit.
- **Guaranteed Student Path:** Approved based on NBCC Nursing enrollment (no full-time job or credit history required).
- **$500 Safety Guardrail Limit:** Low ceiling prevents debt or overwhelm.
- **RBC App Integration:** Appears right alongside her RBC student chequing account.

---

## 🚀 The 3-Step "Autopilot" Formula (OCD & ADHD-Friendly)

1. **Pick ONE Recurring Monthly Bill:**
   - Put **one fixed monthly bill** on the card (e.g., her cell phone bill, Spotify, or one tank of gas per month: $50–$100 max).
   - Nothing else ever gets charged to this card.
2. **Turn on Pre-Authorized Auto-Pay:**
   - Inside RBC, set up automatic payment from her Student Chequing account to pay the **Full Statement Balance** on the due date every single month.
3. **Keep the Physical Card in a Safe Place:**
   - Rayne does not need to carry the card in her purse or wallet. It quietly does its job in the background.

---

## ⚠️ The Golden Rules
- **Keep balance under 30%:** Never have more than $150 sitting on the card at any time.
- **Never carry a balance:** Always pay 100% in full every single month ($0 interest paid).
- **Avoid Retail Store Cards:** Skip store credit cards (e.g., Sephora, Walmart, clothing stores) which charge 29%+ interest.

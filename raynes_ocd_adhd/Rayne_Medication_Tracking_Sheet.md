## 2. Key Clinical Context & Genetics (Pillcheck Report)
* **CYP2D6 Normal Metabolizer:** Amphetamines and fluoxetine are cleared normally. However, Fluoxetine at 50 mg is a strong CYP2D6 inhibitor, which can slow the breakdown of Vyvanse. Therefore, **10 mg is a safe, gentle starting dose** that avoids overwhelming her system.
* **Emotional Affect / Personality Check:** On 30 mg Vyvanse previously, Rayne experienced feeling *"very flat, no emotions at all, just blah."* A primary goal of this 10 mg trial is to verify whether it supports school focus **without** inducing emotional blunting.
* **Antiplatelet / Bleeding Watch:** Fluoxetine has an antiplatelet effect that previously caused heavier menstrual periods and breakthrough spotting. Keep an eye on bruising, nosebleeds, or spotting.

## 3. Daily Checkpoints to Log

1. **ADHD & School Function (1 to 5):** Ease of starting schoolwork, sitting still, less fidgeting, smoother transitions between tasks.
2. **Emotional Affect:** Vibrant, laughing, expressive vs. flat, detached, or "blah."
3. **Appetite at Lunch:** Eating lunch vs. complete appetite suppression.
4. **Physical Symptoms:** Headaches (time of day), clenched jaw, stomach ache, rapid heart rate/palpitations, dizziness on standing.
5. **Afternoon Rebound (Crash):** Fatigue or irritability around 3:30 PM – 5:00 PM as the 10 mg wears off.
6. **Evening Sleep:** Falling asleep easily at bedtime without delay.
7. **OCD Loops:** Checking behaviors remaining calm and quiet.

## 5. Red Flags — When to Contact the Clinic
* Unexplained shivering, chills, or heavy sweating without fever
* Visible muscle tremors, twitches, or unusually brisk reflexes
* Agitation, confusion, or pupils that remain unusually dilated
* Resting heart rate that feels consistently racing or pounding

# WHAT HAPPENS WHEN PROZAC AND POT COMBINE IN AN OCD BRAIN
## A Science-Based Guide for Rayne

### DEAR RAYNE
First off: **No, you did not wreck, ruin, or permanently damage your brain.** Your brain is young, resilient, and safe.

Because you are working so hard for your health and your nursing education, here is the exact science of what happens in your body when **Fluoxetine (Prozac 50 mg)** meets **cannabis (pot)**, and why you might be feeling extra anxious today.

---

### 1. LIVER COMPETITION (THE ENZYME TRAFFIC JAM)
Prozac and pot (THC) are processed by the exact same liver enzyme pathway (`CYP2D6` and `CYP2C9`). When you take 50 mg Prozac and smoke pot, your liver gets backlogged. This causes the THC to stay in your bloodstream much longer and act unpredictably, which can trigger sudden paranoia, racing heart, or high anxiety.

---

### 2. THE REBOUND HANGXIETY EFFECT
Think of pot like alcohol:
- Alcohol might make someone feel relaxed for a couple of hours, but as it leaves the body, it causes a severe hangover and anxiety spike ("hangxiety") the next day.
- Pot does the exact same thing to an OCD brain. It might feel relaxing for an hour, but as it wears off over 24 to 48 hours, it causes a sharp **rebound surge in glutamate and adrenaline**.
- That intense worry you feel today (*"Did I ruin my brain?"*) is **NOT** brain damage. It is literally just the chemical hangover as the pot clears out of your system.

---

### 3. PROTECTING YOUR PROZAC BALANCE
You take 50 mg of Prozac daily to calm your brain's false alarm system (the CSTC circuit) so you can thrive in school and daily life. Smoking pot pulls the emergency brake on the steady, calm balance Prozac is working so hard to build in your brain.

---

### 4. NEXT STEPS & OPEN DOCTOR'S VISIT
- You are not in trouble, and you haven't ruined your progress.
- Be honest with your doctor at your next appointment. Doctors hear this all the time and need accurate information to manage your care safely.
- Give your body a couple of days of clean hydration, good rest, and regular Prozac doses to let your brain re-balance.

---

### REMEMBER
Your worry today is just your OCD reacting to a chemical hangover. Take slow breaths, drink water, and let the wave pass. You are safe, and you are going to be an incredible nurse.

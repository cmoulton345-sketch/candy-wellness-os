## 2. GI, Celiac & Comprehensive Annual Blood Work Panel
Given Rayne’s EDS (where connective tissue laxity commonly drives gastric dysmotility, lower esophageal sphincter laxity/GERD, and malabsorption), we request the following comprehensive investigations:

1. **Celiac Disease Screening:**
   * **Anti-tTG IgA** (Tissue Transglutaminase IgA)
   * **Total Serum IgA** (required to rule out selective IgA deficiency, which causes false-negative Celiac results)
2. **Gastric Reflux / Heartburn Check:**
   * Consideration of an **H. Pylori** test (stool antigen or breath test) given her ongoing heartburn and epigastric discomfort.
3. **Autoimmune & Inflammatory Arthritis Screen:**
   * **ANA Screen by IFA** (Antinuclear Antibodies — screening for systemic connective tissue / Lupus overlap)
   * **Rheumatoid Factor (RF) & Anti-CCP** (screening for inflammatory/rheumatoid arthritis)
   * **Inflammatory Markers: hs-CRP & ESR** (to differentiate mechanical hypermobility joint pain from active autoimmune inflammatory processes)
4. **Thyroid & Metabolic Function:**
   * **TSH + Free T4** (screening for hypothyroidism / metabolic slowdown contributing to fatigue)
   * **Anti-TPO** (Thyroid Peroxidase Antibodies)
   * **Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP):** Electrolytes, Renal function (Creatinine, eGFR), and Hepatic function (ALT, AST, ALP)
5. **Bone Density & Neurological Support:**
   * **25-Hydroxy Vitamin D** (critical for bone and joint health in EDS)
   * **Serum Vitamin B12 & Serum Folate**

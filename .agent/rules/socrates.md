## Core Operational Domains

### 1. Registered Education Savings Plans (RESP) Operations
* **Post-Secondary Education Withdrawals:** EAP (Educational Assistance Payments) vs. PSE (Post-Secondary Educational capital withdrawals).
* **Accumulated Income Payments (AIP):** How to handle remaining growth once schooling is complete.
  - Rollover of up to $50,000 to subscriber's RRSP (requirements: Canadian resident, plan open 10+ years, beneficiary 21+ and not in school, RRSP contribution room available).
  - Return of original principal/contributions (tax-free to subscriber).
  - Paying taxes on non-rolled earnings (regular income tax + 20% penalty tax, Form T1171).

### 2. Retirement Income Funds (RIF & LIF)
* **Annual Minimums:** Age-based formula for mandatory minimum withdrawals.
* **LIF Maximums:** Provincial/federal pension jurisdiction maximum withdrawal formulas.
* **Unlocking Provisions:** 50% unlocking rules, small balance exemptions, financial hardship applications.
* **Withholding Taxes:** Federal & provincial lump-sum withholding rates on amounts above the minimum.

### 3. Corporate Account Onboarding & Compliance
* **KYC & Beneficial Ownership:** Identifying all 25%+ shareholders, Directors, and Authorized Signing Officers.
* **Required Documentation:** Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Incorporation, Corporate Resolution, Form of Indemnity, Banking Resolution.
* **Trading Authorization:** Corporate trading resolutions and authorized signatories.

### 4. Boss Request Decryption Protocol
* When Candy pastes a messy, disjointed email from her boss:
  - 1. Identify the core financial objective.
  - 2. Flag missing information or required forms.
  - 3. Draft a crisp, professional reply: *"Hi [Boss], I can execute this. Here are the 3 steps and the 2 forms I need signed to submit to compliance."*

# 🛡️ Built-In Trauma Safety Protocols (Mandatory)

Every trauma release or deep subconscious processing session generated by **Erickson** must automatically incorporate these safety mechanisms:

1. **Safe-Haven Somatic Anchor**: Installed early in the induction. A physical or mental sanctuary that the client can immediately return to if overwhelmed.
2. **Dissociation Control & Dual Awareness**: Keeping a "watching self" on a virtual screen or distance frame to prevent re-traumatization during memory processing.
3. **Abreaction Management Protocol**:
   - If strong emotional release occurs: *"That's right... just letting that feeling pass through like waves on an ocean shore... you are safe here in this room... sitting securely in your chair..."*
   - Lowering pitch, slowing tempo, grounding to present physical touch or breath.
4. **Somatic Discharge Completion**: Ensuring emotional energy is gently discharged from the nervous system before reorientation.

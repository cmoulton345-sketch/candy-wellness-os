## Workflow Protocol

### Phase 1: Audio Brief

- Purpose: What should this audio achieve?

- Context: Where will this be heard?

- Audience: Who is listening?

- Duration: Exact timing requirements

- Technical Specs: Format, quality, platform requirements

### Phase 2: Sonic Concept

- Present 2-3 audio approaches with different emotional directions

- Reference style/genre influences and why they match the objective

- Identify tempo, key, instrumentation, and mood

### Phase 3: Creative Execution

- Layer elements strategically: rhythm, melody, harmony, effects, ambience

- Mix for clarity and impact across different playback systems

- Master for specified technical requirements

### Phase 4: Sonic Rationale

- Why specific musical choices were made

- How the audio serves the stated objective

- Suggested usage contexts and technical notes

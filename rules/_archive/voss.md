## 🏛️ Core Methodologies & Architecture

### 1. The Implication & ROI Calculator (SPIN + Hormozi)
Never pitch price without establishing the financial bleed of the status quo. Before quoting a $3,000/month retainer, calculate:
- **Time Bleed:** How many hours a week does their staff spend on manual intake, follow-up, or scheduling? (e.g., 20 hrs/wk @ $30/hr = $2,400/mo wasted).
- **Revenue Leakage:** How many missed calls or unworked leads slip through every weekend? (e.g., 4 missed dental implants/mo @ $4,000 each = $16,000/mo lost).
- **Total Cost of Status Quo:** $18,400/month bleeding out.
- **The Pitch:** *"We invest $3,500/month to plug an $18,400/month leak. You are netting $14,900 in new enterprise value every 30 days."*

### 2. Calibrated Negotiation & Objection Disarming (Chris Voss)
When facing price resistance or stalling, never argue or defend. Use tactical mirroring and calibrated questions:
- **Price Objection ("That's too expensive"):**
  *Label:* "It sounds like you feel the upfront investment outweighs the daily cash flow leak we identified."
  *Calibrated Question:* "How am I supposed to justify leaving an $18,000 monthly leak open to save a $3,500 monthly investment?"
- **Stall ("Let me think about it / talk to my partner"):**
  *Accusation Audit:* "You probably feel like I'm rushing you into a decision before you've had time to verify our numbers, and that if we start next week, your staff might feel overwhelmed by new automation."
  *Calibrated Question:* "What specifically about the implementation timeline feels riskiest to your team right now?"

### 3. Commercial Teaching & Frame Control (Challenger + Klaff)
- **The Prize Frame:** Never chase a client. Frame FlowstateAI as the scarce resource. *"We only onboard two Automation Sprint clients a month because we custom-code each n8n workflow. Let's see if your clinic's call volume even warrants our infrastructure."*
- **The Challenger Reframe:** Show them a trend in their industry they haven't noticed. *"Most clinic owners think their biggest problem is lead generation. Our data shows it's actually lead decay—if a lead isn't answered by an AI agent within 4 minutes, your closing probability drops by 80%."*

# Candy's Nutrition Protocol

> **Core Rule:** You eat on a SCHEDULE, not by hunger. Ozempic + Vyvanse suppress your appetite signals — but your body still needs fuel. If you wait until you're hungry, you won't eat enough, and your body will revolt at night.

> **Mantra:** *"I eat to fuel my body, not because I feel hungry. Hunger is broken right now — the schedule is my guide."*

---

## The Under-Fueling Problem (Why You Crave at Night)

```
MORNING: Vyvanse (appetite ↓↓) + Ozempic (appetite ↓↓↓)
     ↓
DAYTIME: "I'm not hungry" → skip meals or eat tiny portions
     ↓
EVENING: Medications wear off → body realizes it's 600+ calories short
     ↓
NIGHT: Survival-level cravings kick in → crunchy, high-calorie foods
     ↓
GUILT: "I have no willpower" → shame → restrict harder tomorrow → repeat
```

**The fix:** Front-load calories. Eat by the clock, not by your stomach. Your appetite is medically suppressed — that doesn't mean your body doesn't need the fuel.

## Late-Night Craving Defense System

### Why You Crave Crunchy
- **Stress/ADHD connection:** Crunching is a self-soothing sensory behavior — the jaw movement and sound activate your parasympathetic nervous system
- **Under-fueling:** Your body wants calorie-dense food fast. Crunchy foods (chips, crackers, cereal) are usually carb-heavy and quick energy
- **Habit loop:** Evening = relaxation = reward seeking = crunch

### The Defense Layers

**Layer 1: Prevention (Daytime Eating)**
- Eat all 5 scheduled meals/snacks — no skipping
- Hit 1,200+ kcal by 4:00 PM
- If you ate less than 1,000 kcal by dinner, make dinner larger

**Layer 2: The Closing Snack (6:45 PM)**
- Always include something CRUNCHY (seeds, almonds, rice cakes, popcorn)
- Always include protein (slows digestion, keeps you full longer)
- Brush teeth immediately after

**Layer 3: If Cravings Hit After 7 PM**
Before eating, ask yourself one question: *"Did I eat all my meals today?"*

- **If NO:** This craving is biological. You under-ate. Have a small protein + crunch combo (cottage cheese + pumpkin seeds) and forgive yourself. Tomorrow, hit every meal.
- **If YES:** This craving is emotional/habitual. Try these first:
  1. Glass of water with lemon (sometimes thirst mimics hunger)
  2. 5 min walk or stretch
  3. Peppermint tea (kills sweet cravings)
  4. Call a friend, cuddle the pup, change rooms

**Layer 4: If You Eat After 7 PM**
- **Zero guilt.** Log what you ate in your craving journal and move on.
- Note: What did you crave? What did you eat? How were you feeling before?
- This data reveals patterns over 4 weeks — is it hormonal (Protect Phase), stress-triggered, or under-fueling?

## Natural Diuretics & Anti-Inflammatory Foods

**Emphasize during Protect Phase:**

| Food | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Cucumber | Diuretic + hydrating |
| Celery | Diuretic + crunchy (double duty) |
| Asparagus | Strong natural diuretic |
| Dandelion tea | Gentle diuretic, replace evening herbal tea |
| Ginger | Anti-inflammatory, aids Ozempic nausea |
| Turmeric + black pepper | Anti-inflammatory (add to scrambled eggs) |
| Tart cherry juice (2 oz) | Anti-inflammatory, improves sleep quality |
| Fatty fish (salmon, sardines) | Omega-3 anti-inflammatory |

**Reduce during Protect Phase:**
- Pre-made dressings (high sodium → water retention)
- Bread and processed carbs (swap for rice cakes or Ezekiel toast)
- Soy sauce (swap for coconut aminos)

## Weekly Meal Prep (Sunday — 30-45 min)

To make clock-based eating possible when your brain doesn't want to think about food:

1. Hard-boil 6 eggs
2. Wash and slice celery + cucumber for the week
3. Portion pumpkin seeds into 1 oz bags (x5)
4. Grill or bake 2 lbs chicken breast (slice and refrigerate)
5. Pre-make 2-3 tuna salads in containers
6. Stock Fairlife shakes (check supply)
7. Pop 2 cups popcorn, store in airtight container for crunchy snacking

> **Prep is not optional.** When your appetite is suppressed and your ADHD brain says "I'll figure it out later," having food ready and visible is the difference between eating and not eating.

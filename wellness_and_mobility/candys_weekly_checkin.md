
# Candy's Sunday Weekly Check-In — "The Harvest"

> *"The only way to know which direction to grow is to stop and honestly look at where you are."* — Rohn
>
> **When:** Every Sunday morning. Before the week starts.
> **Time needed:** 15-20 minutes.
> **What you need:** Your dashboard, your Oura app, and a cup of coffee.

---

## 🌅 STEP 1: BODY CHECK-IN (2 min)

Before you look at any data, just check in with your body:

**How does my body feel today compared to last Sunday?**
- Better / About the same / Worse
- Where does my body feel well? ___
- Where does my body feel uncomfortable? ___

**Check Oura ring:**
- Readiness score: ___
- Temperature trend this week: ___
- Based on this, I am heading into: 🟢 Push / 🟡 Protect this week

**Take Sunday measurements (Push Phase only — skip during Protect Phase):**
- Weight: ___ lb
- Waist: ___ in
- Hip: ___ in

---

## 🏆 STEP 2: WINS (2 min)

*Jim Rohn said small wins compound. You must acknowledge them or they don't register as evidence that you're changing.*

**What are 3 things that went WELL this week — any category:**

1. ___
2. ___
3. ___

> They don't have to be big. "I ate my mid-morning snack every day even when I wasn't hungry" is a massive win when you're on Ozempic.

---

## 🔍 STEP 3: HONEST REVIEW (5 min)

Open your dashboard from the week. Look at the traffic lights:

**Nutrition:**
- Did I eat all 5 meals on schedule? ___ / 7 days
- Did I have late-night cravings? ___ nights
- What triggered them? (Under-ate / Stress / Hormonal / Habit) ___
- Protein hit most days? Y / N

**Movement:**
- Strength sessions: ___ / target (3 Push or 2 Protect)
- Yoga sessions: ___ / 2
- Walks: ___
- How did my body feel during workouts? ___

**Sleep & Recovery:**
- Average Oura sleep score: ___
- Did magnesium and Legs Up the Wall happen consistently? Y / N
- Did I feel rested most mornings? ___

**Mental & Emotional:**
- Did I do my morning prime most days? ___ / 7
- Did anxiety spike this week? When? ___
- Did I use the circuit breaker protocols? Y / N
- How heavy was Rayne's situation this week? ___
- Did I protect my emotional boundaries? ___

**Body Image:**
- How many times did I check the scale or measurements outside of today? ___
- How am I feeling about my body right now? (Honest — no right answer) ___
- Did body image fear spike this week? What triggered it? ___

**Alcohol:**
- Total drinks this week: ___
- Ceiling: ___ — Met it? Y / N
- Any shame spiral moments? Y / N
- If yes: What was I feeling before I started drinking that night? ___

---

## 📐 STEP 4: ONE MISS (1 min)

*"Don't beat yourself up over what didn't work — extract the lesson and move on."* — Rohn

**What ONE thing fell short this week?**
___

**What's the honest reason it fell short?** (No judgment — just truth)
___

**What ONE small adjustment would prevent this next week?**
___

> Keep it to one miss. One adjustment. More than that becomes a shame list, not a growth tool.

---

## 🔮 STEP 5: PHASE FORECAST & WEEK PLAN (3 min)

Based on your Oura temperature trend and body signals this week:

**Next week I will be in:** 🟢 Push / 🟡 Protect

**Training plan for this week:**
- Strength: ___ sessions (Mon ___ / Wed ___ / Fri ___)
- Yoga: Wed + Thu ✓
- Walks: ___
- Extra mobility needed? Y / N

**Nutrition focus for this week:**
- Any meals to prep Sunday? (Hard-boil eggs / Grill chicken / Portion seeds / Slice veggies)
- Do I need to restock anything? ___
- Closing snack plan for this week: ___

**Supplement check:**
- Magnesium: Stocked? Y / N
- B12: Stocked? Y / N
- Fish Oil / Vitamin D3: Stocked? Y / N

---

## 💛 STEP 6: EMOTIONAL PULSE (2 min)

*This is not optional. Your emotional health is not a bonus category — it is a foundation.*

**How am I feeling about myself right now, in this moment?** (1 sentence, honest)
___

**How am I feeling about my relationship with my body right now?**
___

**Sunday Mirror Practice (60 seconds):**
Stand in front of the mirror. Name 3 things your body DID this week:
1. ___
2. ___
3. ___

Say aloud: *"I am not at war with this body. We are on the same team."*

---

## 🌱 STEP 7: THIS WEEK'S INTENTION (1 min)

*Plant one seed. One focus. One shift.*

**This week I will focus on:** ___

**My one small adjustment from last week's miss:** ___

**My personal mantra for this week:** *(choose one or write your own)*
- *"I eat to fuel my body, not because I feel hungry."*
- *"I am not at war with this body. We are on the same team."*
- *"Push or Protect — either mode is the right mode."*
- *"My worthiness is not on trial."*
- *___"*

- A pattern keeps repeating that you can't break
- You're confused about your phase or your data
- You want to adjust the program

**Reach out to Soma.** That's what I'm here for. The program is not rigid — it's a living document. What works in September may need tweaking in November. That's not failure. That's data.

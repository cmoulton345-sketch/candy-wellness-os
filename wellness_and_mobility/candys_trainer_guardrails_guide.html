Trainer Guardrails & Safe Swaps

What to Avoid, What to Celebrate & Copy-Pastable Script
🛑 What to Avoid (Red Light List)
1. No Deep Squats Below 90°: Dropping deep causes anterior femoral glide, where the ball of the thigh bone pinches the front hip capsule and labrum (C-sign).
2. No Deep Walking Lunges or Bulgarian Split Squats: Deep trailing-leg hip extension under load irritates the psoas and hip flexor.
3. No Deep Leg Presses: Sled must NOT descend so far that knees push into chest or lower back rounds off the pad. Stop at 90°!
4. No Behind-the-Neck Movements: Pulling or pressing behind the neck causes severe cervical and rotator cuff impingement.
5. No Standing Bent-Over Barbell Rows: Unsupported lumbar loading strains spinal facet joints.
6. No Flat-Palm Floor Pushups / Bent-Wrist Barbell Presses: Protects the thumb CMC basal joint from hyperextension compression.
7. No Exhausting HIIT / CrossFit to Failure: Spikes cortisol and causes 4-day inflammatory fluid holding in perimenopause.
🟢 What Candy Thrives On (Green Light List)
Box Squats to Bench (90°): Sitting back to a box at 90°, driving through heels to recruit glutes with zero front hip pinch.
Seated Leg Press (High & Wide Feet, 90° Stop): Maximum glute and hamstring loading with zero spinal compression.
Seated Hip Abduction Machine: The ultimate glute shelf builder (gluteus medius) to stabilize the pelvis.
Chest-Supported Machine Rows: Chest pad supports the lower back while strengthening upper posture muscles.
Lat Pulldown to Upper Chest: Wide/neutral grip pulled to collarbone to widen the lats and narrow the waistline.
Neutral-Grip Dumbbell Presses & Machines: Keeps wrists straight to protect thumb joints.
📋 Copy-Pastable Message to Your Trainer

Send this directly to your trainer—trainers love clear, professional guidance:

HIPS / LOWER BODY: - I have a sensitive front hip flexor (anterior hip pinch / C-sign). - Please NO squats below 90 degrees, deep walking lunges, or Bulgarian split squats. - I do amazing with Box Squats to a bench (stopping at 90°), Seated Leg Press (stopping at 90° with feet high on the plate), the Hip Abduction machine, and Glute Bridges/Thrusts. 2. UPPER BODY & NECK: - My neck and lower back do best when my back is supported. - Please NO behind-the-neck pulldowns or upright barbell rows. - I do great with Chest-Supported Rows, Lat Pulldowns pulled to the upper chest, Seated Chest Press machines, and Cable Tricep pushdowns. 3. WRISTS / THUMBS: - I have a sensitive thumb joint, so neutral-grip dumbbells or machines with straight wrists work much better for me than straight barbell pressing or floor pushups on flat hands. 4. OVERALL STYLE: - My goal is lean muscle tone, glute sculpting, posture, and joint longevity. - 3 days a week, 10–12 reps with controlled form and moderate rest works best for my recovery. Thank you so much—excited to get started!

GoodLife Circuit & RPM Guide 🏢

Protected Strength, Glute & Arm Sculpting & Smart RPM Guidelines

🚴 Les Mills RPM: 100% Green Light!

Because you have done RPM many times in the past, your muscle memory and cadence coordination are already locked in! Cycling is zero ground impact (saves joints and feet) and acts as a powerful lymphatic fluid pump to de-puff your legs.

🛡️ Setup Rule #1: Handlebars HIGH (C-Sign Hip Protection)
Set handlebars 1 to 2 notches HIGHER than your saddle. This keeps your pelvis upright and prevents deep hip flexion (< 60°), stopping anterior femoral pinching and psoas irritation completely!
⚡ Setup Rule #2: Cadence over Heavy Knee Grinding
Keep your pedals spinning smoothly between 70 to 90 RPM. Never grind below 60 RPM with extreme torque—smooth cadence builds cardiovascular endurance and tones without knee or sacroiliac strain.
🍑 Setup Rule #3: Glute Drive on Standing Climbs
When standing out of the saddle, keep your chest proud and hips floating over the nose of the saddle. Drive through your heels and mid-foot to activate glutes and hamstrings rather than pushing through toes.
⚠️ The Hip Whisper Rule
If you ever feel even a slight pinch in your front hip or groin during a climb, sit down immediately, back the resistance off one turn, and ride the flat road. You never have to match the instructor's resistance call!
🦿 Part A: Lower Body Sculpt & Pelvic Stability
1. Seated Leg Press Machine
Target: Glutes, Hamstrings & Quads
🍑 Glute Focus
  • Sit back firmly with lower back and sacrum flat against backrest.
  • Place feet high and shoulder-width apart on the footplate (feet high shifts load directly into glutes & off knees).
🛡️ SOMA PROTECTION GUARDRAIL: Stop at 90 degrees! Do NOT let the sled come down so deep that your knees hit your chest or your lower back peels off the pad. Stop at 90°, drive through heels, and never lock your knees at the top.
3 sets × 10–12 reps
2. Seated Hip Abduction (Outer Thigh)
Target: Gluteus Medius (Upper Glute "Shelf")
🍑 Glute Lift
  • Pads set on outside of knees. Lean torso forward 10–15° with flat back.
  • Smoothly press knees outward against pads, hold squeeze for 1 second.
  • Control the return (2 seconds)—do not let weights slam together.
🛡️ SOMA PROTECTION GUARDRAIL: Do not arch your lower back to force the pads wider. Keep your core gently braced and pelvis anchored to the seat.
3 sets × 12–15 reps
3. Seated Leg Curl Machine
Target: Hamstrings & Posterior Chain Tone
🦵 Hamstrings
  • Back flat against rear pad; lap thigh pad locked snugly over quads.
  • Lower leg roller sits just below calves (above Achilles tendon).
  • Pull heels smoothly down and back toward seat, squeeze 1s, return slow.
🛡️ SOMA PROTECTION GUARDRAIL: Do not point toes inward. Keep feet flexed and straight forward to balance inner and outer hamstring tendons.
3 sets × 10–12 reps
4. Standing or Seated Calf Press
Target: Lower Leg Tone & Lymphatic Drainage
💧 Fluid Flush
  • Balls of feet on edge of platform; heels hanging off.
  • Press up high onto toes, pause 1 second, lower down below platform level.
  • Acts as a physical muscle pump to drain fluid from ankles and knees!
2 sets × 15 reps
🏹 Part B: Upper Body Posture & Sculpt
5. Lat Pulldown Machine
Target: Upper Back & Hourglass Taper
🏹 Back Sculpt
  • Thigh pads locked firmly over quads. Grasp bar wider than shoulders.
  • Slight backward lean (10°), chest proud.
🛡️ SOMA PROTECTION GUARDRAIL: Pull bar strictly to your collarbone / upper chest. NEVER pull behind your neck! Behind-the-neck pulling strains cervical vertebrae and causes severe trapezius stiffness. Drive elbows toward ribcage.
3 sets × 10–12 reps
6. Seated Cable Row (Chest-Supported)
Target: Mid-Back, Rhomboids & Open Posture
✨ Posture Decompress
  • Chest resting against front pad (takes all stress off lumbar spine).
  • Grasp neutral handles (palms facing each other).
  • Pull elbows back past ribs, squeeze shoulder blades together for 1 second.
🛡️ SOMA PROTECTION GUARDRAIL: Keep shoulders pressed down away from your ears. Do NOT shrug into your neck or upper traps.
3 sets × 10–12 reps
7. Seated Chest Press Machine
Target: Pectorals & Front Shoulders
🛡️ Shoulder Safe
  • Seat height adjusted so handles line up with mid-chest (nipple line).
  • Press handles forward smoothly, pausing before elbows lock out.
🛡️ SOMA PROTECTION GUARDRAIL: Tuck elbows at 45 degrees (arrow shape). Flaring elbows out wide at 90 degrees pinches the front shoulder joint capsule.
3 sets × 10 reps
8. Shoulder Lateral Raise Machine / Cable
Target: Side Deltoid Sculpting
💪 Sleeveless Tone
  • Padded arms against sides of forearms. Sit tall with chest open.
  • Raise arms out to sides until parallel to floor, hold 1s, return slow.
3 sets × 12 reps (Light Weight)
9. Cable Tricep Rope Pushdown
Target: Back of the Arms (Toned Triceps)
➰ Arm Firming
  • High cable pulley with rope attachment. Pin elbows against ribcage.
  • Push straight down and flare rope ends apart at the bottom.
  • Squeeze triceps for 1 second, control return up to 90 degrees.
3 sets × 12 reps

High repetition barbell endurance. Mandatory rule: Use warm-up / lightest plates only. Never squat past 90°, avoid lunges if hips are tight, and stop clean & press at chest height.

Repetitive high-impact jumping and explosive kicks can trigger sacroiliac joint strain, plantar foot pain, and elevated cortisol fluid rebound. Wait until your joint foundation is completely solid!

## The Core Insight: You Are Under-Fueled

**Ozempic (18 clicks) + Vyvanse (40mg)** = dual appetite suppression. Both medications reduce hunger signals during the day. This means:

- You're eating far below what your body needs during waking hours
- By evening, your body's survival systems override the medication and scream for calories
- The late-night cravings are not a discipline failure — they are a biological alarm
- **The fix is not more willpower at night — it's more food during the day**

This program front-loads your calories and protein into the morning and midday window when your appetite is suppressed, so your body is properly fueled before the medications wear off.

## The Two Operating Modes

### 🟢 Push Phase (Follicular-Equivalent)
**Signal:** Oura temperature at or below your personal baseline
- Higher energy, better recovery, lower inflammation
- Train at full intensity, progressive overload
- Standard calorie target (1,500-1,600 kcal — increased from 1,400)
- Body handles carbs and sodium better

### 🟡 Protect Phase (Luteal-Equivalent)
**Signal:** Oura temperature rises 0.3-0.5°F above baseline
- Expect: Water retention, joint flares, cravings, mood shifts, brain fog
- Reduce training intensity, increase recovery work
- Allow +100-150 kcal, emphasize anti-inflammatory foods
- Increase magnesium, potassium, hydration
- **No scale, no measurements, no mirror checking during this phase**

## Measurement Rules (Boundary Protocol)

> **The scale and tape measure are data tools, not judgment tools. We use them on a schedule — not on an impulse.**

| What | When | Frequency | Rule |
|------|------|-----------|------|
| **Scale** | Sunday AM only | 1x/week | Naked, after bathroom, before food. Record and walk away. |
| **Waist** | Sunday AM only | 1x/week | At belly button, relaxed exhale. |
| **Hip** | Sunday AM only | 1x/week | Widest point. |
| **Mirror check** | Sunday only (Mirror Practice) | 1x/week | Structured gratitude practice, not scanning for flaws. |

**During Protect Phase:** Skip ALL measurements. Zero scale, zero tape, zero mirror scanning. Trust the process. The data will be there when the phase passes.

**Hard Rule:** If you catch yourself stepping on the scale or checking measurements outside of Sunday, gently put it down and say: *"This information will not help me right now. I check on Sundays."*

# 🏋️ Candy's GoodLife Personal Trainer Programming Guide
## Clinical Guardrails, Safe Swaps & Biomechanical Parameters

**Client:** Candy Moulton  
**Facility:** GoodLife Fitness  
**Focus:** Full-Body Functional Toning, Glute Sculpting, Posture & Joint Longevity  
**Stage of Life:** 48, Perimenopausal (Preserving muscle mass & bone density without spiking systemic cortisol)  

---

## 🛑 1. The "Red Light" List: What to Avoid & Why

### A. Anterior Hip & Psoas (C-Sign Protection)
* ❌ **Avoid Deep Squats Below 90°:** Dropping into deep hip flexion causes anterior femoral glide, where the thigh bone pinches the front hip capsule and labrum.
* ❌ **Avoid Heavy Walking Lunges or Bulgarian Split Squats:** Deep hip extension under heavy load puts shear stress on the trailing hip flexor and psoas.
* ❌ **Avoid Deep Leg Press Sleds:** Do not let the carriage descend so far that knees compress into the chest or the lower back rounds off the backrest.
* ❌ **Avoid Unsupported Tabletop / Hanging Leg Raises:** Hanging knee-to-elbows or unsupported leg lowers force the psoas and TFL into protective spasm.

### B. Cervical Spine & Neck
* ❌ **Avoid Behind-the-Neck Pulldowns or Presses:** Forces extreme cervical flexion and shoulder internal rotation.
* ❌ **Avoid Heavy Barbell Back Squats:** Direct bar compression on the upper spine/trapezius triggers cervical tension headaches.
* ❌ **Avoid Upright Barbell Rows:** Narrow-grip pulling up to the chin pinches the subacromial space.

### C. Lower Back & Sacrum
* ❌ **Avoid Standing Bent-Over Barbell Rows:** Places continuous shear across the lumbar facet joints without support.
* ❌ **Avoid Heavy Conventional Deadlifts from the Floor:** High risk of lumbar flexion under fatigue.

### D. Wrists & Thumb Basal (CMC) Joint
* ❌ **Avoid Flat-Palm Floor Pushups or Burpees:** Hyperextending the wrist while loading bodyweight irritates the basal thumb joint.
* ❌ **Avoid Heavy Barbell Bench Press with Bent Wrists:** Forces the wrist backward. Prefer neutral dumbbells or machine presses.

### E. Metabolic & Nervous System
* ❌ **Avoid Exhausting CrossFit / HIIT Grinding:** High-intensity jumping intervals and training to total muscular failure spike cortisol and trigger multi-day fluid retention in perimenopause.

## 🟢 2. The "Green Light" List: What Candy Thrives On

### A. Lower Body & Glutes
* ✅ **Box Squats to Bench (90°):** Sitting back to a box at 90°, driving through heels to recruit glutes with zero front hip pinch.
* ✅ **Seated Leg Press (High & Wide Feet, 90° Stop):** Feet placed high on the plate keeps load in glutes/hamstrings and off knees.
* ✅ **Seated Hip Abduction Machine:** Leaning forward 15° with flat back to sculpt the upper glute shelf (glute medius).
* ✅ **Banded Glute Bridges & Hip Thrusts:** Bench or floor bridges isolate glute max and trigger reciprocal inhibition to relax hip flexors.
* ✅ **Seated Leg Curls & Standing Calf Raises:** Great posterior chain balance and active lymphatic calf pump.

### B. Upper Body Sculpt & Posture
* ✅ **Lat Pulldowns (Pulled to Collarbone):** Wide or neutral grip, driving elbows down to ribs.
* ✅ **Chest-Supported Machine Rows:** Padded chest support fully offloads the lower back while strengthening mid-back posture muscles.
* ✅ **Seated Chest Press Machine (45° Elbow Angle):** Handles aligned with mid-chest, elbows tucked like an arrow (not flared at 90°).
* ✅ **Dumbbell Lateral Raises in Scapular Plane:** Arms raised 30° forward of the body, thumbs slightly higher than pinkies.
* ✅ **Cable Tricep Rope Pushdowns:** Controlled lockouts with flare at the bottom.

### C. Programming Parameters
* **Frequency:** 3 days per week (e.g., Mon / Wed / Fri).
* **Rep Range:** 10–12 controlled reps (2 seconds down, 1 second pause/squeeze).
* **Rest:** 60–90 seconds between sets to allow full ATP recovery without cortisol elevation.

## ☀️ Morning Neural Prime (5-10 min, before opening work email)

This is your daily operating system boot sequence. Do it before email, before Slack, before anyone else's urgency enters your brain.

### The Sequence

| Step | Time | What | Why |
|------|------|------|-----|
| 1 | 30 sec | **Check Oura Ring** — temperature trend, sleep score, readiness | Determines Push or Protect mode for the day |
| 2 | 1 min | **Box Breathing** — 4 counts in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold (x3 cycles) | Activates parasympathetic nervous system, lowers morning cortisol |
| 3 | 1 min | **Morning Affirmations** (from `morning_affirmations.md`) | Anchors identity before the world tries to define you |
| 4 | 30 sec | **1 Gratitude** — feel it, don't just name it. One specific thing. | Shifts brain from threat-scanning to appreciation |
| 5 | 1 min | **#1 Priority** — name the single most important thing to complete before 1:30 PM | Gives ADHD brain a clear target instead of chaos |
| 6 | 30 sec | **Body Check** — notice stiffness or pain without judgment | Informs whether to modify movement later |

### Phase-Specific Morning Addition

- **🟢 Push Mode:** Add a power statement: *"I have energy today. I'm going to use it."*
- **🟡 Protect Mode:** Add a permission statement: *"Today I go at 70%. That is enough. I am not falling behind."*

## 🧠 ADHD Brain Fog Protocols

### Understanding Your Fog

Your brain fog has THREE contributing causes — not just one:

1. **ADHD working memory fatigue** — your brain burns more glucose doing "normal" tasks
2. **Perimenopause** — declining estrogen directly impacts cognitive function, word recall, and focus
3. **Ozempic + calorie restriction** — your brain runs on glucose. Under-fueling makes fog worse.

**This is why eating on schedule is a MENTAL HEALTH intervention, not just a nutrition one.**

### When Fog Hits (Rescue Protocols)

#### The 2:30 PM Slump (Predictable — Plan for It)

Your Vyvanse is wearing off. Your blood sugar may be dipping. This is NOT the time to try to do complex work.

| Option | What | Time |
|--------|------|------|
| **A** | Walk with mom and/or pup (movement + sunlight + connection) | 15-20 min |
| **B** | Cold water on wrists + splash face + 5 deep breaths | 2 min |
| **C** | One song dance break (put on a banger, move your body) | 3-4 min |
| **D** | Step outside, look at sky, name 5 things you see | 2 min |

After the slump protocol: **Do only easy, no-brainer tasks until 4:30 PM shutdown.** Filing, formatting, simple replies. Save nothing complex for after 2:30.

#### Random Fog (Could Strike Anytime)

1. **Eat something.** First and always. If you haven't eaten in 3+ hours, fog may be blood sugar.
2. **Hydrate.** Dehydration mimics brain fog. Drink 16 oz water.
3. **Stand up and move.** 60 seconds of movement. Anything.
4. **Reduce inputs.** Close tabs, silence notifications, simplify your visual field.
5. **If it persists:** Accept the fog day. Switch to mechanical tasks only. Tomorrow will be different.

### Fog Day Permission Slip

> *"Brain fog is not laziness. It is my brain conserving energy because multiple systems are competing for resources. I am not failing. I am adapting. The fog will lift."*

## 🛡️ Anxiety & Overwhelm Circuit Breakers

### The 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding (When Anxiety Spikes)

Stop. Wherever you are. Do this:

- **5** things you can **see** (name them aloud)
- **4** things you can **touch** (touch them)
- **3** things you can **hear** (listen)
- **2** things you can **smell** (breathe in)
- **1** thing you can **taste** (sip water, notice it)

This pulls your brain out of future-catastrophizing and back into present-moment reality.

### The Body Scan (When Tension is Physical)

1. Drop your shoulders half an inch. (They're up by your ears right now, aren't they?)
2. Unclench your jaw. Let your tongue drop from the roof of your mouth.
3. Soften your hands. Open your fists.
4. One breath: 4 in, 7 hold, 8 out.
5. Say: *"I am safe right now. Nothing needs to be fixed in this exact moment."*

### The "What's Actually True?" Test (When Spiraling)

When your mind is racing with worst-case scenarios:

1. **What am I afraid of right now?** (Name it specifically)
2. **Is this happening right now, or am I imagining it?**
3. **What is actually true in this moment?**
4. **What is ONE thing I can control right now?**

> *"Anxiety is my brain trying to solve problems that haven't happened yet. I can acknowledge the fear without obeying it."*

## 💊 Medication & Mental Health Interactions

### Vyvanse (40mg)
- **Peak effect:** ~2-4 hours after dosing (roughly 10:30 AM - 12:30 PM = your Golden Focus window)
- **Wear-off:** ~2:00-3:00 PM (explains the afternoon slump)
- **Critical:** Must be paired with 30g+ protein at dosing. Acidic foods/drinks reduce absorption.
- **Watch for:** Increased anxiety in the first hour, appetite suppression all day, potential irritability at wear-off

### Ozempic (18 clicks)
- **Appetite suppression:** Can last 5-7 days after injection
- **GI effects:** Nausea, bloating, constipation — manage with hydration, fiber, ginger tea
- **B12 depletion:** Long-term use can deplete B12 → brain fog, fatigue, numbness/tingling. **Supplement with 1,000 mcg sublingual B12.**
- **Muscle loss risk:** GLP-1 medications can cause lean mass loss. High protein (135g+) and strength training 3x/week are your defense.
- **Critical:** Eat on schedule even without hunger. Your appetite signal is medically suppressed — it is not trustworthy right now.

### Combined Effect
- Both drugs suppress appetite → under-fueling → worse brain fog → worse mood → worse cravings at night
- **The single most powerful mental health intervention you can make is eating your scheduled meals during the day.** This is not about weight — it's about giving your brain enough fuel to function.

## 🌙 Evening Wind-Down Protocol

### 8:30 PM — Transition to Rest

1. **Magnesium Bisglycinate** (200-400 mg) with water
2. Phone on Do Not Disturb or in another room
3. Herbal tea (chamomile, peppermint, or dandelion during Protect Phase)
4. Low lighting — dim overhead lights, use warm lamp or candles
5. Legs Up the Wall (5-10 min) with breathing

### Sleep Hygiene Rules

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| No work email after 4:30 PM shutdown | Your brain needs 3+ hours of non-work before sleep |
| No screen scrolling in bed | Blue light + comparison + news = activated nervous system |
| Room temp 65-68°F | Optimal for sleep, especially during perimenopause hot flashes |
| Magnesium at 8:30 PM consistently | Builds a sleep-prep signal your body learns to expect |

### If You Can't Sleep (Racing Mind Protocol)

1. **Don't fight it.** Resistance makes insomnia worse.
2. **4-7-8 breath:** Inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8 (x4 cycles)
3. **Body scan:** Start at your toes, relax each muscle group upward
4. **Thought dumping:** Keep a notepad by the bed. Write down whatever's racing. Get it out of your head.
5. Say: *"Everything on that paper will still be there tomorrow. Right now, my only job is rest."*

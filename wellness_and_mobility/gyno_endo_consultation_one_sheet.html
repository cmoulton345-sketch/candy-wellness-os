2. PRIMARY REFERRAL CATALYST: Post-Physiotherapy Adverse Flare & Physio Note

Critical Clinical Trigger: This specialist consultation was directly precipitated by an extreme adverse reaction to specialized pelvic floor manual physical therapy.
  • The Clinical Event: A Pelvic Health Physiotherapist conducted internal manual mobilization/massage of the post-hysterectomy vaginal cuff scar along with manual hip traction.
  • The Reaction (Months of Incapacitation): The treatment provoked an immediate, catastrophic pain flare that "wrote the patient off for months." The pain was so agonizing and severe that she was barely able to walk, move, or perform daily living activities.
  • Direct Clinical Consultation Note Sent: Recognizing that such a profound, multi-month reaction was completely outside the normal response of simple muscular hypertonicity, the Pelvic Floor Physiotherapist sent a formal clinical consultation note directly to the Gynecologist advocating for specialist intervention.
  • Diagnostic Implications: Severe multi-month pain following direct cuff scar mobilization strongly indicates:
    • Deep infiltrating endometriosis (DIE) embedded in or adjacent to the vaginal cuff, uterosacral ligaments, or pouch of Douglas.
    • Dense fibrous surgical adhesions tethering the left adnexa or pelvic sidewall, mechanically strained by traction.
    • Severe pudendal or pelvic plexus nerve entrapment triggered by manual scar pressure.

## 4. FINGER PRICK HORMONE TEST GUIDE & OPTIMAL WINDOW

### Target Testing Window:
* **Optimal Days:** **5 to 7 days AFTER your Oura temperature shifts UP** into your elevated range (+0.2°C to +0.4°C).
* **Why:** This window captures your **mid-luteal peak** (Protect Phase equivalent), allowing your doctor to evaluate your peak Progesterone level and your Estrogen-to-Progesterone (E2:Pg) ratio.

### Testing Protocol Checklist:
1. **Track Oura Temp:** Wait for 2-3 consecutive nights of elevated temp (+0.2°C to +0.4°C).
2. **Count 5 Days:** Schedule your capillary blood draw on Day 5, 6, or 7 of that elevated temp plateau.
3. **Daily BHRT Timing:** Follow your kit's exact instructions regarding hormone cream application on test day (typically blood is collected in the morning before applying your morning BiEst, or 12-14 hours after evening progesterone).
4. **Log Test Date:** Record the test date and Oura temp in this file when completed.

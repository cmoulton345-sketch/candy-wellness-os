> **How to weigh yourself:** Sunday morning. Naked. After bathroom. Before food. Step on, read the number, write it down, step off, put the scale away. You are not allowed to weigh yourself again until next Sunday.
>
> **7-Day Average Weight:** Add your last 7 daily weights (if you tracked them during Push Phase) and divide by 7. This is the number that actually matters — not any single day's reading. Water fluctuations of 1-4 lb daily are completely normal and tell you nothing about fat.

## 🚦 WEEKLY TRAFFIC LIGHT SYSTEM

Each Sunday, give yourself an honest color rating across your 6 pillars:

| Pillar | 🟢 Green (On Track) | 🟡 Yellow (Needs Attention) | 🔴 Red (Fell Off — Needs Reset) |
|--------|--------------------|-----------------------------|--------------------------------|
| **Nutrition** | Hit all 5 meals 5+ days | Hit meals 3-4 days | Skipped meals most days, late-night eating all week |
| **Movement** | Completed all planned sessions | Missed 1 session | Missed 2+ sessions |
| **Sleep** | 7+ hours, Oura score 70+ | 6-7 hours, some disruptions | Less than 6 hours consistently |
| **Mental** | Morning prime done daily | Done 3-4 days | Skipped most days |
| **Emotional** | No compulsive checking, mirror practice done | 1-2 impulse scale checks | Daily compulsive weighing or measuring |
| **Alcohol** | At or below ceiling | 1-2 drinks over ceiling | Significantly over, shame spiral happened |

> **If you have 4+ greens:** You're doing it. Keep going. Celebrate this.
> **If you have 2-3 greens:** Pick the ONE yellow or red that will have the most impact if fixed. Address only that one next week.
> **If you have mostly reds:** Something big happened (Protect Phase flare, stressful week, family crisis). This is not failure — it's being human. Sunday Harvest helps you identify what to reset first.

## 🌡️ OURA RING GUIDE — Reading Your Phase Signal

| What You See | What It Means | Your Mode |
|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Temperature at or below your personal baseline | Likely follicular-equivalent phase | 🟢 **Push** |
| Temperature rising 0.3-0.5°F above baseline | Approaching or in luteal-equivalent phase | 🟡 **Protect (incoming)** |
| Temperature elevated + finger swelling + mood shifts | Full luteal-equivalent phase | 🟡 **Protect (active)** |
| Temperature dropping back to baseline | Phase ending, energy returning | 🟢 **Push (returning)** |

> **You cannot predict the exact length of your cycle without a uterus.** Your Oura data, combined with your body signals (finger swelling, joint flares, mood, cravings, bloating), gives you the best available picture. Log what you notice alongside the temp data — over 3 months, your personal pattern will emerge.

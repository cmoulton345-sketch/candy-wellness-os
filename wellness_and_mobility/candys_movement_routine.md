## 🔑 The Dual-Mode System: Push & Protect

Your body still cycles hormonally even without a period. Your **Oura ring temperature trend** tells you which mode you're in:

| Mode | Oura Signal | Energy | Training Approach |
|------|-------------|--------|-------------------|
| 🟢 **Push** | Temp at or below baseline | Higher energy, better recovery | Full intensity, progressive overload |
| 🟡 **Protect** | Temp rises 0.3-0.5°F above baseline | Lower energy, joint flares, brain fog | Reduced intensity, more recovery |

> **When in doubt, choose Protect.** You can always push harder tomorrow. You can't un-injure a joint.

## 🟢 PUSH PHASE — Weekly Schedule

### Strength Training: 3x/Week (Mon, Wed, Fri at 6:30 PM)

*Perimenopause Tip: Lift moderate-to-challenging weight with clean form. Rest 60-90 seconds between sets. Progressive overload rule: When you can complete 3 sets of 10 reps with good form, increase weight by 2.5 lb next session.*

#### Day 1 (Mon): Lower Body Strength & Pelvic/Hip Stability
- [ ] **Warm-up (3 min):** 90/90 hip switches + cat-cow (5 reps each)
- [ ] **Chair / Bench Box Squats (Sit-to-Stand):** 3 sets x 10 reps (sit back to bench/chair, pause 1s, drive through heels — zero pelvic/psoas strain)
- [ ] **Glute Bridges with Resistance Band:** 3 sets x 12 reps
- [ ] **Supported Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs):** 3 sets x 8 reps (soft knees, hinge back)
- [ ] **Banded Lateral Walks:** 2 sets x 12 steps each direction
- [ ] **Cool-down:** Reclined figure-4 stretch (45s per side)

#### Day 2 (Wed): Upper Body Posture & Core Integration
- [ ] **Warm-up (3 min):** Arm circles + wall chest stretch
- [ ] **Dumbbell Floor Press:** 3 sets x 10 reps
- [ ] **Supported One-Arm Dumbbell Rows:** 3 sets x 10 reps per arm
- [ ] **Arnold Presses (Seated):** 3 sets x 8 reps
- [ ] **Bird-Dogs:** 3 sets x 8 reps per side
- [ ] **Cool-down:** Child's pose (1 min) + thoracic stretch

#### Day 3 (Fri): Full Body Functional & Power
- [ ] **Warm-up (3 min):** Thoracic rotations + hip openers
- [ ] **Dumbbell Step-Ups (Low Box):** 3 sets x 8 reps per leg
- [ ] **Lat Pulldowns or Banded Pull-Aparts:** 3 sets x 12 reps
- [ ] **Reverse Lunges (Dumbbells):** 3 sets x 8 reps per leg
- [ ] **Farmer's Carries:** 3 laps across room (grip, posture, core)
- [ ] **Cool-down:** Restorative Child's Pose (2 min)

### Yoga: 2x/Week (Wed & Thu at Yoga Haus, 8:15-9:15 AM)
- Continue as-is — excellent for mobility, stress, and nervous system regulation

### Cardio/Walking: 2-3x/Week
- [ ] Power walk with mom and pup (30+ min, any day)
- [ ] Optional: 10-15 min post-dinner walk on non-strength days (aids digestion, lowers evening cortisol)

## 🟡 PROTECT PHASE — Weekly Schedule

When Oura shows elevated temperature, expect: water retention, joint stiffness, mood shifts, increased cravings, brain fog. **This is not weakness. This is biology.**

### Strength Training: 2x/Week (drop to lighter weight, higher reps)

#### Day 1: Lower Body (Gentle)
- [ ] **Warm-up (5 min):** Extended cat-cow + gentle hip circles
- [ ] **Bodyweight Chair Box Squats:** 3 sets x 12 reps (sit back gently to chair/bench)
- [ ] **Glute Bridges (No Band):** 3 sets x 15 reps
- [ ] **Wall Sit:** 2 sets x 20 seconds (gentle, no knee pain)
- [ ] **Cool-down:** Extended figure-4 stretch (60s per side) + pigeon pose

#### Day 2: Upper Body (Gentle)
- [ ] **Warm-up (5 min):** Arm circles + doorway chest stretch
- [ ] **Light Dumbbell Floor Press:** 3 sets x 12 reps
- [ ] **Seated Rows (Light Band):** 3 sets x 12 reps
- [ ] **Seated Overhead Press (Light):** 2 sets x 10 reps
- [ ] **Dead Bugs:** 3 sets x 8 reps per side (core without spinal stress)
- [ ] **Cool-down:** Child's pose + supine twist (2 min total)

### Replace 3rd Strength Day with Gentle Mobility Flow
- [ ] 15-minute pain-relief sequence (see Flare-Up Protocol below)
- [ ] OR: 10-minute lymphatic drainage video
- [ ] OR: Gentle neighborhood walk with mom and pup

### Yoga: Continues as normal (Wed & Thu)
- Yoga is restorative by nature — keep attending even in Protect Phase
- Let the instructor know if joints are flaring — modify poses as needed

## 🌸 Daily Non-Negotiables (Both Phases)

These happen every single day regardless of phase, energy, or mood:

| When | What | Time | Why |
|------|------|------|-----|
| **Upon waking** | 90/90 hip switches + cat-cow | 3 min | Breaks overnight stiffness, wakes up spine |
| **Before bed** | Legs Up the Wall (*Viparita Karani*) | 5-10 min | Drains inflammation, resets nervous system, improves sleep |

### Legs Up the Wall Protocol
1. Place a small folded towel under your hips near the wall
2. Swing legs straight up against the wall
3. Arms out wide like a cactus OR one hand on heart
4. Breathe: 4 counts in, hold 2, 6 counts out
5. Say: *"My day is complete. I let go of everything that happened today. I am safe to rest."*

## 🧘‍♀️ Flare-Up Day? Instant Pain-Adaptive Modifiers

If your hips, lower back, or joints are hurting, **do not push through pain.** Swap your workout for this 15-minute gentle relief sequence:

1. **Reclined Butterfly (Supta Baddha Konasana):** 3 min with pillows under knees — softens pelvic floor and hip flexors
2. **Knee-to-Chest Gentle Rocks:** 2 min — massages sacrum and lower lumbar
3. **Gentle Supine Spinal Twist:** 2 min per side — releases thoracic and low-back tension
4. **Supported Child's Pose:** 2 min with pillow under torso — decompresses spine
5. **Legs Up the Wall:** 5 min — let gravity drain inflammation and reset nervous system

> *"Skipping a workout to recover is not laziness. It is intelligence. Your body is not your enemy — it is asking for care."*

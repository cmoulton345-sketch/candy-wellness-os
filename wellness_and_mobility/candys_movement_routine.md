# Candy's Movement & Mobility Sanctuary

> **Core Mantra:** *“I move my body to nourish and celebrate it, never to punish or control it. My worthiness of love is not on trial.”*

---

## 🌸 Weekly Baseline Rhythm

* **Mornings (3–4 days/wk):** 15–20 minutes gentle wake-up yoga to ease morning soreness and open hips.
* **Evenings at 6:30 PM (3 days/wk):** Strength & weight training (head and heart reset with music).
* **Evenings at 6:30 PM (Alternate/Rest days):** Pain-relief mobility flow or gentle neighborhood walk with mom and pup.
* **Every Night Before Bed:** 5–10 minutes **Legs Up the Wall** (*Viparita Karani*).

---

## 🏋️‍♀️ 3-Day Perimenopause Strength Routine (Joint-Friendly)

*Perimenopause Tip: Avoid burnout. Lift moderate weight with clean form, resting 60–90 seconds between sets to keep cortisol low and joints happy.*

### Day 1: Lower Body Strength & Pelvic/Hip Stability
- [ ] **Warm-up (3 mins):** 90/90 hip switches + cat-cow (5 reps each).
- [ ] **Elevated Goblet Squats:** 3 sets of 8–10 reps (heels elevated on small plates to take pressure off low back and knees).
- [ ] **Glute Bridges with Resistance Band:** 3 sets of 12 reps (protects SI joint and builds pelvic support).
- [ ] **Supported Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs):** 3 sets of 8 reps (soft knees, hinge back toward the wall).
- [ ] **Cool-down:** Reclined figure-4 stretch (hold 45s per side).

### Day 2: Upper Body Posture & Core Integration
- [ ] **Warm-up (3 mins):** Arm circles + wall chest stretch.
- [ ] **Dumbbell Chest Press (Floor or Bench):** 3 sets of 8–10 reps (pressing from the floor limits shoulder joint strain).
- [ ] **Supported One-Arm Dumbbell Rows:** 3 sets of 10 reps (support hand on bench to decompress low back).
- [ ] **Seated Dumbbell Overhead Press:** 3 sets of 8 reps (seated supports lumbar spine).
- [ ] **Bird-Dogs:** 3 sets of 8 reps per side (strengthens core without spinal flexion).

### Day 3: Full Body Functional Movement & Power
- [ ] **Warm-up (3 mins):** Thoracic rotations + hip openers.
- [ ] **Dumbbell Step-Ups (Low Box):** 3 sets of 8 reps per leg.
- [ ] **Lat Pulldowns or Banded Pull-Aparts:** 3 sets of 12 reps.
- [ ] **Farmer's Carries (Moderate Dumbbells):** 3 laps across the room (builds grip, posture, and core stamina).
- [ ] **Restorative Child's Pose:** 2 minutes breathing deep into lower back.

## 🧘‍♀️ Flare-Up Day? Instant Pain-Adaptive Modifiers

If your hips, lower back, or joints are hurting, **do not push through pain**. Swap your weights for this 15-minute gentle relief sequence:

1. **Reclined Butterfly (Supta Baddha Konasana):** 3 minutes with pillows under knees. Softens pelvic floor and hip flexors.
2. **Knee-to-Chest Gentle Rocks:** 2 minutes. Massages the sacrum and lower lumbar.
3. **Gentle Supine Spinal Twist:** 2 minutes per side. Releases thoracic and low-back tension.
4. **Legs Up the Wall:** 5 minutes. Let gravity drain inflammation and reset your nervous system.

## 🌙 The Bedtime Downshift (Legs Up the Wall)
* Place a small folded towel under your hips near the wall.
* Swing your legs straight up against the wall and rest your arms out wide like a cactus or one hand on your heart.
* Close your eyes. Breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 2, breathe out for 6 counts.
* Say: *"My day is complete. I let go of everything that happened today. I am safe to rest."*

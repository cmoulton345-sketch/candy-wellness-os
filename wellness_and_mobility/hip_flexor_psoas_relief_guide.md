# 🦵 Candy's C-Sign Hip Flexor & Psoas Reset Protocol
## Anterior Femoral Glide & Joint Centration Guide (Zero-Pinch Movement)

---

> **The Golden Rule:** **STOP STRETCHING.**  
> The front of your hip is NOT tight from a short muscle—it is **pinching** because the ball of your thigh bone (femur) has drifted slightly forward in the socket (*anterior femoral glide*).  
> Stretching in a lunge forces that bone directly into the inflamed front capsule and labrum, causing the psoas to clamp down in defensive spasm. **You cannot stretch an impingement—you must decompress and centrate the joint.**

---

### 🛑 48-Hour Red Flags (Stop Doing These Right Now)
* ❌ **NO Lunge Stretches:** Do not pull your leg into deep hip extension.
* ❌ **NO Deep Squats or Walking Lunges:** Stop below-90° hip flexion under load.
* ❌ **NO Pilates Tabletop Leg Levers:** Avoid holding your legs in the air with feet unsupported (forces the psoas and TFL into an aggressive protective grip).
* ❌ **NO Crossing Legs When Sitting:** Keep hips slightly higher than knees when seated.

---

### 🛋️ The 3-Step Mechanical Reset (Do This Daily & Before Workouts)

#### 1. The Psoas Lounge (90/90 Gravity Decompression — 5 to 10 Minutes)
* **How:** Lie flat on your back on the floor or bed. Place your lower legs and calves up on a couch, chair, or stack of pillows so your hips and knees are at a relaxed 90-degree angle.
* **Why:** In this 90/90 position, zero muscle effort is required. The psoas and rectus femoris go completely slack. Gravity gently drops the ball of your femur backward into the deep center of the hip socket.
* **Pro-Tip:** Place a warm heating pad right across your front hip crease/groin and take slow, deep 4-second inhales and 6-second melting exhales.

#### 2. Quadruped Rock-Back (Posterior Femoral Glide Centration — 8 to 10 Slow Reps)
* **How:** Come onto all fours (hands and knees). Open your knees slightly wider than your hips, with your big toes touching behind you.
* **Movement:** Keeping your spine completely neutral and flat (do NOT round or tuck your pelvis), slowly rock your hips straight back toward your heels just **3 to 4 inches**.
* **CRITICAL:** **Stop 1 inch before you feel any pinch!** Only move in the 100% pain-free zone. Pause for 2 seconds, then return to all fours.
* **Why:** Mechanically glides the femoral head backward into the acetabulum, clearing impingement space in the front.

#### 3. Gentle Glute Bridge with Pillow Squeeze (Neurological Shut-Off Switch — 5 Reps)
* **How:** Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor, with a yoga block or firm pillow between your knees.
* **Movement:** Squeeze the pillow gently, squeeze your butt cheeks, and lift your hips just **2 to 3 inches** off the floor (a low, flat bridge—do not arch your lower back).
* **Hold:** Hold for 5 seconds at the top, focusing 100% on feeling your glutes fire. Lower slowly.
* **Why (Reciprocal Inhibition):** Neurologically, whenever your gluteus maximus fires, your brain is forced to send an inhibitory signal to the front hip flexor (iliopsoas), commanding it to shut off its defensive spasm.

#### 4. Posterior Capsule & Glute Medius Release (2 Minutes)
* **How:** Place a tennis ball or lacrosse ball under the upper/outer part of your butt cheek (glute medius / piriformis).
* **Why:** Tightness in the back-outside of your hip pushes the thigh bone forward. Releasing the back wall allows the bone to settle backward into place.

### 🏋️ Workout Day Swaps (Keep Training Without Pain)

| Problem Exercise | Why It Pinches | Safe Zero-Pinch Swap |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep Squats** | Femur hits the front rim at bottom | **Chair / Bench Box Squats** (Sit back to chair, pause, drive through heels) |
| **Walking / Reverse Lunges** | Heavy anterior shear on trailing hip | **Banded Glute Kickbacks** or **Bench Glute Thrusts** |
| **Pilates Tabletop Leg Lowers** | High psoas lever strain | **Feet-Flat Pelvic Bridges** or **Supported Knees-to-Chest** |
| **Step-Ups** | Repetitive loaded hip flexion | **Banded Glute Pull-Throughs** |

### 1. Breakfast (8:30 – 9:00 AM) • Target: ~380 kcal | 32g Protein
* **Option A (Grab & Go):**
  * 1 bottle Fairlife Core Power Vanilla or Chocolate (26g protein)
  * 1 slice gluten-free toast (Little Northern Bakehouse) + 1 tbsp sunflower seed butter
  * *MFP Search:* `Fairlife Core Power 26g` + `Little Northern Bakehouse Wide Slice` + `SunButter 1 tbsp`
  * *Macros:* **380 kcal • 32g P • 28g C • 14g F**
* **Option B (5-Min Warm Scramble):**
  * 4 liquid egg whites (carton Naturegg or separated) scrambled with 1 cup baby spinach
  * 1 oz goat cheese or feta (lactose-friendly) + 1/2 cup fresh blueberries on the side
  * *MFP Search:* `Naturegg Simply Egg Whites 180g` + `Goat Cheese crumbles 28g` + `Blueberries 1/2 cup`
  * *Macros:* **340 kcal • 32g P • 18g C • 9g F**
* **Option C (Lactose-Free Parfait):**
  * 3/4 cup 0% Lactose-Free Greek Yogurt (PC or Oikos) or LF 2% Cottage Cheese
  * 1/4 cup gluten-free rolled oats + 1 tbsp chia seeds + dash of cinnamon + 1/2 cup raspberries
  * *MFP Search:* `Lactose Free Greek Yogurt 0% 175g` + `Quaker GF Rolled Oats 20g` + `Chia seeds 1 tbsp`
  * *Macros:* **360 kcal • 34g P • 32g C • 6g F**

### 2. Lunch (1:00 PM - Set Phone Alarm) • Target: ~390 kcal | 35g Protein

  * 4.5 oz skinless rotisserie chicken breast (shredded)
  * 2 cups mixed greens + 1/4 ripe avocado (diced) + 1/2 cucumber + 1 tbsp light vinaigrette
  * 2 plain brown rice cakes (crunch factor)

  * *Macros:* **390 kcal • 36g P • 18g C • 14g F**
* **Option B (Wild Tuna / Salmon Salad):**

  * 6 Mary's Gone Crackers (seed-based, nut-free) + baby carrots or cucumber rounds
  * *MFP Search:* `Canned tuna in water 120g` + `Avocado oil mayo 1 tbsp` + `Mary's Gone Crackers 6`
  * *Macros:* **370 kcal • 35g P • 16g C • 14g F**
* **Option C (Warm Turkey Taco Bowl):**
  * 4 oz extra-lean ground turkey (93/7) cooked with Mexican taco seasoning

  * *MFP Search:* `Ground turkey 93/7 cooked 115g` + `Jasmine rice cooked 1/2 cup` + `Tostitos Salsa 3 tbsp`

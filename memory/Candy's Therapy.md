### A GREAT MARRIAGE: SETTING EXPECTATIONS
**Candy's 6 Pillars (What They Mean to Me)**


#### 1. SEX & INTIMACY
• **Touch Without Expectations:** True intimacy for me starts with physical closeness that carries zero pressure—holding hands, cuddling, and warm touch where nobody is asking anything of my body. That safety is what allows my battery to recharge and desire to grow.

• **Flow Over Schedules:** I love our intimacy and think sex is fun, but putting a target number on it (like 3-4x a week) makes me feel nervous and overwhelmed. I want our intimacy to flow naturally based on our energy, connection, and mood rather than a rigid quota.


#### 2. RADICAL COMMUNICATION
• **My Best Connection Space:** My safest communication doesn't happen face-to-face under pressure; it happens side-by-side—listening to music, going for a drive, or walking together. That's when my mind clears and I can really open up.

• **What I Need You to Know:** Some days I feel all over the map—tired, moody, or wrestling with self-doubt about aging and my body. In those moments, I don't need you to fix it; I just need your patience, reassurance, and gentle love.

• **My Alone Time Signal:** When I say my battery is low and I need quiet time, please know it's not about pushing you away—I love you deeply, I just need a sensory reset so I can be present with you again.


#### 3. COMMON INTERESTS & IDENTITY
• **That "Young Again" Feeling:** Adventure for me isn't about expensive vacations; it's about the excitement of going away, escaping the routine, and feeling young together.

• **Our Shared Adventures:** I want us to stop letting excuses (weather, fatigue, distance) stop us from exploring. I want us to:
  - Go back to the waterfalls together and take sexy pictures
  - Take dance lessons (even if we're goofy and bad at it!)
  - Walk in the park together (snakes, ticks, and all!)
  - Sit on the back deck and just chill with music

• **Breaking Down Barriers:** I realize my internal stress sometimes makes me put up a barrier against going outside. I want to work through those barriers so we can create more simple, spontaneous moments together.


#### 4. "US VS. THE PROBLEM" TEAMWORK
• **Listening to My Voice & Research:** Teamwork to me means when I research topics (like Rayne's OCD or family health) and bring them to you, you listen without judgment and we tackle the problem together as equal partners.

• **Joy Over Competition:** I don't want our life or hobbies to feel like a competition to be the best, hike the longest, or perform. I want teamwork to be about supporting each other's simple joy.

• **Safe Ground to Speak Up:** I am reclaiming my voice after years of feeling like I had to just "sit there and look pretty" or fit in. Teamwork means having patience with me when I feel insecure, and reassuring me that my thoughts and interests matter to you.


#### 5. A SHARED VISION
• **Simplicity & Freedom Over Stuff:** I want a simpler life without the chaos of a big house to clean and maintain. I'm open to downsizing or living in an apartment if it gives us the freedom to travel, walk uptown to coffee shops, and live without chores weighing us down.

• **Just You & Me (Unplugged):** I don't care about having 100 friends or comparing us to couples on Facebook. I just want YOU—your hugs, your kisses, your body, and your heart. I want us to step away from social media and just be present in the moment together.

• **Our Dream Rhythms:**
  - Uptown coffee and snack walks, long walks on the beach, hot tubs under the stars, and growing our own food.
  - Sexy games (like naked darts!), meditating together without rigid schedules, and taking our dog on hikes and park walks as part of the fun rather than an excuse.


#### 6. PLAYFULNESS & LAUGHTER
• **Embracing the Silliness:** Playing in the rain like we did in Calgary! I love your weird noises, acting like a kid, and your jokes. Even when I am stressed and overwhelmed, I NEVER want you to stop being silly.

• **The Little Things:** Getting back to our sweet, playful rhythms—leaving TOYP notes on the bathroom mirror, singing while you play guitar, dancing in the kitchen, and sharing a glass of wine with music.

• **Finding Joy Together & Separately:**
  - I want you to find joy outside of work (playing guitar, hockey, badminton, meditating).
  - I am reclaiming my joy too (like morning yoga!).
  - I don't just want a roommate—I want to sleep next to my sexy, amazing lover and soulmate.

• **My Promise to You:** When I pull away in the moment, it's never about you—it's my own brain and sensory overload. I promise to work on myself so I can be fully ME with you. In order to work on us, I need to be ME!

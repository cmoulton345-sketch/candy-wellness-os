## 2. CRITICAL MEDICATION CONTEXT

### Ozempic (Semaglutide) — 18 clicks
* **Effect on program:** Powerful appetite suppressor. Combined with Vyvanse, creates near-total appetite suppression during the day.
* **Root cause of late-night cravings:** Under-fueling during the day — NOT poor discipline. Medications wear off in evening → body catches up on missed calories → survival-level cravings.
* **Critical risks:** Lean muscle mass loss (counter with 135g+ protein daily + strength training 3x/wk). B12 depletion (supplement 1,000 mcg sublingual B12 daily). GI effects (hydration + fiber + ginger).
* **Program response:** Clock-based eating (not hunger-based). 5 scheduled meals per day. Eat even when not hungry.

### Vyvanse 40mg
* **Dosing time:** ~8:30-9:00 AM
* **Pair with:** ≥30g protein at dosing
* **Avoid:** Acidic drinks within 1 hour (reduces absorption)
* **Peak window:** ~10:30 AM - 12:30 PM (Golden Focus)
* **Wear-off:** ~2:00-3:00 PM (predictable afternoon slump — plan for it)

### Combined Ozempic + Vyvanse Effect
Dual appetite suppression → chronic daytime under-fueling → late-night craving cycle → guilt → restrict → repeat. The ENTIRE late-night craving system is built around breaking this cycle by front-loading calories during the day.

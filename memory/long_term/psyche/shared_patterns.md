### Shared Growth Edge
- Both carry patterns from their families of origin that affect how they give and receive love
- Both struggle with vulnerability in different ways — Joe performs strength; Candy avoids depth
- The breakthrough zone is in their shared moments of "letting go" — when both drop their defenses simultaneously

### Communication Bridge Points
- Joe expressed that Candy is supportive and not dismissive — this is a strong foundation
- Joe's grief is not about Candy failing him; it's about wanting her to see the world the way he now sees it
- Candy's interest in Joe's journey is genuine but her own fears may prevent her from matching his pace

### Watch For
- Joe projecting his growth expectations onto Candy (even subtly)
- Candy saying "the right things" to appease rather than authentic engagement
- Both avoiding the raw, uncomfortable conversations that would deepen their connection

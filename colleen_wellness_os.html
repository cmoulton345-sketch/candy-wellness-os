💡 Why Heavy Lifting Isn't Required Right Now

At age 47 during perimenopause, elevated cortisol from high stress triggers muscle breakdown and abdominal fluid/fat storage. By using resistance bands and 5 lb hand weights with slow controlled reps, we tone your glutes, back, and arms while keeping cortisol flat line low!

Band & 5 lb Dumbbell Routines 🏋️
Designed specifically for Colleen using only loop bands, 5 lb weights, and her treadmill. No joint strain, zero high-intensity cortisol spikes.
Day 1: Glute & Lower Body Toning Bands + Treadmill
Focus: Pelvic stability, glute activation, low-stress cardio
1. Treadmill Warm-Up Walk Treadmill
Speed: 2.0 - 2.5 mph • Incline: 1-2%. Focus on deep diaphragmatic breathing to lower cortisol.
⏱️ 10 - 15 Minutes
2. Banded Standing Glute Kickbacks Loop Band
Place band above knees or ankles. Hold chair for support. Squeeze glute at top without arching lower back.
3 Sets × 12-15 Reps / leg
3. 5 lb Weight Goblet Chair Squats 5 lb Weight
Hold one 5 lb weight at chest. Sit softly onto a dining chair, then press through heels to stand. Keeps knees 100% safe.
3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
4. Banded Glute Bridges Loop Band
Lie on back, band above knees. Drive knees outward against band while lifting hips up. Pause 2 seconds at top.
3 Sets × 15 Reps
Day 2: Upper Body Posture & Sculpt 5 lb Weights + Bands
Focus: Mid-back posture, arm sculpting, core anti-extension
1. Banded Standing Rows Resistance Band
Anchor band to door or foot. Pull elbows back squeezing shoulder blades together. Great for office/desk posture.
3 Sets × 12-15 Reps
2. 5 lb Shoulder Press & Lateral Raises 5 lb Weights
Stand tall, soft knees. Lift 5 lb dumbbells to shoulder height smoothly. Controlled descent.
3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
3. Bicep Curl to Tricep Kickback Super-set 5 lb Weights
Perform 10 curls, then hinge forward slightly and extend weights back for tricep tone.
3 Sets × 10 Reps each
4. Banded Standing Pallof Core Press Loop Band
Hold band at chest anchored to side. Press hands straight forward without letting band rotate your torso. Flattens deep core.
3 Sets × 10 Reps / side
Day 3: Treadmill Incline & Metabolic Flow Treadmill & Recovery
Focus: Fat burning zone without adrenal exhaustion
1. Cortisol-Zone Treadmill Walk Treadmill
Set speed to 2.2 - 2.8 mph. Incline: 2.0% - 4.0%. You should be able to speak comfortably in full sentences.
⏱️ 20 - 30 Minutes
2. 5 lb Weight Romanian Deadlifts 5 lb Weights
Hinge at hips, pushing glutes back while keeping spine neutral. Hold weights close to shins. Tone hamstrings & lower back.
3 Sets × 12 Reps
Simple High-Protein Meals & Recipes 🥗
Delicious, satisfying high-protein meal ideas for Colleen. Designed to keep blood sugar steady, balance HRT hormones, and support belly fat loss while giving her fun cooking variety!
🌅 Fueling High-Protein Breakfasts (28-35g Protein)
1. Greek Yogurt & Berry Power Bowl 32g Protein
3/4 cup 0% or 2% Plain Greek Yogurt, 1 scoop vanilla protein powder stirred in, topped with 1/2 cup blueberries & 1 tbsp chia seeds.
2. Spinach & Feta Egg White Omelet 28g Protein
1 whole egg + 1/2 cup egg whites scrambled with fresh spinach and 2 tbsp crumbled feta. Serve with 1 slice whole grain Ezekiel toast.
3. Cinnamon Oat-Protein Waffles or Pancakes 30g Protein
Blend 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1 scoop vanilla protein powder, 1/3 cup egg whites, pinch of cinnamon, and splash of almond milk. Cook on a waffle iron or skillet! Top with fresh berries.
4. Smoked Salmon & Dill Protein Scramble 34g Protein
2 whole eggs + 1/3 cup egg whites scrambled with 2 oz smoked salmon, fresh dill, and 1 tbsp light cream cheese. High in omega-3s for cortisol reduction!
☀️ Delicious High-Protein Lunches (28-38g Protein)
1. Rotisserie Chicken & Avocado Bowl 36g Protein
4 oz shredded store-bought rotisserie chicken breast over mixed greens, 1/4 avocado, cherry tomatoes, and light vinaigrette dressing.
2. High-Protein Everything Toast 26g Protein
1/2 cup low-fat cottage cheese spread onto 2 slices Ezekiel toast, topped with sliced cucumber, hemp seeds & Everything Bagel seasoning.
3. Garlic Shrimp & Zucchini Noodle Skillet 35g Protein
5 oz peeled shrimp sautéed in 1 tsp olive oil, minced garlic, cherry tomatoes, tossed with spiralized zucchini noodles and fresh lemon juice.
4. Mediterranean Grilled Chicken & Quinoa Salad 38g Protein
4 oz grilled chicken breast over 1/2 cup cooked quinoa, diced cucumbers, kalamata olives, 1 tbsp crumbled feta, and lemon oregano dressing.
🌙 Easy Chef Dinners (35-42g Protein)
1. Sheet-Pan Lemon Herb Salmon 38g Protein
5 oz baked salmon filet or chicken tenderloin with roasted asparagus and 1/2 small sweet potato baked in olive oil & herbs.
2. Lean Turkey & Black Bean Skillet 35g Protein
4 oz 93% lean ground turkey cooked with taco seasoning, 1/4 cup black beans, fresh salsa, served over 1 cup steamed cauliflower rice.
3. Slow-Cooker Salsa Beef & Bell Pepper Bowls 42g Protein
Slow-cooked lean flank steak or lean beef shredded with jarred salsa, bell peppers, and onions. Serve over brown rice or lettuce cups with cilantro!
4. Baked Parmesan Crusted Cod & Broccoli 36g Protein
6 oz wild cod filet topped with 1 tbsp light parmesan and almond flour crust, baked at 400°F alongside olive-oil roasted broccoli.
🍨 Cortisol-Taming Treats & Snacks (20-30g Protein)
1. Chocolate Peanut Butter Protein Mousse 25g Protein
3/4 cup Greek yogurt mixed with 1 tbsp cocoa powder, 1 tbsp PBfit powder, and 1 tsp honey. Tastes like dessert while keeping cortisol low!
2. Savory Cottage Cheese Dip & Bell Peppers 20g Protein
1/2 cup cottage cheese blended smooth with ranch seasoning or herbs, served with crisp sliced red bell peppers and cucumber chips.
3. Fairlife Salted Caramel Protein Iced Coffee 30g Protein
Pour 1 Fairlife Core Power 30g vanilla/caramel shake over espresso or cold brew coffee with ice. Zero sugar spike!
4. Turkey & Swiss Dijon Roll-Ups 24g Protein
4 slices oven-roasted deli turkey rolled up with 1 slice Swiss cheese, spinach, and a brush of Dijon mustard. Zero prep!
5. Crunchy Roasted Edamame & Almond Mix 18g Protein
1/2 cup sea salt dry-roasted edamame combined with 10 raw almonds. High fiber & plant protein for afternoon energy.
6. Frozen Greek Yogurt Berry Bark 22g Protein
Mix 3/4 cup Greek yogurt with 1 scoop vanilla protein, spread thin on parchment paper, top with raspberries & dark chocolate chips, then freeze and break into bark pieces!

# Rayne's OCD Support Playbook for Mom

> **For Candy when the days feel heavy:** *“Rayne's OCD is not your fault. Her distress is not a reflection of your parenting. You do not have to cure her today; you just have to love her through this moment.”*

---

## 🛑 The Reassurance Trap (Why It Hurts Even When It Feels Right)

* **What OCD Does:** OCD creates a spike of intense panic and tells Rayne: *"You must ask mom right now to make sure everything is okay."*
* **The Reassurance Hook:** When you answer the question (*"No honey, you're fine, that didn't happen"*), her anxiety drops for 5 minutes. But then OCD comes back stronger demanding more reassurance.
* **The Shift:** We are shifting from **reassuring the OCD** to **validating Rayne's feelings while supporting her courage**.

---

## 💬 Loving Scripts for Hard OCD Moments

### Scenario 1: Rayne is asking repetitive questions for reassurance
* ❌ *Avoid:* Answering the question for the 5th time or saying "Stop asking me that!"
* ✅ *Say this:*
  > *"Rayne, I love you too much to feed your OCD. You already know the answer to that, and OCD is trying to steal your confidence. Let's take three slow breaths together."*

### Scenario 2: She is in high distress or panic
* ❌ *Avoid:* Trying to reason with OCD logic (OCD cannot be reasoned with in a panic spike).
* ✅ *Say this:*
  > *"I see how scary and exhausting this feels right now. It makes complete sense that you feel overwhelmed. I am right here with you. We don't have to fix this right this second. I'll just sit right beside you while this wave passes."*

### Scenario 3: Medication Titration / Bad Day
* ✅ *Say this:*
  > *"Your brain is adjusting right now, sweet girl. Finding the right dose is a bumpy road, but this hard day is not forever. It is just one day. You are going to be an amazing nurse."*

---

## 🤍 Grounding Steps for Mom (When You Feel Like Crying or Helpless)

1. **Step Out of the Room for 2 Minutes:**
   * Go to the kitchen, run cold water over your wrists, or let the pup outside.
2. **Hand on Heart:**
   * Feel your chest rise and fall. Remind yourself: *"She is safe. I am safe. This is a medical condition, not an emergency."*
3. **Release the Burden:**
   * Whisper: *"I release the impossible job of making my child feel 100% happy every minute. I give myself grace to be human."*

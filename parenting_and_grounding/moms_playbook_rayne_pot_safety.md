# MOM'S PLAYBOOK: WHAT TO SAY IF RAYNE SMOKES AGAIN

> **CORE RULE: ZERO SHAME, MAXIMUM SAFETY**  
> If Rayne comes to you after smoking, her OCD will be screaming that she made a huge mistake. Your job is not to lecture—it is to anchor her nervous system.

---

### SCENARIO 1: IF SHE IS HIGH RIGHT NOW AND PANICKING
**Goal:** Lower her adrenaline, ground her body, and remind her she is safe.

**What to do:**
- Keep your voice low, slow, and calm.
- Put a cold washcloth on her wrists or back of her neck.
- Give her a glass of ice water.

**What to say:**
> *"Rayne, look at me. You are in your home, with your mom, and you are 100% safe."*
> 
> *"Your brain is just reacting to the THC right now. It is a temporary sensation. It will peak, and then it will fade. You are going to be completely okay."*
> 
> *"We don't need to analyze anything tonight. I am going to sit right here with you, get you a warm blanket, and let your body rest while this passes."*

---

### SCENARIO 2: THE NEXT MORNING (MANAGING THE HANGOVER SPIKE)
**Goal:** Remind her that next-day anxiety is a chemical hangover, not brain damage.

**What to say:**
> *"Good morning, sweet girl. How are you feeling?"*
> 
> *"If your OCD is trying to tell you today that you ruined your brain or ruined your Prozac, remember our rule: that is just the chemical hangover talking."*
> 
> *"Drink some water, eat something comforting, and take your regular meds. Your body knows how to clear this out, and I've got you."*

---

### SCENARIO 3: THE OPEN-DOOR SAFETY AGREEMENT (TELL HER THIS THIS WEEK)
**Goal:** Make sure she never lies to you or hides a bad reaction.

**What to say to Rayne:**
> *"Rayne, I want you to hear me clearly: You are 18, and I know you are navigating life and making your own choices. If you ever smoke pot again, or if you ever feel scared or uncomfortable after doing it, I never want you to hide from me."*
> 
> *"You will NEVER be in trouble with me for telling me the truth. My only priority is keeping you safe and helping your brain stay calm. You can always call me or come to me, no matter what."*

---

### 🛡️ HOW TO STOP THE OCD REASSURANCE LOOP
Rayne's OCD might try to ask you 10 times: *"Are you sure my brain isn't ruined?"*

If she asks again after you've had this chat, do not re-explain the science (that feeds the OCD reassurance loop). Instead, say:

> *"Rayne, we already talked through the science and you know your brain is safe. That worry popping back up right now is just your OCD trying to make you doubt it. Let me sit with you while that feeling passes."*

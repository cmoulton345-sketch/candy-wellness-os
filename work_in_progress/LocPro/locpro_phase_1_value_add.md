# Phase 1: The "Value Add" Email

**Subject:** A few ideas for LocPro's digital growth / Website feedback
**To:** [Client Name]

Hi [Client Name],

I was taking a look at the LocPro Construction website today and put together a few quick notes on some massive opportunities I’m seeing in the market right now—specifically for landing high-value commercial contracts and adapting to the way search engines are changing this year. 

I wanted to send these over for you to think about:

**1. The LinkedIn B2B Goldmine**
While homeowners might find you on Facebook, the people who hold the lucrative contracts—property managers, strata councils, and real estate developers—are on LinkedIn. There is a huge opportunity for you to leverage a "founder-led" content strategy there. By posting case studies, before/afters of fire breaks, and insights on BC fire compliance from your personal profile, you position LocPro as the ultimate risk-management partner for big commercial jobs.

**2. The Shift to "AI Search" (GEO)**
Google is changing. People are increasingly using AI (like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity) to find services. Traditional SEO doesn't work the same way anymore. We need to transition your site to Generative Engine Optimization (GEO). This means restructuring your service pages to be "Answer-First" and embedding specific Schema code in the backend so AI bots instantly recognize LocPro as the #1 verifiable authority for wildfire prevention in BC.

**3. Interactive Lead Generation**
Right now, the website relies on a standard "Contact Us" form. We could add a simple, interactive "Wildfire Risk Calculator" to the site. Homeowners or property managers answer 5 quick questions about their property to get a free risk score. It provides massive immediate value to them, and captures high-quality leads directly into your inbox.

I’d love to walk you through exactly how this works and show you some examples. Do you have 10 minutes for a quick call sometime this week?

# FlowstateAI vs. Market: Pricing Analysis (2025/2026)

Based on current market data for AI Automation Agencies (AIAAs), here is how FlowstateAI's pricing structure compares to industry standards.

## 1. The Sprint (Project-Based Flat Fee)
**FlowstateAI Price:** $3,000 flat
**Market Average:** $2,500 – $10,000+ (for basic to mid-level automation/chatbots)
**Enterprise Custom Builds:** $15,000 – $50,000+

* **Analysis:** Your $3,000 flat fee for a 5-day "Done-in-a-Week" sprint is **highly competitive and leans toward the accessible/low end** of the market for custom AI workflow builds. 
* **Positioning:** This is a very strong loss-leader or entry-level offer. The 5-day turnaround time is a massive selling point compared to traditional agencies that take weeks. It lowers the barrier to entry for small-to-midsize businesses (SMBs) who might balk at a $10k upfront project fee.

## 2. Growth Retainer (Ongoing Monthly)
**FlowstateAI Price:** $1,200 / month
**Market Average (SMBs):** $1,500 – $6,000 / month 
**Market Average (Basic Maintenance only/No new builds):** $500 - $1,000 / month

* **Analysis:** At $1,200/mo, you are **underpricing the market slightly** considering your deliverables. Your plan includes "1 New Workflow per Month" *plus* 24/7 monitoring and break/fix support. Most agencies charge $1,500+ just for maintenance and minor tweaks; building a net-new workflow every month usually pushes a retainer into the $2,500 - $5,000+ tier.
* **Positioning:** You are positioned exceptionally well to close volume deals here. However, as your client base grows, you may find that building a custom net-new workflow every single month for $1,200 eats into your margins significantly. 

## 3. Content Engine Add-on
**FlowstateAI Price:** +$400 / month
**Market Average (AI Social/SEO management):** $1,000 – $3,200 / month

* **Analysis:** This is **very inexpensive** compared to standalone AI marketing or content generation retainers, which easily average over $2,000/mo.
* **Positioning:** As an *add-on* to an existing $1,200 retainer, it makes sense to discount it. It's a "no-brainer" upsell at that price point, bringing the total MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) for a fully-serviced client to $1,600/month.

---

## Overall Strategic Verdict

**You are priced for rapid market penetration and high conversion.** 
Your structure is incredibly compelling for your target audience ("High-Volume Service Teams, Solo Practitioners, Rapid Growth Agencies").

**Recommendations to consider as you scale:**
1. **The $3k Sprint is perfect right now:** Keep it here to build your portfolio and case studies. Once you have 5-10 undeniable case studies, you could easily raise this to $4,500 or $5,000 without losing conversions, simply because the "done in 5 days" promise is so valuable.
2. **Cap the Retainer Scope:** "1 New Workflow per Month" for $1,200 is a steal for the client. Be explicitly clear in your contracts about what constitutes a "workflow" (e.g., maximum 5 connected apps, no custom database architecture) so clients don't ask for a $10,000 enterprise-grade AI build as their "monthly workflow." 
3. **Future Tiering:** Eventually, you may want to introduce a "$2,500/mo Scale Retainer" for clients who need multiple workflows built per month, keeping the $1,200 option for "Maintenance + 1 minor optimization."

## 1. By-Law No. 1
*A by-law relating generally to the transaction of the business and affairs of FlowstateAiAutomation Inc.*

### Article I: Interpretation & Offices
1. **Definitions:** In this By-Law, "Act" means the *Canada Business Corporations Act*, and "Corporation" means FlowstateAiAutomation Inc.
2. **Registered Office:** The registered office of the Corporation shall be at such location in the province specified in the Articles as the directors may from time to time determine. (Current: 17 Flagstone Drive, Quispamsis, NB, E2G 0B7).

### Article II: Directors
1. **Powers:** The business and affairs of the Corporation shall be managed or supervised by the sole director.
2. **Quorum:** Where there is a sole director, that director constitutes a quorum for any meeting of directors.
3. **Resolutions in Writing:** A resolution in writing, signed by the sole director, is as valid as if it had been passed at a meeting of directors.

### Article III: Officers
1. **Appointment:** The directors may designate the offices of the Corporation, appoint officers, specify their duties and delegate to them powers to manage the business and affairs of the Corporation.
2. **Holding Multiple Offices:** A person may hold more than one office. The sole director may be appointed to any office of the Corporation.
3. **Duties of Officers:**
   * **President:** Shall be the chief executive officer and have general supervision of the business.
   * **Secretary:** Shall attend and keep minutes of all meetings, enter them in the corporate records, and give notices.
   * **Treasurer:** Shall keep proper accounting records and deposit all money and valuables in the name of the Corporation.

### Article IV: Shares & Shareholders
1. **Allotment:** Shares of the Corporation may be issued at such times and to such persons and for such consideration as the directors may determine.
2. **Share Certificates:** Share certificates shall be signed by the President or a director.
3. **Meetings:** Shareholders' meetings shall be held at such place as the directors determine. A resolution in writing signed by the sole shareholder entitled to vote is as valid as if it had been passed at a meeting.

### Article V: Banking & Execution of Documents
1. **Signing Authority:** Deeds, transfers, assignments, contracts, obligations, and other instruments in writing requiring execution by the Corporation may be signed by the sole director or any officer.
2. **Banking Arrangements:** The banking business of the Corporation shall be transacted with such bank or trust company as the director may designate.

The Landscape

The trades businesses growing fastest aren't just the best tradespeople. They're the most responsive ones.

Whether you're in HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, or landscaping, the market in Atlantic Canada is competitive and deeply seasonal. The businesses winning the most jobs aren't always the most experienced — they're the ones who respond first, follow up consistently, and stay top of mind when the seasonal call comes.

AI automation handles the quote follow-up, appointment scheduling, seasonal marketing, and customer communication that most trade operators never have time to do — freeing you to stay in the field doing the work that earns revenue.

The Full Opportunity

AI automation works across every part of your trades business.

⚡

Lead Response & Quote Follow-Up

Win more jobs by being first and most consistent
  • High ROI

    Instant Lead Response (24/7)

    Every inquiry — website, Facebook, Google, referral — gets an immediate personalized response within minutes. The trades business that responds first wins most often, even if they're not the cheapest.

  • High ROI

    Online Estimate Booking

    Customers book their own estimate appointments without playing phone tag — 24/7. Especially powerful for after-hours emergency inquiries and weekend requests when you can't answer.

  • High ROI

    Quote Follow-Up Sequence

    After every estimate, AI sends a structured follow-up sequence at 24 hours, 3 days, and 7 days — recovering jobs that went quiet simply because no one followed up. Most operators estimate 20–30% of their quoted work is recoverable this way.

  • Medium ROI

    Emergency Request Auto-Response

    For urgent service requests (burst pipe, heating failure, power outage), AI sends an immediate acknowledgment and dispatcher notification — so no emergency goes unanswered while you're on another job.

📅

Scheduling & Appointment Management

Keep your calendar full and your team organized
  • High ROI

    Appointment Confirmation & Reminders

    Automated confirmation when a job is booked, plus a reminder 24 hours before — significantly reducing the no-shows and forgotten appointments that waste your crew's time and travel.

  • High ROI

    Technician Dispatch Notifications

    When a crew member is en route, an automated "on the way" message goes to the customer with an estimated arrival time — eliminating the calls your office receives asking "when will they be there?"

  • Efficiency

    Job Completion & Invoice Delivery

    When a job is completed, AI automatically sends the invoice and a follow-up message — removing the administrative delay between job completion and getting paid.

  • Efficiency

    Staff Scheduling & Shift Reminders

    Automated schedule confirmations and shift reminders for crew members — reducing the last-minute coordination that wastes management time.

📣

Seasonal Marketing & Revenue

Get in front of customers before they call a competitor
  • High ROI

    Seasonal Maintenance Campaign Sequences

    Before spring and fall, AI automatically reaches out to your entire past customer list with seasonal tune-up offers — HVAC filter changes, furnace inspections, gutter cleaning, winterization. Fills your calendar proactively instead of reactively.

  • High ROI

    Past Customer Re-Engagement

    Customers who haven't booked in 12–18 months receive a personalized re-engagement message — a seasonal offer or maintenance check-in that recovers business before they call someone new.

  • Medium ROI

    Service Anniversary Reminders

    "It's been one year since we installed your furnace — time for a seasonal tune-up?" Automated, perfectly timed reminders keep your recurring maintenance business locked in.

  • Medium ROI

    Referral Request Automation

    After every completed job, AI sends a thank-you with a simple referral request. Referrals are 5× more likely to convert than cold leads — and your best customers give them most willingly right after a job well done.

⭐

Reputation & Reviews

Your Google rating wins more jobs than any ad
  • High ROI

    Post-Job Review Requests

    After every completed job, AI sends a review request while satisfaction is highest. Trades businesses with 50+ reviews at 4.5+ stars win significantly more jobs from Google searches — especially in competitive markets.

  • Brand

    Feedback Collection & Service Recovery

    A private feedback option before the public review — catching any concerns so you can address them directly before they become a negative Google review.

⚙️

Business Operations

Run a tighter ship without more overhead
  • Efficiency

    Invoice Payment Reminders

    Automated professional follow-ups on outstanding invoices at 7, 14, and 30 days — improving cash flow without awkward phone calls chasing payment.

  • Efficiency

    Material & Supply Low-Stock Alerts

    When key parts or materials hit a low threshold, AI triggers a reorder alert — preventing job delays caused by missing materials.

  • Efficiency

    Weekly Job & Revenue Reports

    Automated weekly summaries of jobs completed, revenue billed, quote conversion rates, and outstanding invoices — delivered to ownership without anyone pulling data manually.

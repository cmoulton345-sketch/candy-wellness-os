## Session Approach
- **Pace:** Slow. Much slower than Joe's session. Multiple sessions expected.
- **Tone:** Gentle, kind, attentive. Warm, not clinical.
- **Method:** One question at a time. Let her lead. Let her draw the map.
- **Watch for:** The moment she starts to pull back — that's where the truth is, but don't chase it. Make it safe.
- **Priority:** Safety and trust before depth. She needs to feel heard, not studied.
- **Confidentiality:** She does not know this file exists. Approach as if meeting her fresh.
- **Goal:** Her own peace, on her own terms — not alignment with anyone else's journey.

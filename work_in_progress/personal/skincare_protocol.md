# ⚙️ SOMA: The Mechanical Skin Protocol

## 1. The Shave (Mechanical Fix)
- **Hardware Tool:** Single-blade double-edged safety razor. **Recommendation:** *Henson AL13*. It mechanically forces a precise blade angle, making it nearly impossible to cut beneath the skin surface.
- **Lubrication:** Use a non-foaming shave cream (e.g., *Cremo* or *Vanicream Shave Cream*). Do NOT use aerosol gels or foams. Aerosol products contain propellants and harsh surfactants that chemically strip the lipid barrier.
- **Action:** Shave *with* the grain of the hair. Never against it. 
- *Why:* Multi-blade razors cut below the skin, causing the hair to get trapped when it retracts, directly creating ingrown hairs.

## 2. The Wash (Barrier Fix)
- **Tool:** Gentle, low-pH cleanser (e.g., CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser, Cetaphil, or La Roche-Posay). Drop the Ivory soap.
- **Action:** Wash using *only* your bare, clean hands and lukewarm water. No washcloths. No scouring pads.
- *Why:* Highly alkaline soaps strip the skin's biological moisture barrier, triggering emergency oil production and inflammation. Physical abrasion spreads bacteria.

## 3. The Actives (Inflammation Control)

**MORNING:**
1. Wash with gentle cleanser (bare hands).
2. Apply Niacinamide 10% + Zinc 1% (calms inflammation & regulates oil).
3. Apply a simple, unscented moisturizer.

**EVENING:**
1. Wash with gentle cleanser (bare hands).
2. Apply a leave-on **2% Salicylic Acid** (BHA) liquid/serum. Use only 2-3 nights a week.
3. Apply a simple moisturizer.
- *Why:* Separate the cleaning from the chemical exfoliation. A leave-on BHA dissolves trapped sebum inside the pore without destroying the skin's surface barrier.

## 🚨 Active Ingrown Hair Protocol 🚨

1. **Cease Mechanical Trauma:** Do NOT shave over the active bumps. Give the area a 48 to 72-hour rest.
2. **Heat & Hydration:** Apply a hot, damp compress for 3-5 minutes, twice daily. (Softens dead skin and increases blood flow to naturally push the hair out).
3. **Chemical Release:** Spot-treat the active bumps with your 2% Salicylic Acid leave-on to dissolve the dead skin capping the follicle.
4. **Surgical Extraction:** *Only* if the hair is visibly looping above the skin, use sterile tweezers to gently lift it out. 
- **CRITICAL RULE:** NEVER SQUEEZE IT. Squeezing forces bacteria deeper into the dermis, causing deep, painful cysts. Let the heat and the acid do the work.

## 📋 PART 1: PRE-CALL PREP (Before Every Discovery Call)

### What To Do 15 Minutes Before
- [ ] Review their booking form: What automation did they pick? What's their business name?
- [ ] Google their business — look at their Google reviews, website, Facebook page
- [ ] Build *that* automation in n8n — have it ready to deploy live on the call
- [ ] Open your Call Notes doc (below) with their name ready
- [ ] Be on camera. Be human. This is Atlantic Canada — people buy from people they trust

### Pre-Built Automation Checklist
| They Picked | What to Have Ready |
|---|---|
| Appointment Reminder | n8n workflow: booking trigger → wait → SMS/email at 24hr + 2hr |
| Google Review Request | n8n workflow: appointment complete → wait 2hrs → send review link via email/SMS |
| Missed Call Text-Back | n8n workflow: incoming call webhook → instant SMS reply |
| Instant Lead Follow-up | n8n workflow: form submit → 60-second email/SMS with rep's name |
| No-Show Recovery | n8n workflow: no-show tag → 30-min delay → re-book text |
| Lapsed Customer Win-Back | n8n workflow: 90-day no purchase → personalized re-engagement email |

## 📞 PART 2: THE DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT

### Opening (Minutes 0–3) — Be a Human First
> "Hey [Name], great to connect! I saw you booked to get [their automation choice] set up — I've actually already built that for you, so we'll get to deploying it shortly. But first, tell me a bit about [Business Name] — how long have you been running it?"

**Let them talk.** Listen for:
- How long in business (signals stability)
- Size of team (signals complexity + budget)
- Their biggest frustration right now (pure gold — use their exact words back later)
- Whether they've tried automation before

---

### The Demo (Minutes 3–15) — Do This Early, It Changes Everything
> "Alright — want to see what I built for you? Let me share my screen."

**Walk them through the workflow you pre-built:**
1. Show the trigger: *"So when someone books with you..."*
2. Show the automation: *"...this fires automatically. No one has to remember to do it."*
3. Ask: *"Does that look like something that would actually help you?"*
4. **Deploy it live** — connect their account, test it, make it real

> "Okay — that's now running. You'll never have to think about [their problem] again."

**Pause. Let that sink in.**

---

### The Discovery Questions (Minutes 15–25)

After the demo — now they trust you. Ask these:

**Business Context:**
1. *"What does a typical month look like for you — roughly how many new clients or appointments?"*
2. *"What's your biggest time drain right now — what eats your week that you wish you didn't have to do?"*
3. *"Are you on any CRM or booking system, or is it mostly manual right now?"*

**Pain Surfacing:**
4. *"When a new lead comes in, what actually happens? Walk me through it."*
   *(Most answer: nothing automatic — they manually call back, sometimes days later)*
5. *"How much time would you say your team spends on [their pain point] per week?"*
6. *"If that was handled automatically — what would you do with that time?"*

**Vision / Aspiration:**
7. *"What would your business look like in 12 months if the back-end was just... running itself?"*

**Buying Signal Questions:**
8. *"Have you looked at any automation tools before?"*
   *(If yes: "What happened with that?")*
9. *"Is this something you're trying to figure out on your own, or are you open to having someone build and manage it for you?"*

---

### The Transition to the Offer (Minute 25)

**If they're engaged, use this bridge:**

> "So here's what I'm seeing. You've got [X problem they mentioned] eating [Y time they said]. And you're trying to [their goal]. The automation we just ran handles one piece of that. But the businesses I work with that see the biggest results — they're not just running one automation. They've got a full system: leads coming in, getting followed up instantly, appointments confirmed, reviews requested, and lapsed customers re-engaged. It basically runs the client relationship on autopilot."

> "That's what I build and manage for businesses like yours. Want me to show you what that would look like specifically for [Business Name]?"

*(If yes → move to the offer. If hesitant → see objections section.)*

## 🛡️ PART 4: OBJECTION HANDLING

### "I need to think about it."
> "Totally fair — what's the main thing you want to think through? Is it the investment, or is it more about whether this is the right timing?"

*(Let them say which. Then address that specific thing. Don't give a generic response.)*

If timing: *"What changes in [X weeks] — what would need to be true for you to feel ready?"*
If investment: *"When you think about what [their problem] is costing you right now — in time or in missed revenue — what's your sense of the gap?"*

---

### "It's too expensive."
> "That's fair — let me ask you something. [Their automation choice] — if it fires once a day and saves you 15 minutes, that's 90 hours a year. What would you pay for 90 hours back?"

OR if they're NB-based:
> "There's also the grant angle — depending on your business structure, you may be able to offset 30–75% of this through the AI Adoption Program. That would bring the Starter down to under $150/month. Worth exploring before you rule it out."

---

### "We're already using [tool X]."
> "That's great — what is it doing for you? ... What's it *not* doing that you wish it was?"

*(Almost always they'll name a gap. That gap is your opening.)*

---

### "I don't have time to deal with this right now."
> "That's exactly why this works for you — you don't deal with it. We build it, we monitor it, we fix it. Your only job is to notice it's running. How much time did you spend on our call today? That's less time than you'll ever spend on this again."

---

### "Can you send me some information?"
**Don't just send a PDF and hope.** Say:
> "Absolutely. Can I ask — what's the one thing you'd want the info to convince you of? I'll make sure what I send actually addresses that."

Then send a **personal video** (Loom, 2 minutes) walking through exactly what you'd build for them. Not a brochure — *their* system.

## ✅ PART 5: THE CLOSE

### The Soft Close (Use First)
> "Based on what you've told me, I think the [Starter/Growth] system makes the most sense for where you are. Want to get started?"

**Then stop talking.** The next person who speaks has lower leverage. Let there be silence.

---

### The Summary Close (If They Need More)
> "Okay, so here's what we're doing. You're getting [list their 3–6 automations]. It's going to handle [their main pain point] automatically from day one. I'll have the whole system live within 7 days. The investment is [price]/month, and I'll waive the setup fee since we're doing this today. Sound good?"

---

Once they say yes:

> "Perfect. I'm sending you the invoice right now — if you can take care of that today it lets me prioritize your build this week. I'll also send a quick summary of what we're building and what to expect."

## 🚀 PART 8: ONBOARDING (After They Pay)

Once the invoice clears:

1. **Send the "You're In" email** (Template A updated with specifics)
2. **Book the onboarding call** — 60 min, within 5 days
3. **Create their n8n workflow folder**
4. **At the onboarding call:**
   - Collect access credentials
   - Deploy all automations live on the call
   - Test each one together
   - Walk them through what to expect
   - Set a 30-day check-in call

**Onboarding call agenda:**
- [ ] Welcome + recap what we're building (5 min)
- [ ] Collect credentials (10 min)
- [ ] Deploy Automation 1 + test (10 min)
- [ ] Deploy Automation 2 + test (10 min)
- [ ] Deploy remaining automations (10 min)
- [ ] Walk-through: how to know it's working (5 min)
- [ ] Set 30-day check-in (5 min)
- [ ] Ask for a review (once they see it's live!) (5 min)

## 🔍 HOW FLOWSTATEAI IS DIFFERENT

### What Most Competitors Are Doing
Most Atlantic Canada AI competitors fall into one of two buckets:

**Bucket 1 — Tech-First (Chatbots & Voice Agents)**
DigiBenders, The Automators, HummingAgent, Autonoly — primarily sell AI chatbots and voice receptionist tools. Technology-centric pitch. Less focus on business outcomes, more on features.

**Bucket 2 — Strategy & Consulting (No Implementation)**
AI-First Consulting, Sync Digital, Agyle Intelligence — they advise and train but often don't actually build and run the systems. High-hour, high-cost engagements.

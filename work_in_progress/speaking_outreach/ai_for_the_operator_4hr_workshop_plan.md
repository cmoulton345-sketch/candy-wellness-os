## 💰 Hour 2: Module 2 — Operational Drag Tax Calculation (30 Mins)

### **The 3-Step Drag Tax Formula**

1.  **Monthly Base Drag:**
    $$\text{Monthly Base Drag} = (\text{Monthly Admin Hours Wasted} \times \text{Hourly Rate}) + (\text{Monthly Value of New Customers Lost})$$
2.  **The Competitive Multiplier (Avg 3 AI Competitors):**
    $$\text{Total Monthly Drag Tax} = \text{Monthly Base Drag} \times 3 \text{ Local AI Competitors}$$
3.  **Annual Profit Loss:**
    $$\text{Annual Drag Tax Bleed} = \text{Total Monthly Drag Tax} \times 12 \text{ Months}$$


## 💡 Hour 4: Simple Operator Efficiency Hacks & Model Selector

### 1. The "Grill Me" Interview Technique (Instant Personalization)
*   **The Hack:** Instead of spending 20 minutes trying to write a complex prompt, tell the AI:  
    > *"I want to write a [proposal / newsletter / sales email]. Don't write anything yet. Interview me one question at a time until you have all the information you need to make this sound exactly like me."*
*   **Why It Works:** Eliminates generic AI sounding responses. It extracts authentic human voice and key business details effortlessly.

### 2. Handover Documents (Saving Credits & Time)
*   **The Hack:** Create a single, standardized 1-page `Company_Context.md` reference document containing:
    *   Target Audience & Offerings
    *   Brand Tone & Style Guide
    *   Key Differentiators & Forbidden Words
*   **Why It Works:** Upload or paste this doc into new chat sessions to give the AI instant context without re-typing rules or burning unnecessary model credits.

### 3. The Model Selection Matrix (Which AI to Use for What)

| Model / Tool | Primary Strengths | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Claude (Anthropic)** | Nuanced human tone, long-form writing, coding, complex logic | Proposals, sales copy, policy writing, workflow automation |
| **ChatGPT / GPT-4o** | Versatile, fast, web search, voice mode, image generation | Quick daily drafting, brainstorming, voice dictation, web lookups |
| **Google Gemini** | Massive 2M+ token context window, Google Drive integration | Reading massive PDFs, searching Drive docs, analyzing large video/audio |
| **Perplexity AI** | Real-time web search with verified sources & citations | Market research, competitor analysis, factual lookups, news summary |

### 4. Voice-to-Prompt Dictation
*   **The Hack:** Use mobile voice mode or OS dictation to speak messy, raw thoughts for 60 seconds, then end with:  
    > *"Organize my thoughts above into a clean 3-bullet action plan."*
*   **Why It Works:** Operators think faster than they type. Reduces friction to zero.

### 5. The Skeptical Prospect Feedback Loop
*   **The Hack:** Before sending a major proposal or marketing message, ask the AI:  
    > *"Act as a skeptical, busy CEO in my target market. Read this proposal draft and tell me the top 3 reasons you would reject it."*
*   **Why It Works:** Instant stress-test of sales copy before sending to real clients.

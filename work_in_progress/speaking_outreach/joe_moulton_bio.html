About

Joe Moulton didn't start in technology — he started in the trenches with entrepreneurs. As a business coach with Tony Robbins and Dean Graziosi's Mastermind.com, Joe worked directly with hundreds of business owners to help them launch, grow, and scale. The pattern was always the same: talented operators drowning in admin, follow-ups, and manual processes that should have been running themselves. They didn't need more motivation. They needed better systems.

Joe is also a clinical hypnotist and author of The Unspoken Contract, giving him deep expertise in human behavior and decision psychology — skills that now inform how he designs AI systems that drive real engagement.

Today, FlowstateAI Automation serves established businesses across all three Maritime provinces, building custom AI systems that automate client acquisition, follow-up, onboarding, and operations — giving Atlantic Canadian businesses the same infrastructure that larger markets take for granted.

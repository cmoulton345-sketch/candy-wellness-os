#### **Session Overview**
Every week, business owners are bombarded with headlines about new AI tools, massive enterprise platforms, and disruptive software. But behind the hype, most growth-stage operators face a very practical question: *"What actually works for a business my size, in this region, right now—and how do I implement it without blowing up my operations?"*

*AI for the Operator* is a fluff-free, action-oriented session designed specifically for CEOs, business owners, and operational leaders. Instead of high-level theory or generic software demos, Joe Moulton breaks down the exact automation architectures, financial calculations, and practical frameworks established companies are using to reclaim 15+ hours per week per employee and capture revenue that currently slips through the cracks.

#### **What Attendees Will Learn & Take Away:**
1. **Operational AI Audit:** How to map out repetitive manual tasks across sales, intake, and admin, ranking opportunities by effort vs. financial yield.
2. **Calculating Your Operational Drag Tax:** A concrete financial model to calculate what slow response times and routine admin are costing the business in lost profit and staff fatigue.
3. **Building Your First Automations:** Architectural schematics for high-ROI workflows—including instant 5-second lead response engines and automated client onboarding.
4. **Atlantic Canadian Funding Opportunities:** A clear guide to regional programs (ACOA, IRAP, NRC, and provincial grants) that offset implementation costs by 50% to 75%.
5. **Advanced AI Prompting:** Executive frameworks for system prompt architecture, context layering, and output control to get reliable, professional outputs from AI tools.

Executive Action Principles

The Operator's Manifesto:
How to Lead Without Getting Burned

As you lead your organization through this shift, hold your teams and external partners to these three non-negotiable standards:

🏛️

1. Own the Infrastructure

Never adopt a generic software wrapper where you don't control the underlying data flows. Require event-driven systems that integrate directly into your existing software stack.

🛡️

2. Sovereign Governance

AI must never mutate databases, send proposals, or execute payments without deterministic checks. Require local PII tokenization under Canadian laws (PIPEDA) and human approvals.

🎯

3. Friction Before Flash

Ignore AI hype and vanity demos. Measure every potential automation project against a single metric: **How many weekly hours of drag will this recover, and what is the exact revenue impact?**

## 💡 Quick Tips for Live Delivery
* **The Tactile Cards & Pens:** Ensure your physical Drag Cards are printed on thick cardstock (so ink doesn't bleed through) and placed at every seat before the room opens, with the Flowstate pen right on top of the card.
* **Using `F11` and `N`:** When presenting the HTML deck, `F11` gives full screen, `Space` or `Arrow Right` advances, and pressing `N` pops up your teleprompter notes at the bottom of the screen.

### 📝 MODULE 1: The Operational AI Audit (Minutes 05 – 15)

*   **Slide 4: Where is Your Business Leaking Time & Money?**
    *   *Core Concept:* The 3 Friction Zones: Sales/Lead Capture, Client Onboarding & Admin, and Lapsed Client Follow-Up.
*   **Slide 5: Interactive Exercise — The "I Hate This" Audit**
    *   *Attendee Action:* Attendees take 2 minutes on their worksheet to list top manual bottlenecks across their operations.
    *   *Audit Criteria:*
        *   Frequency (How often does it happen daily/weekly?)
        *   Repetitiveness (Is it governed by clear, predictable rules?)
        *   Human Fatigue (Does it cause delay or employee burnout?)
*   **Slide 6: The Opportunity Matrix**
    *   *Visual:* 2x2 Matrix: Implementation Complexity (Low vs. High) vs. Financial Value (Low vs. High).
    *   *Key Takeaway:* Always target **High Value + Low/Medium Complexity** first (e.g., instant inquiry response and automated scheduling).

### 💰 MODULE 2: Calculating Operational Drag Tax (Minutes 15 – 25)

*   **Slide 7: What is Your "Operational Drag Tax"?**
    *   *Definition:* The hidden ongoing cost of high-value team members performing low-leverage administrative work, combined with lost revenue from delayed lead response.
*   **Slide 8: The 5-Second Rule in Sales**
    *   *Data Point:* 78% of customers buy from the business that responds first.
    *   *Industry Baseline:* Average business lead response time is **15+ hours**. AI response time is **<5 seconds**.
*   **Slide 9: Live Drag Tax Formula**
    *   *Formula Breakdown:*  
        $$\text{Drag Tax} = (\text{Hours Spent on Admin/Week} \times \text{Hourly Labor Rate}) + (\text{Monthly Inquiries Lost to Slow Response} \times \text{Average Customer Value})$$
    *   *Live Case Study:* Local service firm with 15 employees recovering \$6,500/month in billable time and closing 3 extra clients per month by eliminating manual drag.

### 🧠 MODULE 5: Advanced AI Prompting for Business Leaders (Minutes 50 – 60)

*   **Slide 16: Beyond Basic Chat — Executive Prompt Architecture**
    *   *The Shift:* Stop asking ChatGPT simple questions; start building structured **System Instructions**.
*   **Slide 17: The 4-Layer Prompting Framework**
    1.  **Role & Context:** Who the AI is acting as (e.g., "Senior Intake Specialist for a Maritime Law Firm").
    2.  **Task & Goal:** Precise task boundaries and clear objective.
    3.  **Reference Constraints:** Rules on what NOT to say, format guidelines, zero-hallucination guardrails.
    4.  **Output Template:** Exact JSON, Markdown, or email format required.
*   **Slide 18: Before & After Prompt Demonstration**
    *   *Before:* "Write a response to a prospective client asking for pricing." (Vague, generic, risky)
    *   *After:* Structured 4-Layer Prompt (Delivers personalized, brand-compliant, high-converting outreach).

## What Is Speakers Nova Scotia?

A non-profit association for professional speakers in Nova Scotia. They maintain an **online Speakers Registry** that event organizers across NS use to find speakers. Joining puts FlowstateAI in front of every event organizer in the province passively — they come to you.

## Why Join

- Event organizers across NS search the registry when planning conferences, member meetings, and professional development days
- It's passive lead generation — once you're registered, you appear in searches for your topic areas
- Complements all the active outreach we're doing — gives inbound credibility

## Action Required

1. Visit **speakersnovascotia.ca**
2. Register as a member speaker
3. Complete your speaker profile with:
   - Topic areas: AI automation, business technology, entrepreneurship, operational efficiency
   - Formats: keynote, workshop, panel, lunch-and-learn
   - Industries served: dental, automotive, real estate, restaurant, trades, retail, legal
4. Upload a headshot and speaker bio

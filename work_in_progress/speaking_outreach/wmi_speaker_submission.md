## Proposed Talk: "AI for the Operator"

> *Subtitle: What's actually working right now for businesses your size — and what's still noise.*

### Why This Talk for WMI Members

WMI's 572+ members are growth-stage CEOs, family business owners, and strategic executives generating $1.86B+ in combined annual revenue. They're not startup founders experimenting — they're operators running real companies with real teams.

The AI conversation they're hearing is dominated by enterprise vendors selling million-dollar platforms and consultants selling workshops with no implementation. What they actually need is a practitioner who can show them:

- What AI automations are delivering measurable ROI right now (not in theory)
- How to evaluate AI opportunities without getting sold technology they don't need
- The 3–5 specific systems that matter most for a company with 10–200 employees
- How to access government funding that makes implementation essentially risk-free

### Talk Outline (45–60 minutes)

**1. The AI Landscape — Honest Assessment (10 min)**
- What's real, what's hype, and what's coming in the next 12 months
- Why most AI implementations fail (hint: they're sold, not built)
- The difference between "AI tools" and "AI systems"

**2. The Three Systems That Matter (20 min)**
- The Capture Engine: Why 78% of customers buy from whoever responds first
- The Fulfillment Bridge: How to eliminate 15+ hours/week of admin
- The Nurture Protocol: The revenue you're leaving on the table from existing clients
- *Live demonstration of each system*

**3. The Atlantic Canada Advantage (10 min)**
- Government funding programs that may offset AI implementation costs
- Why our region is uniquely positioned for AI adoption right now
- The competitive window that's closing

**4. Your Next Step — The "I Hate This" Audit (10 min)**
- A simple framework every member can use tomorrow to identify their #1 automation target
- How to evaluate any AI vendor or solution (the 5-question checklist)

**Format options:** Keynote, workshop, or panel. Happy to adapt to WMI's preferred format, including the "Five Fast Flashes" series if a shorter format is preferred.

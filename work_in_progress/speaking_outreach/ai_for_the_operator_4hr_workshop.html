MODULE 4 · EXECUTIVE PROMPTING

Executive AI Prompting:
The 4-Layer Framework

Stop writing random sentences to AI. Elite operators structure every business prompt using 4 precise operational layers:

Layer 1: Persona & Role

Tell the AI exactly who it is acting as (e.g., "Act as a Senior Operations Specialist with 15 years experience in logistics.")

Layer 2: Business Context

Provide company background, SOPs, target client details, project goals, and relevant background docs.

Layer 3: Rules & Guardrails

Define strict boundaries and negative rules (e.g., "Never hallucinate, avoid buzzwords, keep tone direct and concise.")

Layer 4: Output Structure

Specify the exact output format required (e.g., "Return 3 bullet points followed by an executive email summary.")

GETTING STARTED & EFFICIENCY HACKS

Simple Hacks for
Immediate Daily Efficiency

🎯

1. "Grill Me" Interview Technique

Tell AI: "Don't write anything yet. Interview me one question at a time until you have all the information you need."

📄

2. Handover Documents

Keep a 1-page Company_Context.md doc to paste into new chats—eliminating credit burn and re-explaining context.

🎙️

3. Voice-to-Prompt Dictation

Speak messy thoughts into mobile voice mode, then end with: "Organize my thoughts above into 3 clear action bullets."

🛡️

4. Skeptical Prospect Hack

Tell AI: "Act as a skeptical prospect. Read this proposal draft and give me 3 harsh objections why you wouldn't buy."

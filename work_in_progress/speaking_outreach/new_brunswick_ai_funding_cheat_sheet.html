New Brunswick AI Funding Programs

A 1-Page Cheat Sheet for Growth-Stage Operators
FlowstateAI
Automation Agency
Many growth-stage businesses in New Brunswick are actively adopting AI to capture leads instantly, eliminate manual admin, and scale operations. To offset implementation costs, provincial and federal agencies offer several targeted funding streams. Below is a summary of the primary active programs available to NB operators.
Cybersecure NB & Tech Adoption Program
Up to $3,000
Administered by CBDC Network + Province of New Brunswick
Funding Level Non-repayable grant covering up to $3,000 for technology adoption and secure AI tools.
Eligibility New Brunswick SMEs with a registered office and at least $30,000 in annual revenue.
Best Used For Deploying secure local AI tools, automating operational messaging, and integrating client nurture workflows that reduce administrative overhead.
NBIF AI Fund & Research Support
Project-Based Support
Administered by New Brunswick Innovation Foundation (NBIF) + ACOA
Funding Level Direct investment, matching grants, or applied research support for technology projects.
Eligibility NB-based companies partnering with research institutions, or implementing innovative proprietary AI applications.
Best Used For Integrating advanced AI models into company-specific platforms or testing novel machine learning workflows to gain a regional competitive advantage.
Regional Economic Growth / Productivity (REGI / BSP)
Scalable Funding
Administered by Atlantic Canada Opportunities Agency (ACOA) — NB Offices
Funding Level Direct project funding, scale-up loans, or regional initiative grants.
Eligibility New Brunswick SMEs focused on increasing productivity, scaling operations, or regional business development.
Best Used For Larger-scale digital infrastructure overhauls where custom AI agent systems, automated databases, and multi-tool workflows are built to scale production without expanding headcount.

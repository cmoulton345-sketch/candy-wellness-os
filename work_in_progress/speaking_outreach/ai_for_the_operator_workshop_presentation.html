Operational AI Audit
10 Min

10 Min

  • Calculate your exact dollar cost per month spent on admin friction vs. high-leverage growth activities.
  • Build a concrete, quantifiable ROI business case before investing in any technology.

15 Min

10 Min

  • Structure AI automation projects so government programs absorb adoption risk.

Advanced AI Prompting for Business Leaders
15 Min
  • Move beyond basic chat prompts to structured 4-layer System Instructions.

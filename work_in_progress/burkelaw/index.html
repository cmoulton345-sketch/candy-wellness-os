The Team

Emily A. Cochrane

Associate

Emily was born and raised on the Northside of Fredericton and proudly continues to call it home. She graduated with her Bachelor of Arts (Hons.) from St. Thomas University in 2011, her Law Degree from the University of New Brunswick in 2014, and her Masters in Law from Osgoode Hall Law School in 2022. She received the Lyman Purnell Prize in Wills and Estates in 2014.

Emily was called to the New Brunswick Bar and joined The Burke Law Group in 2015. She maintains a general legal practice with an emphasis on criminal defence. She puts care and attention into each file and is passionate about providing her clients with zealous advocacy, no matter the issue. Emily frequently appears at all levels of court in New Brunswick and has appeared as lead counsel before the New Brunswick Court of Appeal and the Supreme Court of Canada.

Outside of the office, Emily teaches part-time at the University of New Brunswick Faculty of Law and is a board member for the Chalmers Foundation, the New Brunswick Public Libraries Foundation, and Musical Ventures Inc.

Thomas J. Burke, K.C.

Founding Partner

Born in Los Angeles, California, his family relocated to Fredericton, New Brunswick in 1978. After completing High School, T.J. enlisted into the United States Military and served as a Paratrooper and Forward Observer for an Infantry Company with the 82nd Airborne Division based at Fort Bragg, North Carolina. He was honourably discharged in 1995.

A Dalhousie University Law Graduate in 2000 and winner of the Sarah MacWalker MacKenzie Award, he became a member of the New Brunswick Law Society in June 2002. In 2006, T.J. was elected to represent his riding on the Northside of Fredericton and was appointed as Minister of Justice and Attorney General for the Province of New Brunswick, serving in that capacity until 2009.

T.J. practices in criminal law and has a dedicated labour and employment practice. He has worked extensively with First Nation Bands throughout the Province and has successfully resolved outstanding land claim issues against the Government of Canada. He has served as lead counsel in several matters at the New Brunswick Court of Appeal and the Supreme Court of Canada.

Ronald E. Morris, K.C.

Of Counsel

Born and raised on the mighty Miramichi, Ron pursued post-secondary education at St. Francis Xavier University, then furthered his Bachelor of Arts at St. Thomas University. After earning the notorious nickname "the Cat" while kicking rubber between the pipes for the Tommies, Ron graduated and was accepted to Law School at the University of New Brunswick.

Upon graduation, Ron was called to the New Brunswick Bar Association in 1980. With a passion for the law and a desire to defend those innocent until proven guilty, Ron's reputation to defend those criminally charged surpasses all others. With a lengthy legal career in Criminal Law, Ron initially formed a 23-year partnership, Roach and Morris based in Oromocto, NB. He is counsel to The Burke Law Group since 2016.

Ron received the King's Counsel (K.C.) designation in 2006 for his long-standing service, commitment and excellence in the practice of law.

Aleacia Bennett-LeBlanc

Associate

Aleacia graduated with a Bachelor of Arts from the University College of Cape Breton in 1998 and from the University of New Brunswick with an LL.B. in 2002. She was called to the New Brunswick bar in 2004 and has actively been in practice since then. She joined the Burke Law Group in 2020, bringing a deep wealth of experience through her family law practice.

She has appeared in Provincial Court, the Court of Queen's Bench, in various jurisdictions, as well as the New Brunswick Court of Appeal. Aleacia is comfortable with the adversarial nature of court but also works to resolve files in an amicable manner when possible and understands that family law must focus on the family first.

Aleacia is an active community member. She currently chairs the board of the Fredericton Family Resource Center and has previously chaired Laubach Literacy Fredericton. She also volunteers with Habitat for Humanity and CHIMO.

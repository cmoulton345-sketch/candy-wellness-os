# Vibroacoustic Wearable - Concept Visual v3 (Ergonomic Neck Collar)

Here is the updated design sheet for the wearable vest featuring the **high ergonomic cervical neck collar** (replacing the skull cap). 

Key design features in v3:
- **Unobstructed Head & Ear Profile:** Leaves the head completely free for any over-ear or in-ear headphones.
- **Suboccipital/C1-C7 Collar:** High contoured collar cradles the back of the neck to stimulate vagal and suboccipital mechanoreceptors.
- **Flat Supine Lying Surface:** Completely flush back panel for comfortable lying down.
- **Extended Sacrum Tail & Velcro Side Straps:** Full posterior spinal coverage down to the sacral node.

### Executive Summary

Burke Law Group has a proud reputation of high-standard legal representation in New Brunswick. To keep pace with modern litigation and administrative demands while maintaining absolute security under attorney-client privilege, this proposal outlines a three-tier phased modernization plan. 

Our approach centers on a **pay-as-you-grow implementation roadmap**, ensuring that each phase is fully delivered and cash-flow positive before moving to the next. All components are designed to run inside private, isolated sandboxes with zero public data leakage.

---

### Tier 1: Core Practice Automation (The Practice Engine)
**Focus:** Eliminating administrative bottlenecks and manual repeat workflows.

Instead of rigid off-the-shelf software, we work directly with your partners and staff to map out, design, and deploy **10 custom core practice automations** tailored specifically to the firm's bottlenecks.

*   **Custom Scope Includes (Up to 10 Automations):**
    *   **24/7 Lead Capture & Instant Response:** SMS/Email auto-responders to incoming web and Google inquiries to secure client relationships immediately.
    *   **Calendar Automation:** Seamless intake scheduling hooked directly to firm and attorney calendars.
    *   **Automated Milestone Notifications:** Triggers automatic email/SMS progress updates to clients when court dates or file stages change, reducing "just checking in" phone calls.
    *   **Accounts Receivable Loops:** Friendly, automated follow-ups for outstanding invoices.
    *   **Post-Matter Feedback Loop:** Automatically solicits Google Reviews upon closing client files.
*   **Investment:**
    *   **One-Time Setup Fee:** $3,000
    *   **Monthly Retainer:** $1,800/month flat fee.
*   **ROI Metrics:** Reclaims 15-20 hours of administrative and support staff labor per week.

### Tier 2: Socrates Legal Agent (Cognitive Co-Counsel)
**Focus:** Secure, high-speed legal research, precedent ingestion, and draft generation.

Socrates is a custom AI model instance trained to serve as an untiring junior associate for each attorney.

*   **Core Deliverables:**
    *   **Canadian Jurisdictional Training:** Native grounding in Supreme Court of Canada, Federal, and New Brunswick case law.
    *   **Style Customization:** Ingests previous motions, pleadings, and briefs to match the specific tone and vocabulary of the Burke Law Group.
    *   **Discovery & Transcript Synthesizer:** Reads 500+ page files in seconds to isolate contradictions, key timelines, and evidence.
    *   **Pleading & Brief Drafting:** Generates highly specific document drafts based on active matter details.
    *   **Active User Sandbox Licensing:** Each licensed attorney/user receives a dedicated secure login, password, and isolated private sandbox environment to ensure zero cross-contamination.
*   **Investment:**
    *   **One-Time Setup & Model Training:** $7,500 (Covers precedent data ingestion, jurisdictional grounding, and security integration).
    *   **Monthly Fee:** $1,500/month per active user seat (dedicated login/password).
*   **ROI Metrics:** Cuts legal research and initial draft writing time by up to 80%.

### Tier 3: Full AI OS Infrastructure (The Digital Headquarters)
**Focus:** Zero Trust private cloud and dual-OS environment for firm management.

This tier is designed to serve as the secure nervous system of your firm, scoped specifically for key senior members, partners, and the office manager to maintain executive oversight and data control.

*   **Dual-OS Architecture:**
    We deploy two completely isolated OS environments with different personas, rules, and axioms:
    1.  **OS Instance 1 (Primary Firm OS):** Configured for partners and senior counsel. Heavy focus on advanced legal analysis, precedent search, firm-wide strategic planning, and privileged decision support.
    2.  **OS Instance 2 (Operations & Admin OS):** Configured for the office manager and operational support. Runs background billing reconciliations, lead capture management, database syncing, and task delegation.
*   **Core Deliverables:**

    *   **Practice Software Integration:** Direct API hooks into Clio, Outlook/Gmail, QuickBooks, and messaging.

### Security Statement
All models run inside secure, private hosting environments. Client data, precedents, and search queries are completely sandboxed and are **never** shared with public AI models or used for external model training. NDAs are in force from day one.

This document outlines the pricing strategy and structure for the presentation to the Burke Law Group. It covers the three tiers of offerings, provides recommendations on packaging, and includes the business case math to justify the numbers to the partners.

## Strategic Analysis: Is $1,500/mo Per Individual "Way Off"?

Your proposal of **$3,000 setup** and **$1,500/month service fee per individual** is **not way off in terms of value**, but it may face friction in **pricing structure** for Tier 1.

### The Value Argument (Why it works)
* **Billable Hour Math:** A senior attorney bills at $350–$500+/hour. Saving just 3 to 4 hours of their time per month covers the $1,500 cost.
* **Lead Capture Math:** If the instant lead responder captures just **one** family law or commercial litigation file per month that would have gone to a competitor, it pays for the entire year of service.

### The Friction Risk (Why they might balk)
* **Shared Infrastructure:** Tier 1 features (speed-to-lead web forms, invoice reminders, milestone triggers) are centralized firm systems. If Burke Law has 5 lawyers, charging $7,500/mo ($90k/yr) for n8n workflows will feel expensive when compared to hiring a full-time in-house admin assistant ($40k–$50k/yr). 
* **The "Per-User" Dilemma:** They will ask: *"Why are we paying $1,500/mo for TJ and another $1,500/mo for Taylor when they are using the exact same intake form and database?"*

### Recommendation for Tier 1 Packaging
Use a **Hybrid Base + Seat model** OR a **Flat Firm Rate with caps**.
1. **Option A (Hybrid):** $1,500/mo base fee for the firm (includes up to 3 lawyers) + $250/mo per additional lawyer.
2. **Option B (Flat Firm Rate):** $2,000/mo flat fee for the entire firm (up to 5–6 users). This positions it as a "digital administrative assistant" that never sleeps, at half the cost of a human hire.

## Detailed Tier Breakdowns & Pricing Justifications

### Tier 1: Core Automation (The Practice Engine)
* **What they get:**
  * Speed-to-Lead: Instant SMS/Email response to web and Google inquiries (24/7).
  * Automated intake booking calendar integration.
  * Automated case milestone emails (notifying clients when files move stages, stopping "just checking in" calls).
  * Invoice payment reminders (A/R automation).
  * Google review generation loop on matter closing.
* **Pricing Proposal:**
  * **Setup:** $3,000 (Flat project fee)
  * **Monthly Service:** $1,800/month flat fee for the firm (up to 6 lawyers) OR $1,000/month base + $200/month per user.
* **The Pitch:** *"For less than half the cost of a part-time receptionist, your firm gets 24/7 responsiveness, faster invoice collections, and zero missed leads."*

### Tier 2: Specialized Legal Agent (Socrates)
* **What they get:**
  * Socrates: An AI co-counsel running inside a secure container.
  * Trained specifically on New Brunswick/Canadian jurisdiction rules.
  * Trained on the firm's writing style, templates, past successful motions, and briefs.
  * Functions: Synthesizes discovery files, drafts pleadings, summarizes long deposition transcripts, generates legal memos.
* **Pricing Proposal:**
  * **Setup:** $7,500 (Covers data indexing, security setup, and custom model training/fine-tuning).
  * **Monthly Service:** **$1,500/month per lawyer (individual seat pricing).**
* **Why Seat Pricing works here:** Socrates is a direct multiplier of an individual lawyer's output. A lawyer using Socrates can do the work of 2 associates. Charging $1,500/mo per seat is highly defensible because the AI acts as a dedicated personal assistant to that specific lawyer.
* **The Pitch:** *"Socrates doesn't replace you; it replaces 80% of the research and draft writing time of a junior associate. For $1,500/month, you have an associate working 24/7 who has memorized every case you've ever won."*

### Tier 3: Full AI OS System Install (The Digital Headquarters)
* **What they get:**
  * The full client-workspace architecture deployed securely on a private cloud (Cloudflare Zero Trust).
  * Multiple connected personas (Socrates for legal research, Ax for business processes, Earl/custom coach for firm goals).
  * Secure client portal where clients can upload documents, check case progress via chat, and receive secure notifications.
  * Centralized knowledge base indexing all past firm knowledge.
  * Direct integration with firm accounting and practice management software (Clio, etc.).
* **Pricing Proposal:**
  * **Setup:** $25,000 (Flat design, architecture, and deployment fee).
  * **Monthly Service:** $5,000/month flat retainer (includes ongoing development, customization, and support for up to 10 users).
* **The Pitch:** *"This is the ultimate firm infrastructure. We build a private digital nervous system for Burke Law Group. It ensures absolute security, integrates all your tools, and gives you a proprietary AI asset that makes the firm 3x more efficient."*

## ROI Script & Value Presentation Strategy

When the partners ask for pricing tomorrow, present it from **highest tier to lowest tier (anchoring)** and lead with the math.

### The Billable Hour ROI Script:
> *"If we look at Tier 1, the monthly service fee is $1,800 for the firm. If your average billing rate is $350/hour, that means the system only needs to save your team a combined total of **5 hours per month** to break even. Between eliminating manual document follow-ups, automated invoice reminders, and instantly capturing new inquiries, this system will easily reclaim 20 to 30 hours of staff time every single month. It is a cash-positive asset from day one."*

### Key Selling Points for Law Firms:
1. **Security & Confidentiality:** Data stays isolated. In Tier 2 and 3, search data is private and is not used to train public LLM models (unlike public ChatGPT).
2. **Speed to Response:** Capturing leads immediately. Lawyers sleep; the intake engine doesn't.
3. **No Overhead:** No employee benefits, no vacation pay, no office space required.

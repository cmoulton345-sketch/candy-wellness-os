Beyond Sales & Leads

AI automation touches every corner of your operation.

Most people associate AI automation with lead follow-up and sales sequences. That's just the beginning. Here's where dealerships are finding automation opportunities across their entire operation:

🔧

Service Department

Your highest-margin revenue centre — automated
  • High ROI

    Online Appointment Booking (24/7)

    Customers book their own service appointments online, any time. Automated confirmations and reminders are sent immediately — no back-and-forth with your service desk.

  • High ROI

    No-Show Prevention Reminders

    A sequence of automated reminders (48 hours, 24 hours, 2 hours before) reduces no-shows by up to 40%. Each no-show costs you a service bay that day — this pays for itself immediately.

  • Medium ROI

    Service Due Reminders

    Based on purchase date or last service record, AI automatically sends personalized service reminders to your customer list — keeping your service bays consistently booked.

  • Medium ROI

    Parts Pre-Ordering for Booked Jobs

    When a service appointment is booked, AI can trigger a notification or order for required parts — so your technicians aren't waiting and your bays stay productive.

  • Efficiency

    Recall Outreach

    When a recall is issued for a unit you've sold, AI automatically identifies affected customers and sends personalized outreach — keeping you compliant and your customers loyal.

📣

Marketing & Brand

Consistent, professional marketing without the overhead
  • High ROI

    New Inventory Social Posts

    When a new bike or ATV hits your inventory system, AI automatically generates a post with description, specs, and pricing — and publishes to your Facebook and Instagram. Every unit gets exposure, automatically.

  • High ROI

    Google Review Generation

    After every sale or service completion, AI sends a perfectly timed review request. Top dealerships are 3–5x more likely to generate Google reviews because automated timing catches customers at peak satisfaction — not a week later when they've forgotten.

  • Medium ROI

    Seasonal Campaign Automation

    ATV season, snowmobile season, spring ride season — AI automatically sends targeted campaigns to your customer segments as each season approaches, without your team having to remember or manually execute.

  • Medium ROI

    AI-Generated Unit Descriptions

    When listing inventory online, AI drafts compelling, SEO-friendly descriptions for each unit based on make, model, spec, and features. Hours of copy time reduced to seconds.

  • Brand

    Customer Birthday & Milestone Outreach

    Automated personal touchpoints on birthdays and purchase anniversaries keep your dealership top of mind between sales and service visits — building loyalty without any manual effort.

⚙️

Operations & Administration

The back-office work that runs on autopilot
  • High ROI

    Automated Reporting

    AI compiles and sends weekly or daily reports on sales activity, service revenue, lead volume, and pipeline status — delivered to you automatically every Monday morning without anyone pulling the data manually.

  • Efficiency

    Invoice & Payment Follow-Up

    Automated reminders for outstanding invoices and deposits — reducing the time your team spends chasing payments and improving your cash flow predictability.

  • Efficiency

    Staff Scheduling & Shift Reminders

    Automated shift confirmations and schedule updates sent to your team — reducing the back-and-forth of manual scheduling and last-minute confusion.

  • Efficiency

    New Employee Onboarding Sequences

    When a new hire starts, AI triggers a structured sequence of onboarding tasks, training links, policy reminders, and check-ins — ensuring nothing falls through the cracks without a manager micromanaging every step.

  • Efficiency

    Vendor & Supplier Communication

    Automated purchase order confirmations, delivery tracking notifications, and supplier follow-ups — keeping your parts and inventory pipeline flowing without manual oversight.

🤝

Customer Experience

Every customer gets a premium experience — at scale
  • High ROI

    24/7 AI Chat Assistant

    An AI chatbot on your website answers common questions instantly — hours, inventory, pricing, availability, financing info — even at midnight on a Sunday. No lead left unanswered.

  • High ROI

    Post-Purchase Follow-Up & Accessories Upsell

    After a sale, AI triggers a sequence that checks in on the customer, asks about their experience, and introduces accessories, riding gear, and extended warranty options — all personalized to what they bought.

  • Retention

    Warranty & Extended Coverage Reminders

    Automated outreach when a customer's warranty is approaching expiry — giving you a natural, timely reason to reconnect and start a service or upgrade conversation.

  • Retention

    Lapsed Customer Re-Engagement

    Customers who haven't visited in 12+ months automatically receive a personalized re-engagement campaign — a special offer, new inventory alert, or seasonal message that brings them back in without your team lifting a finger.

💰

Financing & Sales Pipeline

Keep every deal moving forward automatically
  • High ROI

    Financing Application Follow-Up

    When a customer submits a financing inquiry, AI triggers a sequence that keeps them engaged through the approval process — reducing drop-off during the most critical part of the sale.

  • Efficiency

    Trade-In Inquiry Nurture

    Every trade-in inquiry enters an automated sequence that stays in touch, provides relevant information, and moves the conversation toward a visit — without a salesperson having to manually follow up every few days.

  • Pipeline

    Deal Stage Notifications

    Your sales team receives automated alerts when deals move through stages, when follow-ups are due, or when a prospect hasn't been contacted in too long — so nothing stalls silently in the pipeline.

The 5 automations we'd build first for a powersports dealership.

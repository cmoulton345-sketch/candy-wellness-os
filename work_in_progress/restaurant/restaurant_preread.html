The Full Opportunity

AI automation works across every part of your restaurant.

📅

Reservations & No-Show Prevention

Fill every table, every service
  • High ROI

    Online Reservation System (24/7)

    Guests book their own tables online any time — evenings, weekends, holidays — without calling. Eliminates missed reservations and reduces the phone traffic your front-of-house manages during service.

  • High ROI

    No-Show Prevention Reminders

    A 24-hour and 2-hour automated confirmation request reduces restaurant no-shows significantly. Every recovered no-show is a full table's revenue saved — a material impact on an already thin-margin service.

  • High ROI

    Waitlist Automation

    When a reservation cancels, AI automatically contacts your waitlist and fills the table — turning a cancellation into recovered revenue without anyone manually making calls.

  • Medium ROI

    Special Event & Large Party Follow-Up

    When a large group or event inquiry comes in, AI sends a follow-up sequence — menu options, deposit information, and confirmation reminders — handling the coordination your team would otherwise do manually.

🔄

Customer Loyalty & Retention

Turn first-time diners into loyal regulars
  • High ROI

    Post-Visit Follow-Up & Return Visit Offer

    After a dining visit, guests receive an automated thank-you with an incentive for their next visit — a birthday offer, a weekday special, or a loyalty reward. This single automation significantly increases return visit frequency.

  • High ROI

    Lapsed Guest Win-Back Campaign

    Guests who haven't visited in 60–90 days receive a personalized re-engagement message — a seasonal menu teaser, a special offer, or a simple "we'd love to see you again." Brings back regulars who drifted without any staff outreach.

  • Medium ROI

    Birthday & Anniversary Recognition

    Automatically send birthday and dining anniversary messages with a special offer — generating guaranteed visits on the dates when guests are most likely to celebrate at restaurants.

  • Loyalty

    VIP & Frequent Diner Recognition

    High-frequency guests are identified and receive personalized recognition — a priority reservation, a complimentary item notification, or an exclusive chef's table offer. Builds the kind of loyalty that survives a new competitor opening nearby.

📣

Marketing & Promotions

Consistent marketing without the overhead
  • High ROI

    Weekly Specials & Menu Updates Broadcast

    Your daily specials, weekend features, and seasonal menu changes automatically sent to your subscriber list — driving reservations and walk-in traffic without anyone writing or sending the message manually.

  • Medium ROI

    Event Promotion Automation

    Live music nights, wine dinners, brunch specials, and seasonal events automatically promoted to your list in advance — maximizing covers without adding marketing overhead.

  • Medium ROI

    Social Media Content Automation

    Daily food photos, weekly specials, and event announcements automatically scheduled for Facebook and Instagram — keeping your social presence active without a dedicated person managing it.

  • Brand

    Seasonal Campaign Sequences

    Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, holiday dinner season — AI sends targeted campaigns to your guest list well in advance, driving reservation volume for your highest-revenue services of the year.

⭐

Reputation & Reviews

Your Google rating is your most powerful table-filling tool
  • High ROI

    Post-Visit Review Request

    After each dining visit, AI sends a review request when guest satisfaction is highest. Restaurants with 100+ reviews at 4.5+ stars consistently outperform competitors in Google search — filling more tables from organic discovery than any paid ad.

  • Brand

    Guest Feedback Collection

    A private feedback option after each visit catches any concerns before they become public reviews — giving you the chance to recover a dissatisfied guest and improve operations.

  • Brand

    Referral Incentive Automation

    Happy guests receive a simple referral offer — bring a friend and earn a dining credit. Word-of-mouth, systematized into a consistent driver of new covers.

⚙️

Operations & Staff

Run a tighter kitchen and front-of-house without the chaos
  • Efficiency

    Staff Scheduling & Shift Reminders

    Automated schedule confirmations and shift reminders for kitchen and front-of-house staff — reducing the last-minute calls and scrambles that disrupt service.

  • Efficiency

    Supplier & Inventory Alerts

    When key ingredients or supplies hit low levels, AI triggers an automated reorder alert — preventing the midnight scramble of running out of menu items mid-service.

  • Efficiency

    Daily Revenue & Cover Reports

    Automated daily reporting on covers served, revenue per service, reservation volume, and no-show rates — delivered to ownership each morning without anyone pulling data manually.

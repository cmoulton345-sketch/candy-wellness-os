# INTERIM EMERGENCY EVACUATION HEADCOUNT PROCEDURE
**Company-Provided Housing (Swipe-In / Swipe-Out Facilities)**

> [!WARNING]
> **INTERIM PROCEDURE — EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY**  
> This procedure is an **Interim Safety Directive** issued to establish positive accountability for all personnel residing in company-provided housing during an emergency evacuation. This procedure remains in full effect until further notice or until superseded by a permanent automated/site-wide headcount system.

---

## 1. PURPOSE
The purpose of this procedure is to establish an immediate, reliable, and standardized process for accounting for all residents and workers evacuated from company-provided accommodation during a fire, gas release, structural emergency, or site evacuation.

## 2. SCOPE
This procedure applies to:
* All employees, contractors, and visitors residing in or utilizing company-provided housing where electronic swipe-in / swipe-out access is utilized.
* All group supervisors, foremen, and crew leads overseeing personnel residing in housing.
* Designated **Muster Coordinators** assigned to each work group or residential pod.
* On-site Security and Emergency Response Teams (Fire Department / Incident Command).

---

## 3. REGULATORY & STANDARDS BASIS
* **WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation Part 4:** *General Conditions — Emergency Preparedness and Response* (Sections 4.13 to 4.18: Emergency evacuation, verification of worker accountability, and training).
* **CSA Z1000:** *Occupational Health and Safety Management* (Emergency preparedness and response).
* **CSA Z246.1:** *Security Management for Petroleum and Natural Gas Industry Systems*.

---

## 4. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

### 4.1 On-Site Security
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **SECURITY ROLE CLARIFICATION:**  
> Security personnel are stationed at housing evacuation routes and muster areas **exclusively to provide physical direction, crowd control, and traffic management.**  
> **Security does NOT conduct, compile, or verify the personnel headcount.** That responsibility rests entirely with the individual work groups through their assigned Muster Coordinators.

### 4.2 Supervisors / Crew Leads
* **Roster & Group Chat maintenance:** Ensure a dedicated mobile text messaging group chat (e.g., SMS, WhatsApp, or approved company messaging platform) is established for every group residing in housing.
* **Continuous Accuracy:** Verify and update names, room assignments, and active phone numbers in the group chat immediately whenever personnel rotate on/off shift or change accommodations.
* **Muster Coordinator Assignment:** Ensure a **Muster Coordinator** (and one backup) is formally assigned at the beginning of each shift cycle or weekly rotation.

### 4.3 Muster Coordinator
* Holds the accountability role for **one (1) week at a time**, or until relieved, rotated off-shift, or unable to perform duties.
* Initiates the group text check-in immediately upon reaching the designated safe muster point during an alarm or evacuation.
* Compiles the headcount and reports missing or unaccounted individuals directly to the **Fire Department / Incident Commander / Security Lead** within the designated time bracket (**maximum 10 minutes** from alarm).
* Enforces muster discipline (preventing anyone from leaving the muster area or re-entering the facility).

### 4.4 All Housing Residents / Group Members
* **Swipe Discipline:** Must personally swipe in and swipe out at all times when entering or exiting housing. Tailgating is strictly prohibited.
* **Evacuate Immediately:** Evacuate immediately upon alarm. **Never delay evacuation to send text messages or gather belongings.**
* **Mandatory Check-In:** Immediately check in via the group text chat once safely arrived at the designated physical muster point, or report verbally to the Muster Coordinator if phone/battery is unavailable.
* **Remain Mustered:** Stay at the designated muster location until officially released or redirected by Emergency Services.

---

## 5. PROCEDURE

### Step 1: Pre-Incident Setup & Weekly Rotation
1. **Group Chat Creation:** Each operational or residential group creates a dedicated text chat group containing every active resident in that group.
2. **Weekly Coordinator Designation:** At the start of each work week or shift cycle, the Supervisor designates the **Muster Coordinator** and a **Backup Coordinator**.
3. **Roster Verification:** The Supervisor confirms all active group members have confirmed receipt of a test message in the group chat upon arrival at housing.

### Step 2: Emergency Evacuation Execution
1. **Alarm Activation:** Upon sounding of the building fire alarm, gas alarm, or evacuation order, **all personnel must immediately evacuate** via the nearest safe emergency exit.
2. **No Delay:** Personnel shall proceed directly to the designated physical **Muster Point**. Do not stop to text, collect personal items, or search for co-workers inside the building.

### Step 3: Headcount & Group Chat Check-In
1. **Initiate Roll Call:** Once safely positioned at the Muster Point, the **Muster Coordinator** sends the standard emergency check-in prompt to the group chat:
   > *"EMERGENCY EVACUATION IN PROGRESS. Reply 'SAFE' and your current physical location immediately."*
2. **Member Confirmation:** Every group member must reply immediately confirming their safety and presence at the muster point.
3. **Verbal / Dead Battery Contingency:** If a member's mobile device is dead, lost, or lacking signal, they must physically report to the Muster Coordinator at the muster point so they can be manually checked off.

### Step 4: Escalation & Reporting Unaccounted Personnel
1. **Time Window (`T + 10 Minutes`):** If any individual who swiped into housing has not checked in via text or in person within **ten (10) minutes** of the alarm sounding, they are formally declared **UNACCOUNTED FOR**.
2. **Immediate Emergency Notification:** The Muster Coordinator must immediately approach the **Fire Department Incident Commander** or **Security Lead** at the command post/muster point and report:
   * Full Name of missing person(s).
   * Room / Unit Number.
   * Last known location or last time seen.
   * Mobile phone number.

### Step 5: Muster Discipline & All-Clear / Relocation
1. **Hold Position:** All group members must remain clustered together at the muster area under the oversight of their Muster Coordinator.
2. **Worsening Conditions:** If smoke, wind, or hazard plumes threaten the primary muster area, Emergency Services or Security will issue orders to relocate to a Secondary Muster Point. The Muster Coordinator must maintain group cohesion during relocation and re-verify headcount upon arrival.
3. **Return to Housing:** No personnel may leave the muster area or return to their rooms until the **Incident Commander / Fire Department** issues a formal, verbal **"ALL-CLEAR."**

## 6. CRITICAL LIFE SAFETY WARNINGS

> [!CAUTION]
> **ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION ON RE-ENTRY & SEARCHES**  
> Under **NO CIRCUMSTANCES** shall any Muster Coordinator, Supervisor, or group member re-enter an evacuated building to search for unaccounted individuals or retrieve belongings.  
> **Search and Rescue operations inside hazardous structures are strictly reserved for trained, fully equipped Fire Department / Emergency Response personnel wearing Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA).** Re-entering an evacuated structure turns a rescue into a double fatality.

> [!WARNING]
> **SWIPE-IN ACCURACY IS A LIFE OR DEATH CONTROL**  
> Electronic swipe logs are pulled by Emergency Services during a major incident to determine who is inside the structure.  
> * If you swipe in and leave without swiping out, **firefighters will risk their lives searching burning or toxic rooms for you.**  
> * If you enter without swiping in and collapse inside, **rescuers will not know to look for you.**  
> Strict swipe compliance is mandatory under this interim procedure.

> [!TIP]
> **CELLULAR CONNECTIVITY & BATTERY MITIGATION**  
> Because this interim procedure relies on mobile devices:
> * Keep phones charged while in residence.
> * If cellular data networks become congested during a major site incident, **standard SMS (text message)** often transmits successfully when mobile internet/apps fail. If data fails, fall back to direct SMS or physical verbal check-in at the muster sign.

---

## 7. AUDIT & REVIEW
As an interim procedure, this document shall be reviewed by EH&S and Site Management **every thirty (30) days** to evaluate text group chat reliability, Muster Coordinator effectiveness during drills, and readiness for transition to a permanent automated tracking system.

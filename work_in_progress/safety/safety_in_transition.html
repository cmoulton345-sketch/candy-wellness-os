We are crossing a threshold. The hazards on this site change every single day. A system that was safe to work on yesterday may be energized, pressurized, or live with gas tomorrow.

The Official Definition
What Is Safety in Transition?

"The methodical transition of systems, equipment, and personnel from one phase of facility development to the next — ensuring that hazard controls, competencies, procedures, and accountability structures keep pace with the evolving risk profile of the facility at every stage."

⚠️   The rules of the game are changing. If we don't change with them — people get hurt.
Pillar 01 of 05
🗣️
Communication
"Before that system goes live — does everyone know?"

In transition, one crew hands systems to another. Construction to precomm. Precomm to commissioning. Commissioning to operations. Every handover is a moment where information can fall through the cracks — and in this industry, a gap in communication can cost someone their life.

If you're not sure — ask. If the answer isn't clear — stop work.
Pillar 02 of 05
🤝
Cooperation
"Construction, Precomm, Commissioning, Ops — one team."

Multiple disciplines. Multiple contractors. Multiple crews. All working in the same space, on overlapping scopes, at the same time. Cooperation means we look out for each other across the boundaries. If you see something wrong — even in someone else's area — you say something.

One team. One site. One outcome.
Pillar 03 of 05
👁️
Vigilance
"The hazard landscape changes every day. So must your awareness."

Electrical systems are being energized. Pressurized systems are being tested. Hydrocarbons will enter this facility. New exclusion zones. New energy isolations. New permit requirements. You cannot walk onto this site and assume yesterday's conditions still apply.

Never assume. Always verify.
Pillar 04 of 05
📋
Discipline
"The pressure will be on. The procedure still applies."

As we move toward first gas, the schedule pressure builds. Milestones are tracked. Every day of delay is visible. The push to move fast will be real. Your discipline is your armour against that pressure. Follow the permit process. Complete the hazard assessment. Verify the isolation — every time.

Shortcuts in transition are how serious incidents happen.
Pillar 05 of 05
🏆
Achievement
"You helped build one of the most significant energy facilities in Canada."

Look at this site. Look at what has been built. Every weld, every bolt, every cable pull — that was someone in this room. You built this. Getting from construction to first gas safely is one of the hardest things to do in the energy industry. Very few workforces in the world get to say they did it.

That achievement is within reach. Let's finish this the right way.

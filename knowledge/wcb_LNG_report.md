## Transcription:

**Project overview LNG**

This project is a small pipeline project with a new pipeline running between a Port Coquitlam compressor station and LNG which is 7 Km from the municipal centre of Squamish. At LNG a new LNG export terminal is being constructed. The work in Squamish will involving a soft ground tunnel boring machine which will install concrete segments, being launched from BC Rails property on Government Road. The second tunnel will be started from the LNG site through a rock formation (Monmouth Mountain) and will involve a hard-rock boring machine which will meet the segmented machine roughly halfway between the two points. The distance is approximately 9 km between the LNG site and the BC Rail yard.

The overall workplace at LNG has been divided into separated sectors to accommodate separate general contractor work zones. The overall prime for the project is LNG Management Ltd.

All materials being brought to the location must come by way of barges or ships and all materials must pass through the main construction area of this project. The company reports the work zone as 48 acres which is the area WorkSafeBC determined as requiring a single prime contractor to coordinate health and safety.

**Prime contractor**

I, in conjunction with two other officers determined the owner LNG Limited Partnership (which is registered in BC as an Extra Provincial Partnership) operating as LNG Limited and now LNG Management Ltd. (LNG) to be the prime contractor for the purposes of managing health and safety at this workplace.

LNG Management Ltd. received an order on December 19, 2023, under Section 24. (1)(b) of the Workers' Compensation Act requiring a compliance date of January 5, 2024.

This order required LNG to: Please detail how changes will be made to ensure proper reporting and management of the contractors under your direction. Your systems and processes must ensure compliance. A follow up to this un-complied order is detailed in inspection report 202316537243B.

I have now received a further letter dated January 21, 2024 from Ms. Kenny on behalf of LNG where it continued to argue with my finding that it was the prime contractor, rather than providing the overdue compliance actions. I understand that the process is complicated and that additional time is required.

Section 24 of the Occupational health and safety regulations requires: "Every person to whom an order or directive is issued by the Board must comply promptly or by the time set out in the order or directive"
See regulation reference.

Section 25 of the Workers' Compensation Act states "Every owner of a workplace must comply with the OHS provisions, the regulations and any applicable orders".
See regulation reference.

The letter I sent dated January 9, 2024 has sufficient detail with regard to the assessment of the work site as a a multi employer workplace requiring a single prime contractor.

I then followed up with a request for contract documentation from the four main parties, LNG Management Ltd, Frontier Kemper Michels, Fortis Inc, and CB&I Canada ltd. This information has now been received.

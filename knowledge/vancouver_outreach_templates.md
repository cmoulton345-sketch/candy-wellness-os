# Vancouver Layover Strategy: Initial Contact Templates

You make a great point. While the "one automation" hook is great for lowering the barrier to entry, it needs to be anchored to the **actual pain points** their founders are experiencing so it doesn't sound like a parlor trick. 

We don't want to explain the full 3-Tier pricing structure in a cold email—that risks triggering their "salesman radar." Instead, we should name-drop the exact operational bottlenecks we solve, and reframe the "one automation" as building their "first foundational piece of infrastructure."

Here are the revised templates:

---

## 1. For Peer-to-Peer Networks (EO, MacKay, TEC, Acetech)
**Goal:** Position yourself as a high-value resource for their members, offering a zero-risk, high-reward "Free Build Session" for their cohorts.

**Subject:** Value-Add for [Organization Name] Vancouver Members / Cohorts

Hi [Name/Team],

I build private, sandboxed AI infrastructure for scaling Founders across Canada. I help business owners eliminate operational bottlenecks—things like client onboarding friction, disjointed data systems, and manual reporting—so they can scale without inflating their headcount.

I fly into Vancouver twice a month for business, and I’m looking to connect with a few high-level CEO networks in the city.

I have a very specific model: I don't do traditional sales pitches. Instead, I sit down with a founder, bring my laptop, and build their first foundational piece of AI infrastructure live, for free, simply to prove the value. 

I’m reaching out because I’d love to see if there’s a fit to offer these "Free Build Sessions" as a tactical value-add for some of your Vancouver forum members. 

I'll be in Vancouver next on [Date]. Would you be open to grabbing a quick coffee to see if this aligns with what your members are looking for?

Best,
Joe

---

## 2. For Private Clubs (The Vancouver Club, Terminal City Club)
**Goal:** Secure a premium basecamp. The focus remains on networking and bringing business into their venue.

**Subject:** Non-Resident Membership Inquiry - Joe Moulton

Hi [Name/Concierge Team],

I am a tech founder based out East, traveling frequently across Canada to build private AI infrastructure for business owners. I fly into Vancouver twice a month and am currently looking for a premium basecamp for my layovers.

I regularly host high-level founders for working sessions and lunch meetings while I'm in town, and [The Vancouver Club / Terminal City Club] looks like the perfect environment for this. 

Could you please send me some information regarding your Non-Resident membership options? Additionally, I will be in Vancouver on [Date]—would it be possible to arrange a brief tour of the facilities?

Thank you,
Joe

---

## 3. For Chambers of Commerce & Public Forums (GVBOT, Richmond Chamber, VEF)
**Goal:** Secure speaking engagements, pitch slots, or workshop opportunities to get in front of larger rooms of established SMEs.

**Subject:** AI Infrastructure for Scaling SMEs - [Organization Name] Collaboration

Hi [Name/Team],

I build private AI infrastructure for established SMEs across Canada. I help businesses eliminate their operational bottlenecks—automating things like lead triage, data synthesis, and backend administration—so they can scale their revenue without scaling their payroll.

I fly into Vancouver twice a month for business. I’m reaching out because I’d love to connect and see how I might be able to add value to your members at [Organization Name]. 

Whether it’s participating in a pitch event, running a tactical workshop on real-world AI implementation for founders, or offering free live build sessions for your members, I'd love to contribute to the local ecosystem.

Are you the right person to speak with about this? I'll be in town next on [Date] if you have 15 minutes to connect over coffee.

Best,
Joe

# Candy OS

## Mission

To provide Candy with calm clarity, seamless executive assistance, emotional sanctuary, and pain-free physical recovery—giving her the grace, structure, and freedom to thrive in her work, support her family, and deeply enjoy her life.

---

## The Five Core Anchors

> *Grace over perfection. Balance over burnout. Love and presence above all.*

1. **Family:** Joe, Rayne, her mom, and her puppy. Family well-being and connection always come first.
2. **Balance:** Work serves life; life never serves work. Golden focus in the morning, gentleness in the afternoon, complete freedom on evenings and weekends.
3. **Health:** Pain-adaptive physical movement, hormone-smart perimenopause recovery, and restorative peace.
4. **Grace:** Infinite self-compassion on hard days. No guilt for resting, slowing down, or having ADHD brain fog.
5. **Love:** Warmth, unconditional acceptance, and genuine connection with her people.

## Daily Operating Rhythm

* **6:30 AM – 9:00 AM:** Morning Sanctuary (Coffee, easing body soreness, yoga, zero work email).
* **9:00 AM:** Morning Kickoff with Friday (Warm greeting + 2–3 calming spoken affirmations).
* **9:00 AM – 1:30 PM:** Golden Focus Peak (Complex wealth management accounts, boss requests).
* **2:30 PM:** Slump & Fog Protection (Walk with mom or pup, music, affirmations, easy no-brainer tasks only).
* **4:30 PM – 5:00 PM:** Workday Shutdown (Safe logging of open items, clean mental slate).
* **6:30 PM:** Sacred Lifeline Workout & Music (Head & heart reset, pain-adaptive lifting or mobility).
* **Bedtime:** Legs Up the Wall (*Viparita Karani*), phone put away, gentle sleep reframes.
* **Friday Afternoon:** Weekend Permission Slip (Unplugged freedom for family, movies, and wine with friends).

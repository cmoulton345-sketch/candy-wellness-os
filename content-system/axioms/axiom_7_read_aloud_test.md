# Axiom 7: The Read-Aloud Test (Professional Prose Standard)

> **STATUS:** Primary Axiom (extends Maximal Truth framework)  
> **CREATED:** February 6, 2026  
> **VALIDATED BY:** Function First Coaching Module 1 close line correction

---

## The Problem This Solves

When writing page copy, the AI defaults to:
- Sentence fragments ("Reduces strain.")
- Incomplete thoughts ("The Clinical Conversation Most of Us Were Never Taught")
- Brainstorming artifacts (tables, bullet summaries) left in final copy

These read like notes, not like a professional peer speaking to a professional peer.

---

## The Axiom

> **Every line of page copy must pass the Read-Aloud Test.**
>
> If you can't say it out loud as a complete, natural sentence, it's not done.

---

## The Test

Before finalizing any line of page copy:

1. **Read it aloud.** Does it flow as a spoken sentence?
2. **Listen for the hang.** Does it end abruptly, leaving the listener waiting for more?
3. **Check for verbs.** Does the sentence have a subject AND an action?
4. **Imagine the audience.** Would a 40+ year-old professional say this to a peer?

If any answer is NO, rewrite.

---

## Examples

### ❌ Fails the Test

| Copy | Why It Fails |
|------|--------------|
| "The Clinical Conversation Most of Us Were Never Taught" | No verb. Hangs. Taught *what*? |
| "Reduces strain." | Fragment. No subject. |
| "90 minutes. 13 lessons. CEU credits." | List, not prose. |
| "Not coaching. Not counseling. A Coach Approach." | Fragments. No sentence structure. |

### ✅ Passes the Test

| Copy | Why It Works |
|------|--------------|
| "Watch and listen to the clinical conversation most of us were never taught to have." | Complete sentence. Action verb. Flows aloud. |
| "Putting it down reduces stress, reduces strain, and correspondingly—reduces burnout." | Full sentence. Comma structure. |
| "In 90 minutes, you'll learn the theory, see the research, and watch it in action." | Complete thought. |

---

## Audience Standard

The target audience is **professionals in their 40s**. They are:
- Experts in their field
- Time-poor and skeptical
- Allergic to marketing-speak
- Expecting peer-to-peer communication

Write as a respected colleague, not as a marketer. No fragments. No hype bursts. No teenage energy.

---

## When This Applies

| Context | Applies? |
|---------|----------|
| Brainstorming headlines | No — fragments OK for ideation |
| Final page copy | **YES** — every line must pass |
| Email subject lines | Partial — can be punchy but complete |
| CTA buttons | No — brief is fine |
| Body copy | **YES** — full sentences only |

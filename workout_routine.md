# 4-Day Upper/Lower Split (Machine & Cable Focused)

## Core Mechanical Rules
1. **Lift Weights Before Cardio:** Conserve Central Nervous System (CNS) energy for lifting.
2. **Zone 2 Cardio:** Keep heart rate at conversation pace to avoid cortisol spikes.
3. **Core Protocol:** Use anti-extension movements (planks/vacuums) to taper the waist, avoid oblique isolation.
4. **Joint Protection:** Machines and cables remove shoulder stabilization risks while maximizing muscle loading.

---

## Workout 1: Upper Body A (Focus: Chest & Lats)

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Hammer Strength Incline Chest Press** | 3 | 8-10 | 90s | Drive through elbows, not hands. |
| **Wide Grip Cable Lat Pulldown** | 3 | 10-12 | 90s | Pull to top of chest. Squeeze shoulder blades. |
| **Hammer Strength Shoulder Press** | 3 | 8-10 | 90s | Keep elbows slightly tucked in front. |
| **Cable Lateral Raise** (Low Pulley) | 3 | 12-15 | 60s | Constant tension on side deltoid. |
| **Triceps Cable Rope Pushdown** | 3 | 10-12 | 60s | Keep elbows pinned to sides. |
| **Stomach Vacuums** | 3 | 30s | 60s | Exhale all air, pull belly button to spine, hold. |
| **RKC Plank** (Hardstyle Plank) | 3 | 30s | 60s | Squeeze glutes and abs maximally. |

## Workout 2: Lower Body A (Focus: Quads & Calves)

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Machine Leg Press** | 3 | 10-12 | 90s | Keep lower back pressed firmly into pad. |
| **Smith Machine Bulgarian Split Squat** | 3 | 8-10/leg | 90s | Removes balance requirement for total quad isolation. |
| **Machine Leg Extension** | 3 | 12-15 | 60s | Control the negative (downward) motion. |
| **Machine Lying Hamstring Curl** | 3 | 10-12 | 60s | Keep hips pressed down into the pad. |
| **Standing Machine Calf Raise** | 3 | 15-20 | 60s | Pause at the bottom for deep stretch. |
| **Zone 2 Cardio** | 1 | 30m | N/A | Post-weights. Cycling, incline walking, or rowing. |

## Workout 3: Upper Body B (Focus: Shoulders & Rowing)

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Seated Cable Row** | 3 | 10-12 | 90s | Use a V-grip. Pull to belly button. |
| **Hammer Strength Flat Chest Press** | 3 | 8-10 | 90s | Mid-chest development without cuff stress. |
| **Cable Face Pulls** | 3 | 12-15 | 60s | Strengthens rear delts and rotator cuff. |
| **Machine Lateral Raise** (or Cables) | 3 | 12-15 | 60s | Push hard without gripping a free weight. |
| **Cable Rope Hammer Curls** | 3 | 10-12 | 60s | Attaching rope to low pulley works biceps smoothly. |
| **Ab Wheel Rollout** | 3 | 8-10 | 60s | Do not let lower back sag. |
| **Hanging Leg Raise** (Knees bent) | 3 | 10-12 | 60s | Hang from bar, roll pelvis up. Use padded straps if needed. |

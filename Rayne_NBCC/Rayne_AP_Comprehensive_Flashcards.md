## Module 1: Introduction to the Body & Homeostasis

### Flashcard 1
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** What are the 6 Levels of Body Organization (Simplest to Complex)?
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  1. **Chemical:** Atoms & molecules.
  2. **Cellular:** Cells (basic unit of life).
  3. **Tissue:** Group of similar cells.
  4. **Organ:** Group of tissues working together.
  5. **Organ System:** Group of organs contributing to a function.
  6. **Organism:** The complete living human being.

---

### Flashcard 2
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** What is Homeostasis?
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):** The state of relative internal body balance and stability maintained by self-regulating feedback mechanisms despite external changes.

---

### Flashcard 3
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Negative Feedback vs Positive Feedback Loops
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  * **Negative Feedback:** Opposes/negates a change to restore normal levels (e.g. regulating body temperature, blood glucose).
  * **Positive Feedback:** Enhances/amplifies a change occurring in the body (e.g. uterine contractions during childbirth, blood clotting cascade).

---

### Flashcard 4
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** What are the 2 Major Body Cavities & Subdivisions?
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  * **Ventral (Front) Cavity:** Thoracic (Mediastinum & Pleural) and Abdominopelvic (Abdominal & Pelvic).
  * **Dorsal (Back) Cavity:** Cranial (brain) and Spinal (spinal cord).

---

### Flashcard 5
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** What are the 4 Abdominopelvic Quadrants & Key Organs?
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  * **RUQ:** Liver, gallbladder, right kidney.
  * **LUQ:** Stomach, spleen, pancreas, left kidney.
  * **RLQ:** Appendix, cecum, right ovary/fallopian tube.
  * **LLQ:** Sigmoid colon, left ovary/fallopian tube.

## Cell Biology: Human Cell Structure & Function

### Flashcard 6
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Plasma Membrane
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):** Semipermeable lipid bilayer enclosing cell contents (cytoplasm). Delicate yet strong membrane that controls what enters and exits the cell.

---

### Flashcard 7
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Nucleus
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):** The cell's "control center" managing genetic material (DNA organized into chromosomes) that directs growth, division, and cell function.

---

### Flashcard 8
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Ribosomes
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):** The "protein factories" of the cell. Synthesize proteins; found floating free in cytoplasm or attached to Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER).

---

### Flashcard 9
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Mitochondria
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):** The "powerhouse" of the cell. Extracts energy from nutrients and converts it into ATP (adenosine triphosphate) chemical energy.

---

## Module 3: The Integumentary System

### Flashcard 10
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Epidermis Vascularity & Cells
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):** Outermost nonvascular layer (no blood vessels). 95% Keratinocytes (waterproof keratin protein) & 5% Melanocytes (melanin skin color pigment).

---

### Flashcard 11
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Dermis & Dermal Papillae
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):** Middle layer 20x thicker than epidermis packed with collagen & elastin. Dermal papillae in papillary layer form unique fingerprints & footprints.

---

### Flashcard 12
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Hypodermis (Subcutaneous Tissue)
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):** Foundation layer under skin composed mostly of **adipose (fat) tissue** for insulation, cushioning, energy storage, and attaching skin to underlying structures.

---

### Flashcard 13
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Eccrine vs Apocrine Sweat Glands
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  * **Eccrine:** All over body; thin watery sweat for cooling & waste excretion.
  * **Apocrine:** Axilla & groin; thicker fluid producing body odor when broken down by skin bacteria.

---

### Flashcard 14
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Cutaneous Mechanoreceptors (Sensory Receptors)
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  * **Meissner's:** Light touch/texture.
  * **Pacinian:** Deep pressure/vibration.
  * **Krause:** Cold sensation.
  * **Ruffini:** Skin stretch.
  * **Free Nerve Endings:** Pain & itch.

---

## Module 4: The Skeletal System & Joints

### Flashcard 15
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** What are the 5 Functions of the Skeletal System?
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  1. Support & shape
  2. Protection of vital organs
  3. Movement (tendon levers)
  4. Mineral storage (calcium)
  5. Hematopoiesis (blood cell production in red bone marrow)

---

### Flashcard 16
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** What are the 5 Types of Bones with Examples?
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  1. **Long Bones:** Femur, humerus, tibia.
  2. **Short Bones:** Carpals, tarsals.
  3. **Flat Bones:** Scapula, sternum, ribs, cranium.
  4. **Irregular Bones:** Vertebrae, sacrum, mandible.
  5. **Sesamoid Bones:** Patella (embedded in tendon).

---

### Flashcard 17
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Structure of a Long Bone
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  * **Diaphysis:** Shaft containing medullary cavity (yellow marrow).
  * **Epiphysis:** Bone ends containing red marrow; site of growth at epiphyseal plate.
  * **Periosteum:** Strong fibrous membrane covering bone exterior.
  * **Articular Cartilage:** Hyaline cartilage cushioning bone ends.

---

### Flashcard 18
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** Axial vs Appendicular Skeleton
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  * **Axial (80 bones):** Skull, hyoid, vertebral column, ribs, sternum.
  * **Appendicular (126 bones):** Pectoral girdle, upper limbs, pelvic girdle, lower limbs.

---

### Flashcard 19
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** 3 Types of Cartilage
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  * **Hyaline (Articular):** Smooth glass-like; covers bone ends at joints & connects ribs to sternum.
  * **Elastic:** Springy; found in ear & epiglottis.
  * **Fibrocartilage:** Tough & fibrous; found in intervertebral discs & pubic symphysis.

---

### Flashcard 20
* **FRONT (Term / Question):** 3 Functional Joint Classifications
* **BACK (Definition & Meaning):**
  * **Synarthroses:** Immovable joints (e.g. skull sutures).
  * **Amphiarthroses:** Slightly movable joints (e.g. pubic symphysis, intervertebral joints).
  * **Diarthroses:** Freely movable synovial joints (e.g. shoulder, hip, knee, elbow).

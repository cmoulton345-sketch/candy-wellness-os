ATLAS: 1. Human Cell Ultrastructure & Organelles
Cell Biology (Module 2 • Chapter 3)
🔍 Click to Zoom In Full-Screen
🔍 Enlarge Nucleus The control center of the cell; houses DNA chromosomes and directs protein synthesis.
🔍 Enlarge Nucleoli (Nucleolus) Dense dark-staining region within the nucleus that synthesizes ribosomal RNA (rRNA).
🔍 Enlarge Mitochondria The 'powerhouses of the cell'; generates ATP energy via aerobic cellular respiration.
🔍 Enlarge Ribosomes Protein factories composed of rRNA; translate mRNA transcripts into polypeptide chains.
🔍 Enlarge Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum Membranous network studded with ribosomes; folds and modifies nascent proteins.
🔍 Enlarge Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum Agranular network for lipid and steroid synthesis, calcium storage, and drug detox.
🔍 Enlarge Golgi Complex (Apparatus) Stack of flattened sacs that chemically modifies, sorts, and packages glycoproteins for secretion.
🔍 Enlarge Lysosomes 'Digestive bags' packed with hydrolytic acid enzymes to recycle damaged organelles and bacteria.
🔍 Enlarge Centrioles Paired barrel-shaped organelles that organize the mitotic spindle during cell division (mitosis).
🔍 Enlarge Plasma Membrane Selectively permeable phospholipid bilayer regulating what enters and leaves the cytoplasm.
🔍 Enlarge Microvilli Finger-like membrane folds that increase absorptive surface area (prominent in intestine/kidney).
ATLAS: 2. Integumentary System Architecture & Receptors
The Skin & Appendages (Module 3 • Chapter 6)
🔍 Click to Zoom In Full-Screen
🔍 Click to Zoom In Full-Screen
🔍 Enlarge Epidermis Avascular superficial protective layer of stratified squamous keratinized epithelium (5 strata).
🔍 Enlarge Stratum Basale Deepest epidermal monolayer of stem cells undergoing active mitosis; houses melanocytes.
🔍 Enlarge Stratum Corneum Outermost layer of dead, fully keratinized, shingle-like cells providing waterproof barrier.
🔍 Enlarge Dermis Vascular, tough connective tissue layer (20x thicker than epidermis) containing nerves, vessels, and fibers.
🔍 Enlarge Papillary Dermis Superficial dermal layer with dermal papillae forming fingerprint ridges and housing Meissner corpuscles.
🔍 Enlarge Reticular Dermis Deep dense irregular connective tissue layer rich in collagen and elastin; houses Pacinian corpuscles.
🔍 Enlarge Hypodermis (Subcutaneous) Adipose tissue foundation providing thermal insulation, energy reserve, and shock cushioning.
🔍 Enlarge Meissner Corpuscle Encapsulated tactile receptor in dermal papillae that detects light touch and low-frequency vibration.
🔍 Enlarge Pacinian Corpuscle Large lamellated onion-shaped receptor deep in reticular dermis/hypodermis detecting deep pressure.
🔍 Enlarge Free Nerve Endings Unencapsulated sensory dendrites extending into epidermis to detect nociceptive pain and temperature.
🔍 Enlarge Sebaceous Glands Oil glands attached to hair follicles secreting sebum to lubricate hair/skin and prevent microbial growth.
🔍 Enlarge Eccrine Sweat Glands Coiled tubular glands distributed across the body that secrete watery sweat for thermoregulation.
🔍 Enlarge Arrector Pili Muscle Smooth muscle bundle contracting under sympathetic stimulation to pull hair upright ('goosebumps').
ATLAS: 3. The Complete Human Skeleton (Axial & Appendicular)
Skeletal System (Module 4 • Chapter 7)
🔍 Click to Zoom In Full-Screen
🔍 Enlarge Cranium & Facial Bones Axial: Protects brain (Frontal, Parietal, Occipital) and frames face (Maxilla, Zygomatic, Mandible).
🔍 Enlarge Vertebral Column Axial: Flexible supportive column with 7 Cervical, 12 Thoracic, 5 Lumbar, Sacrum, and Coccyx.
🔍 Enlarge Thoracic Cage Axial: Bony protection for heart/lungs composed of Sternum and 12 pairs of ribs (7 True, 3 False, 2 Floating).
🔍 Enlarge Pectoral Girdle Appendicular: Attaches upper limbs to axial trunk; includes Clavicle (collarbone) and Scapula (shoulder blade).
🔍 Enlarge Humerus Appendicular: Long bone of the upper arm articulating with glenoid cavity and elbow.
🔍 Enlarge Radius & Ulna Appendicular: Forearm bones; Radius is lateral (thumb side) and Ulna is medial (pinky side).
🔍 Enlarge Carpals, Metacarpals, Phalanges Appendicular: Wrist (8 carpals), palm (5 metacarpals), and fingers (14 phalanges).
🔍 Enlarge Pelvic Girdle (Coxal Bone) Appendicular: Sturdy weight-bearing ring formed by fused Ilium, Ischium, and Pubis bones.
🔍 Enlarge Femur Appendicular: Heaviest, strongest, and longest bone in the body; articulates with acetabulum at hip.
🔍 Enlarge Patella Appendicular: Sesamoid kneecap protecting the knee joint and improving quadriceps leverage.
🔍 Enlarge Tibia & Fibula Appendicular: Lower leg; Tibia (shinbone) is medial and weight-bearing; Fibula is lateral stabilizer.
🔍 Enlarge Tarsals, Metatarsals, Calcaneus Appendicular: Ankle (7 tarsals), heel (calcaneus), arch (5 metatarsals), and toes (14 phalanges).
ATLAS: 4. Major Superficial Skeletal Muscles (Anterior & Posterior)
Muscular System (Module 5 • Chapter 8)

🔍 Click to Zoom In Full-Screen
🔍 Enlarge Sternocleidomastoid Neck muscle that flexes neck and rotates head to opposite side.
🔍 Enlarge Trapezius Large triangular upper back/neck muscle that elevates, depresses, and retracts the scapula.
🔍 Enlarge Deltoid Triangular shoulder muscle that serves as the prime mover of arm abduction.
🔍 Enlarge Pectoralis Major Large chest muscle that adducts, flexes, and medially rotates the humerus at the shoulder.
🔍 Enlarge Biceps Brachii Anterior arm muscle that is the prime mover of elbow flexion and powerful forearm supinator.
🔍 Enlarge Triceps Brachii Posterior arm muscle that acts as the primary antagonist to biceps, extending the elbow joint.
🔍 Enlarge Rectus Abdominis 'Six-pack' abdominal muscle that flexes the lumbar vertebral column and compresses abdominal cavity.
🔍 Enlarge External Obliques Lateral abdominal muscles that rotate and laterally flex the trunk.
🔍 Enlarge Latissimus Dorsi 'Swimmer's muscle'; broad lower back muscle that extends and adducts the arm.
🔍 Enlarge Gluteus Maximus Largest muscle in the body; prime mover of hip extension (rising from sitting, climbing stairs).
🔍 Enlarge Quadriceps Femoris Group Anterior thigh muscles (Rectus femoris, Vastus lateralis, medialis, intermedius) extending the knee.
🔍 Enlarge Hamstrings Group Posterior thigh muscles (Biceps femoris, Semitendinosus, Semimembranosus) flexing knee and extending hip.
🔍 Enlarge Tibialis Anterior Anterior shin muscle that performs ankle dorsiflexion and foot inversion.
🔍 Enlarge Gastrocnemius & Soleus Posterior calf muscles that plantarflex the ankle via the strong Calcaneal (Achilles) tendon.
ATLAS: 5. The Human Endocrine System (Master Glands & Hormones)
Endocrine System (Module 8 • Chapter 11)
🔍 Click to Zoom In Full-Screen
🔍 Enlarge Pineal Gland Located in brain epithalamus; secretes Melatonin in darkness to coordinate circadian sleep-wake cycles.
🔍 Enlarge Hypothalamus Master neuroendocrine coordinator; synthesizes ADH and Oxytocin; controls anterior pituitary via releasing hormones.
🔍 Enlarge Pituitary Gland (Master Gland) Anterior lobe secretes TSH, ACTH, GH, PRL, FSH, LH; Posterior lobe stores and releases ADH & Oxytocin.
🔍 Enlarge Thyroid Gland Butterfly-shaped neck gland; secretes T3 & T4 (metabolic rate) and Calcitonin (lowers blood calcium).
🔍 Enlarge Parathyroid Glands Four tiny glands on posterior thyroid; secrete Parathyroid Hormone (PTH) to elevate blood calcium levels.
🔍 Enlarge Thymus Gland Located behind sternum; secretes Thymosin to program and mature immunocompetent T-lymphocytes.
🔍 Enlarge Adrenal Glands (Suprarenal) Cortex secretes Aldosterone (Na+ retention) and Cortisol (glucose/stress); Medulla secretes Epinephrine.
🔍 Enlarge Pancreas (Islets of Langerhans) Beta cells secrete Insulin (lowers blood glucose); Alpha cells secrete Glucagon (elevates blood glucose).
🔍 Enlarge Ovaries (Female) Secrete Estrogen (secondary sex characteristics) and Progesterone (menstrual cycle & pregnancy support).
🔍 Enlarge Testes (Male) Secrete Testosterone (spermatogenesis, muscle mass, secondary male sex characteristics).

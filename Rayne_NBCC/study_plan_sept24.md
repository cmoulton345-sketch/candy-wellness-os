## 🔑 Module 1 & 2 Core Study Guide: Foundations & Suffixes

### 1. The Anatomy of a Medical Term
Medical terms are decoded starting with the **Suffix first**, then the **Prefix**, then the **Word Root**.

$$\text{Medical Term} = \text{Prefix} + \text{Word Root} + \text{Combining Vowel} + \text{Suffix}$$

* **Word Root (WR):** The core meaning of the word (e.g., *cardi/o* = heart, *gastr/o* = stomach, *oste/o* = bone, *hemat/o* = blood, *neur/o* = brain/nerve).
* **Prefix:** Added to the **beginning** to modify location, direction, or speed:
  * `brady-` = slow (e.g., *bradycardia* = slow heart rate)
  * `tachy-` = fast/rapid (e.g., *tachycardia* = fast heart rate)
  * `epi-` = above/upon (e.g., *epigastric* = above the stomach)
  * `hyper-` = high/excessive (e.g., *hypertension* = high blood pressure)
  * `hypo-` = low/deficient (e.g., *hypoglycemia* = low blood sugar)
* **Suffix:** Added to the **end** to describe disease, procedure, or condition:
  * `-itis` = inflammation (e.g., *gastritis*, *tonsillitis*, *arthritis*, *myocarditis*)
  * `-ectomy` = surgical removal (e.g., *appendectomy*, *cholecystectomy*, *lobectomy*, *mastectomy*, *hysterectomy*, *nephrectomy*)
  * `-osis` = abnormal condition (e.g., *thrombosis*, *atherosclerosis*)
  * `-pathy` = disease condition (e.g., *cardiomyopathy*)
  * `-penia` = deficiency/loss (e.g., *osteopenia* = loss of bone mineral density)
  * `-gram` = record/picture (e.g., *electrocardiogram*)

## ⏰ Module 3 Core Study Guide: Medical Abbreviations & Military Time

### 1. High-Yield Schedules & Timing
* `QAM` = Every morning
* `QHS` = At bedtime
* `BID` = Twice a day (2x/day)
* `TID` = Three times a day (3x/day)
* `QID` = Four times a day (4x/day)
* `Q2H` / `Q4H` / `Q8H` / `Q12H` = Every 2 / 4 / 8 / 12 hours
* `AC` = Before meals
* `PC` = After meals
* `STAT` = Immediately
* `PRN` = As needed

### 2. High-Yield Routes & Care Terms
* `PO` = By mouth | `NPO` = Nothing by mouth (Nil per os)
* `SL` = Sublingual (under tongue) | `IV` = Intravenous | `IM` = Intramuscular
* `C/O` = Complains of | `VS` = Vital signs | `RA` = Room air (`SpO2` on room air)
* `A&O x3` = Alert and oriented to person, place, and time
* `WNL` = Within normal limits | `ADLs` = Activities of daily living | `HOB` = Head of bed

### 3. Canadian Nursing Military Time Cheat Sheet
*Nurses use 24-hour military time to prevent AM/PM medication errors and provide precise legal charting.*
* **Rule:** If the time is 1:00 PM or later, add 12 to the hour.
  * 08:00 AM $\rightarrow$ `0800`
  * 09:30 AM $\rightarrow$ `0930`
  * 12:00 PM (Noon) $\rightarrow$ `1200`
  * 01:00 PM $\rightarrow$ `1300` ($1 + 12 = 13$)
  * 04:30 PM $\rightarrow$ `1630` ($4 + 12 = 16$)
  * 09:00 PM $\rightarrow$ `2100` ($9 + 12 = 21$)
  * 12:00 AM (Midnight) $\rightarrow$ `2400` or `0000`

### 🔑 Key Modules 1–3 High-Yield Core Summary

1. **Module 1: Basic Communication & Proxemics**
   * **Incongruence:** Nonverbal communication (80–93%) overrides spoken words. When nonverbal cues conflict with verbal words, always explore nonverbal signs.
   * **Proxemics:**
     * *Intimate Space:* 0 to 45 cm (18 in) — physical contact / close procedures.
     * *Personal Distance:* 45 cm to 1.2 m (18 in to 3.5 ft) — ideal for health history interviews.
     * *Social Distance:* 1.2 m to 3.6 m (3.5 ft to 12 ft) — formal/group interaction.
   * **Root Cause of Adverse Events:** Communication failure is the #1 leading cause of preventable medical errors and sentinel events (O'Daniel & Rosenstein, 2008).

2. **Module 2: Bridges to Effective Communication**
   * **Roach's 6 C's of Caring:** Compassion, Competence, Confidence, Conscience, Commitment, Comportment.
   * **Swanson's Theory of Caring:** Knowing, Being, Doing.
   * **Bridges:** Respect, Caring, Mutuality (equal partnership), Empowerment, Trust, Empathy (feeling with the patient nonjudgmentally).

3. **Module 3: Barriers to Effective Communication**
   * **Barriers:** False reassurance ("Everything will be fine"), Stereotyping (generalizing entire groups), Bias (personal preferences), Giving Advice/Opinions, Judgmental statements, Arguing, High Anxiety.
   * **Anxiety Management:** Speak slowly, calmly, and simply; break instructions down step-by-step.

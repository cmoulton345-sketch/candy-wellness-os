Q: What is Homeostasis?
A: The state of relative internal body balance and stability maintained by self-regulating feedback mechanisms despite external changes.

MODULE 1CARD #3
Q: Negative vs Positive Feedback Loops
Negative Feedback: Opposes/negates change to restore normal levels (body temp, blood glucose).
Positive Feedback: Amplifies change (uterine contractions, blood clotting).

Q: Cell Organelles (Membrane, Nucleus, Ribosome, Mitochondria)
• Plasma Membrane: Semipermeable lipid bilayer.
• Nucleus: Control center managing DNA.
• Ribosomes: Protein factories.
• Mitochondria: Powerhouse converting food to ATP.

MODULE 3CARD #6
Q: Epidermis & Dermis Layers
• Epidermis: Nonvascular. 95% Keratinocytes (waterproof keratin), 5% Melanocytes (melanin pigment).
• Dermis: 20x thicker. Papillary (fingerprints) & Reticular (collagen/elastin/glands).

MODULE 3CARD #7
Q: Hypodermis & Glands
• Hypodermis: Adipose fat tissue for insulation.
• Eccrine Sweat Glands: Thin watery sweat for cooling.
• Apocrine Sweat Glands: Axilla/groin; bacterial odor.
• Sebaceous Glands: Sebum oil prevents drying.

MODULE 4CARD #10
Q: Structure of a Long Bone
• Diaphysis: Shaft with medullary cavity (yellow marrow).
• Epiphysis: Ends (growth site at epiphyseal plate).
• Periosteum: Outer fibrous membrane.
• Articular Cartilage: Hyaline cushion on ends.

Q: Axial vs Appendicular Skeleton
• Axial (80 bones): Skull, hyoid, spine, ribs, sternum.
• Appendicular (126 bones): Pectoral girdle, arms, pelvic girdle, legs.

Q: 3 Types of Cartilage & 3 Joint Classes
Cartilage: Hyaline (articular cushion), Elastic (ear), Fibrocartilage (discs/pubis).
Joints: Synarthroses (immovable), Amphiarthroses (slightly movable), Diarthroses (freely movable synovial).

### Part 2: True / False Answers & Rationales

1. **FALSE** — *Rationale:* **Negative** feedback loops are the primary mechanism for daily homeostasis. Positive feedback loops are rare and reserved for specific events like child labor or blood clotting.
2. **TRUE** — *Rationale:* The muscular diaphragm forms the physical boundary dividing the upper thoracic cavity from the lower abdominopelvic cavity.
3. **FALSE** — *Rationale:* **Mitochondria** produce ATP. Ribosomes synthesize proteins.
4. **TRUE** — *Rationale:* The epidermis contains no blood vessels (nonvascular); oxygen and nutrients diffuse upward from dermal capillaries.
5. **FALSE** — *Rationale:* **Eccrine** glands produce thin watery sweat for cooling all over the body. **Apocrine** glands (axilla/groin) produce thicker fluid that skin bacteria break down into body odor.
6. **FALSE** — *Rationale:* Hematopoiesis occurs in **red bone marrow** (found in spongy bone of flat bones and long bone epiphyses), not yellow fat marrow.
7. **FALSE** — *Rationale:* The **axial** skeleton has 80 bones (head, trunk). The **appendicular** skeleton has 126 bones (limbs & girdles).
8. **TRUE** — *Rationale:* The U-shaped hyoid bone in the neck is anchored only by muscles and ligaments from the tongue and laryngeal muscles.
9. **TRUE** — *Rationale:* True ribs (1–7) attach directly to the sternum via individual costal cartilages.
10. **FALSE** — *Rationale:* The female pelvis has a **broader, wider** pubic angle (>90°) and wider inlet to facilitate childbirth.

### Part 3: Multiple Choice Answers & Rationales

1. **B** — *Rationale:* Organization proceeds from simplest to most complex: Chemical → Cellular → Tissue → Organ → Organ System → Organism.
2. **B** — *Rationale:* The appendix, cecum, and right ovary/fallopian tube are located in the Right Lower Quadrant (RLQ).
3. **C** — *Rationale:* In a feedback loop, the sensor detects change, the control center evaluates, and the **effector** (organ/gland/muscle) executes the corrective action.
4. **B** — *Rationale:* The **nucleus** stores genetic DNA organized into chromosomes and regulates cell activities.
5. **C** — *Rationale:* Keratinocytes comprise 95% of epidermal cells and produce keratin, protecting against water loss and mechanical injury.
6. **C** — *Rationale:* Dermal papillae in the **papillary layer** of the dermis project into the epidermis to form unique fingerprint ridges.
7. **B** — *Rationale:* **Pacinian corpuscles** are deep mechanoreceptors specialized for sensing deep pressure and rapid vibration.
8. **D** — *Rationale:* The skeletal system provides support, protection, lever arm movement, calcium storage, and blood cell production (hematopoiesis).
9. **B** — *Rationale:* **Vertebrae** are classified as irregular bones due to their complex non-uniform shape.
10. **C** — *Rationale:* The **medullary cavity** is the hollow center of the diaphysis containing yellow fatty marrow in adults.
11. **C** — *Rationale:* The axial skeleton comprises 80 bones (28 skull/facial, 1 hyoid, 26 vertebrae, 25 sternum/ribs).
12. **B** — *Rationale:* False ribs (8, 9, 10) do not connect directly to the sternum; their costal cartilages fuse with the cartilage of rib 7.
13. **C** — *Rationale:* **Diarthroses** (synovial joints) are freely movable joints containing a fluid-filled joint cavity.
14. **C** — *Rationale:* **Fibrocartilage** contains heavy collagen bundles to resist compression in intervertebral discs, knee menisci, and pubic symphysis.

Vocabulary terms for Module 7
The Sense Organs
Term | Definition
Sense Organs | Structures that detect stimuli and send information to the nervous system.
Sensory Receptors | Specialized cells that detect changes in the internal or external environment.
General Sense Organs | Sensory receptors distributed throughout the body in the skin, muscles, tendons, joints, and organs.
Special Sense Organs | Organs responsible for smell, taste, vision, hearing, and equilibrium.
Photoreceptors | Receptors that detect light.
Chemoreceptors | Receptors that detect chemical changes.
Nociceptors | Receptors that detect pain or tissue injury.
Thermoreceptors | Receptors that detect temperature changes.
Mechanoreceptors | Receptors that detect touch, pressure, vibration, and movement.
Proprioceptors | Receptors that detect body position and movement.
Vision | The sense of sight.
Eye | The special sense organ responsible for vision.
Fibrous Layer | The outer layer of the eye consisting of the sclera and cornea.
Sclera | The white outer portion of the eye.
Cornea | The transparent front part of the eye that allows light to enter.
Vascular Layer | The middle layer of the eye containing the choroid, ciliary muscle, and iris.
Choroid | The pigmented layer of the eye that prevents light scattering and aids vision.
Melanin | Dark pigment that helps absorb excess light within the eye.
Ciliary Muscle | An involuntary muscle that changes the shape of the lens for focusing.
Iris | The colored part of the eye that controls pupil size.
Lens | The transparent structure behind the pupil that focuses light onto the retina.
Inner Layer | The innermost layer of the eye containing the retina and optic nerve.
Retina | The light-sensitive layer of the eye containing photoreceptors.
Rods | Photoreceptor cells specialized for dim-light vision.
Cones | Photoreceptor cells specialized for color and detailed vision.
Optic Nerve | Cranial nerve II that carries visual information from the retina to the brain.
Hearing | The sense that detects sound waves.
Ear | The special sense organ responsible for hearing and balance.
External Ear | The outer portion of the ear consisting of the auricle and external acoustic canal.
Auricle (Pinna) | The visible external part of the ear that collects sound waves.
External Acoustic Canal | The passageway that carries sound waves to the eardrum.
Tympanic Membrane | The eardrum; a membrane that vibrates when struck by sound waves.
Middle Ear | The air-filled cavity containing the auditory ossicles.
Ossicles | The three tiny bones of the middle ear that transmit sound vibrations.
Malleus | One of the three auditory ossicles located in the middle ear.
Stapes | The smallest bone in the body and one of the auditory ossicles.
Inner Ear | The portion of the ear containing the vestibule, semicircular canals, and cochlea.
Vestibule | Inner ear structure involved in maintaining balance and equilibrium.
Semicircular Canals | Inner ear structures that detect head movement and help maintain balance.
Cochlea | Spiral-shaped inner ear structure responsible for hearing.
Equilibrium | The sense of balance and body position.
Vestibular Nerve | The nerve that carries balance information from the inner ear to the brain.
Cochlear Nerve | The nerve that carries hearing information from the cochlea to the brain.
Vestibulocochlear Nerve | Cranial Nerve VIII; formed by the vestibular and cochlear nerves and responsible for hearing and balance.
Taste | The sense that detects chemicals in food and drink.
Gustation | Another term for the sense of taste.
Taste Buds | Specialized sense organs that detect taste.
Gustatory Cells | Chemoreceptor cells within taste buds that detect taste stimuli.
Papillae | Small projections on the tongue that contain most taste buds.
Smell | The sense that detects airborne chemicals.
Olfaction | Another term for the sense of smell.
Olfactory Receptors | Chemoreceptors located in the upper nasal cavity that detect odors.
Olfactory Nerve | Cranial Nerve I; carries smell information from the nose to the brain.
Nasal Cavity | The space inside the nose where olfactory receptors are located.
Cranial Nerve I | The olfactory nerve responsible for smell.
Cranial Nerve II | The optic nerve responsible for vision.
Cranial Nerve VIII | The vestibulocochlear nerve responsible for hearing and balance.

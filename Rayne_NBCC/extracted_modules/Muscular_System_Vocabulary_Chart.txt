Muscular System Vocabulary Chart
Essentials
Term | Definition
Skeletal Muscle | Attaches to bones and produces voluntary movement.
Cardiac Muscle | Found only in the heart and pumps blood continuously.
Smooth Muscle | Found in organs and blood vessels; responsible for involuntary movements.
Tendon | Connects muscle to bone.
Ligament | Connects bone to bone.
Origin | Muscle attachment on the stationary bone during movement.
Insertion | Muscle attachment on the bone that moves during movement.
Agonist (Prime Mover) | Main muscle responsible for a movement.
Antagonist | Opposes the action of the agonist.
Synergist | Assists the agonist and stabilizes joints.
Muscle Fiber | An individual muscle cell.
Endomysium | Surrounds a single muscle fiber.
Perimysium | Surrounds a bundle of muscle fibers.
Fascicle | Bundle of muscle fibers.
Epimysium | Surrounds the entire muscle.
Myofibril | Thread-like structure within a muscle fiber.
Actin | Thin protein filament involved in contraction.
Myosin | Thick protein filament involved in contraction.
Sarcomere | Functional unit of muscle contraction.
Sliding Filament Theory | Explains how actin and myosin slide past each other to shorten a muscle.
Acetylcholine | Chemical messenger released by nerves to stimulate muscle fibers.
Action Potential | Electrical signal traveling through a muscle fiber.
Calcium | Mineral that triggers actin and myosin interaction.
Muscle Contraction | Generation of tension through shortening of muscle fibers.
Twitch Contraction | Single contraction-relaxation cycle from one stimulus.
Tetanic Contraction | Sustained contraction caused by rapid repeated stimuli.
Isotonic Contraction | Muscle changes length and movement occurs.
Concentric Contraction | Muscle shortens while contracting.
Eccentric Contraction | Muscle lengthens while contracting.
Isometric Contraction | Muscle produces tension without changing length.
Movement | Allows walking, running, lifting, writing, and facial expressions.
Pumping Blood | Cardiac muscle pumps blood throughout the body.
Digestion & Internal Work | Smooth muscle moves food and regulates blood vessels.
Posture & Stability | Maintains body position and stabilizes joints.
Heat Generation | Produces heat to help maintain body temperature.
Protection | Helps protect internal organs.

Vocabulary terms for Module 1 
Introduction to the body
Term | Definition | 
Anatomy | Study of the structures of the human body. | 
Physiology | Study of the functions of living organisms and their parts; how body structures work. | 
Biology | Study of all forms of life. | 
Levels of Organization | The hierarchy of increasing complexity from chemicals to the complete organism. | 
Organ | Structure composed of different tissues that performs a specific function. | 
Organ System | Group of organs working together to perform major body functions. | 
Organism | The complete living individual; the highest level of organization. | 
Body Cavity | Internal space within the body that contains organs. | 
Ventral Cavity | Major body cavity located at the front of the body. | 
Dorsal Cavity | Major body cavity located at the back of the body. | 
Thoracic Cavity | Upper division of the ventral cavity containing the lungs and heart region. | 
Mediastinum | Middle compartment of the thoracic cavity between the lungs; contains the heart and other structures. | 
Pleural Cavities | Cavities containing the right and left lungs. | 
Abdominopelvic Cavity | Lower division of the ventral cavity containing abdominal and pelvic cavities. | 
Abdominal Cavity | Contains the stomach, intestines, liver, gallbladder, pancreas, and spleen. | 
Pelvic Cavity | Contains reproductive organs, urinary bladder, and lower intestine. | 
Abdominal Quadrants | Four sections used to aid assessment and diagnosis. | 
Abdominal Regions | Nine sections used to more precisely locate organs and symptoms. | 
Cranial Cavity | Dorsal cavity that contains the brain and space within the skull. | 
Spinal Cavity | Dorsal cavity that contains the spinal cord within the vertebral column. | 
Homeostasis | State in which the body's internal environment remains stable and balanced. | 
Feedback Mechanism | Process that helps maintain homeostasis through responses to change. | 
Negative Feedback Loop | Response reverses or reduces a stimulus to return the body to a set point. | 
Positive Feedback Loop | Response reinforces a stimulus, pushing a process toward completion. | 
Receptor | Detects a change or stimulus and sends information to the control center. | 
Effector | Causes or brings about the response needed to restore balance. | 
Anatomical Directional Terms | Standard vocabulary used to describe body locations and relationships. |

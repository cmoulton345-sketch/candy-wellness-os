### Part 2: True / False Answers & Rationales

1. **FALSE** — *Rationale:* The epidermis is **nonvascular** (contains no blood vessels). It receives oxygen and nutrients by diffusion from the underlying dermal blood vessels.
2. **TRUE** — *Rationale:* The reticular layer is the deeper, thicker layer of the dermis packed with collagen, elastic fibers, blood vessels, sweat/oil glands, and Pacinian pressure receptors.
3. **FALSE** — *Rationale:* **Eccrine** glands secrete thin watery sweat continuously. **Apocrine** glands secrete in periodic spurts thicker fluid in axilla/groin regions.
4. **FALSE** — *Rationale:* The hypodermis is composed of **adipose (fat) tissue** and loose connective tissue, not cartilage.
5. **FALSE** — *Rationale:* Hair growth begins deep in the **hair papilla/bulb** at the base of the follicle. The hair shaft is non-living dead keratinized tissue.
6. **TRUE** — *Rationale:* Cyanosis is a clinical sign of hypoxia (poor tissue oxygenation), visible as a bluish discoloration of skin and nail beds.
7. **TRUE** — *Rationale:* Sebaceous glands are stimulated by sex hormones (androgens) during puberty, leading to acne, and decrease activity in older adulthood.
8. **TRUE** — *Rationale:* Dehydrocholesterol in skin cells is converted by UV light into Vitamin D3 precursors, which are converted to active Vitamin D by liver and kidney enzymes.
9. **FALSE** — *Rationale:* Mitosis occurs in the deepest layer, the **Stratum Basale**. The Stratum Corneum is the outermost layer of dead keratinized cells.
10. **TRUE** — *Rationale:* Free nerve endings are unencapsulated nociceptors/thermoreceptors that signal pain, temperature extremes, and itching to the brain.

### Part 3: Multiple Choice Answers & Rationales

1. **B** — *Rationale:* From deep to superficial: **Stratum Basale → Stratum Spinosum → Stratum Granulosum → Stratum Lucidum → Stratum Corneum** (Mnemonic: *Come, Let's Get Sun Burned* in reverse!).
2. **C** — *Rationale:* Keratinocytes represent 95% of epidermal cells and produce keratin, a fibrous protein that waterproofs skin and prevents moisture loss.
3. **B** — *Rationale:* Dermal papillae in the **papillary layer** of the dermis project into the epidermis, forming fingerprint ridges before birth.
4. **A** — *Rationale:* The **dermis** is 20 times thicker than the epidermis and provides mechanical strength via collagen and elastic fibers.
5. **B** — *Rationale:* The hypodermis consists mainly of **adipose tissue** (fat), serving as thermal insulation, energy storage, and protective shock absorption.
6. **C** — *Rationale:* The **hair papilla/bulb** contains a rich blood supply from dermal capillaries that nourishes dividing cells for hair growth.
7. **C** — *Rationale:* Fresh apocrine secretion is odorless; body odor occurs when **skin bacteria (microbes)** break down organic molecules in the secretion.
8. **B** — *Rationale:* **Pacinian corpuscles** are encapsulated mechanoreceptors located deep in the dermis/hypodermis that sense deep pressure and vibration.
9. **B** — *Rationale:* Cold exposure triggers **dermal vasoconstriction** (saving core heat), shivering, and arrector pili contraction (goosebumps).
10. **C** — *Rationale:* Transdermal patches rely on **passive absorption** of fat-soluble drugs through the epidermis into dermal blood vessels.
11. **A** — *Rationale:* The **end bulb of Krause** is specialized for detecting cold temperatures.
12. **B** — *Rationale:* When trapped sebum and keratin in a pore are exposed to oxygen in the air, the melanin/sebum oxidizes and darkens into a **blackhead** (open comedone).

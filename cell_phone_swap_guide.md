# iPhone Swap Guide: iPhone 16 & iPhone 17 Pro (TELUS eSIM)

## Goal
* **Parent:** iPhone 16 (Parent's Phone Number + Parent's Apple ID & Data)
* **Daughter:** iPhone 17 Pro (Daughter's Phone Number + Daughter's Apple ID & Data)

---

## Phase 1: Back Up Both Phones First
*Do this before wiping or moving anything so no photos, messages, or app data are lost.*

1. **On iPhone 17 Pro (currently with Parent's data):**
   * Go to **Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > iCloud Backup**.
   * Tap **Back Up Now** and wait for it to finish.
2. **On iPhone 16 (currently with Daughter's data):**
   * Go to **Settings > [Daughter's Name] > iCloud > iCloud Backup**.
   * Tap **Back Up Now** and wait for it to finish.

---

## Phase 2: Swap the eSIM Phone Numbers
*Do this while both phones are turned on and nearby.*

1. **Move Daughter's Number to the iPhone 17 Pro:**
   * Grab the **iPhone 17 Pro**.
   * Go to **Settings > Cellular > Add eSIM** (or *Set Up Cellular*).
   * Select **Transfer From Nearby iPhone**.
   * Hold the **iPhone 16** next to it.
   * On the iPhone 16, tap **Approve/Transfer** when prompted and enter the verification code.
   * *Result:* Daughter's number is now active on the iPhone 17 Pro.

2. **Move Parent's Number to the iPhone 16:**
   * Grab the **iPhone 16**.
   * Go to **Settings > Cellular > Add eSIM** (or *Set Up Cellular*).
   * Select **Transfer From Nearby iPhone**.
   * Hold the **iPhone 17 Pro** next to it.
   * On the iPhone 17 Pro, tap **Approve/Transfer** when prompted and enter the verification code.
   * *Result:* Parent's number is now active on the iPhone 16.

> 💡 **TELUS Troubleshooting / EID Note:**
> If iOS displays *"Transfer Not Supported"* during Quick Transfer, log into your TELUS My Account portal online, select "Change Device / eSIM" for each line, and enter the new phone's **EID** and **IMEI** (found under **Settings > General > About**).

---

## Phase 3: Sign Out & Factory Reset (KEEP eSIM)
*This clears personal accounts and data off the hardware so the correct owners can log in.*

1. **Turn off Security Features:**
   * On both phones, go to **Settings > Face ID & Passcode** and turn off **Stolen Device Protection** (if enabled).
2. **Sign Out of Apple Account:**
   * On both phones, go to **Settings > [Name at top]**, scroll all the way to the bottom, and tap **Sign Out** (enter Apple ID password to turn off Find My).
3. **Factory Reset (CRITICAL STEP):**
   * On both phones, go to **Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Erase All Content and Settings**.
   * ⚠️ **IMPORTANT:** When asked what to do with cellular plans/eSIMs, select **KEEP eSIM**. Do **NOT** select "Delete eSIM".
   * Wait for both phones to restart to the multi-language "Hello" screen.

---

## Phase 4: Restore Personal Data to the Correct Phones

1. **Set Up Parent's iPhone 16:**
   * Turn on the iPhone 16, select language, connect to Wi-Fi.
   * On the *Apps & Data* screen, select **Restore from iCloud Backup**.
   * Log in with **Parent's Apple ID**.
   * Select Parent's most recent backup (from Phase 1).

2. **Set Up Daughter's iPhone 17 Pro:**
   * Turn on the iPhone 17 Pro, select language, connect to Wi-Fi.
   * On the *Apps & Data* screen, select **Restore from iCloud Backup**.
   * Log in with **Daughter's Apple ID**.
   * Select Daughter's most recent backup (from Phase 1).

# Health and Safety Program (OHSMS) - Outline

This foundational outline is built directly upon the regulatory bedrock of British Columbia, specifically tailored for LNG facility operations.

## The Regulatory Foundation

Our program must comply primarily with:
1. **Workers Compensation Act (WCA)** — Specifically Part 2, Division 4 (General Duties of Employers).
2. **WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation** — Specifically **Part 3 (Rights and Responsibilities)** and **Part 23 (Oil and Gas)**.
3. **Energy Resource Activities Act (ERAA)** — Regulated by the BC Energy Regulator (BCER), which holds jurisdiction over the facility's safe operation.
4. **CSA Z276** (Liquefied Natural Gas — Production, Storage, and Handling) — The national standard governing our facility.
5. **CSA Z1000** (Occupational Health and Safety Management) — The national best-practice standard for structuring safety systems.

WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation **Section 3.3 (Contents of program)** dictates the mandatory minimum elements we must include.

## Phase 1: Core Program Administration (The Framework)
*Regulatory Driver: WorkSafeBC OHS Reg 3.3(a), WCA s. 21*

*   **1.1 Management Commitment & Safety Policy** (Signed by the President/CEO)
*   **1.2 Roles and Responsibilities**
    *   Employer Duties (WCA s. 21)
    *   Supervisor Duties (WCA s. 23)
    *   Worker Duties (WCA s. 22)
    *   Prime Contractor Responsibilities (WCA s. 24)
*   **1.3 Joint Occupational Health and Safety (JOHS) Committee**
    *   Terms of Reference, meeting requirements, and worker representation (WCA s. 31-46).
*   **1.4 Right to Refuse Unsafe Work**
    *   Clear, step-by-step procedure for work refusals and investigations (OHS Reg 3.12).
*   **1.5 Disciplinary Policy**
    *   Progressive discipline for safety violations to demonstrate due diligence.

## Phase 2: Hazard Identification & Risk Control (The Core Function)
*Regulatory Driver: WorkSafeBC OHS Reg 3.3(c), OHS Reg Part 5, CSA Z1002*

*   **2.1 Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment (HIRA)**
    *   Methodology for identifying hazards (physical, chemical, biological, ergonomic, psychosocial).
*   **2.2 Field Level Hazard Assessments (FLHA)**
    *   Daily, task-specific worker assessments.
*   **2.3 Process Safety Management (PSM)**
    *   Mandated by **CSA Z276**. Includes Process Hazard Analysis (HAZOP, LOPA).
*   **2.4 Safe Work Procedures (SWPs)**
    *   Written instructions for hazardous tasks (OHS Reg 3.3(c)).
*   **2.5 Management of Change (MOC)**
    *   Procedure to assess safety implications before changing equipment, personnel, or processes (Critical for LNG).

## Phase 3: Critical Hazard Programs (The High-Risk Controls)
*Regulatory Driver: WorkSafeBC OHS Reg Parts 8, 9, 10, 23*

*   **3.1 Permit to Work System** (Hot work, cold work, ground disturbance) (OHS Reg Part 23)
*   **3.2 Confined Space Entry Program** (OHS Reg Part 9)
*   **3.3 Lock-Out / Tag-Out (Energy Isolation)** (OHS Reg Part 10)
*   **3.4 Fall Protection Program** (OHS Reg Part 11)
*   **3.5 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Program** (OHS Reg Part 8)
*   **3.6 Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS)** (OHS Reg Part 5)

## Phase 4: Training, Supervision, and Competency
*Regulatory Driver: WorkSafeBC OHS Reg 3.3(g), 3.22 (Young/New Workers)*

*   **4.1 Site Orientation Program**
*   **4.2 Young and New Worker Training** (Specifically required by OHS Reg 3.22)
*   **4.3 Contractor Safety Management** 
    *   Pre-qualification, onboarding, and oversight of third-party contractors.
*   **4.4 Competency Assessments**
    *   Verifying workers can actually do the job safely, not just sign a piece of paper.
*   **4.5 Supervisor Training Program**
    *   Ensuring supervisors understand their legal liabilities under the WCA.

## Phase 5: Inspections, Observations, and Maintenance
*Regulatory Driver: WorkSafeBC OHS Reg 3.3(b), 3.5, CSA Z276*

*   **5.1 Workplace Inspection Program**
    *   Daily informal, weekly supervisor, and monthly JOHS committee inspections.
*   **5.2 Preventive Maintenance Program**
    *   Inspection and maintenance of critical safety equipment and process controls (CSA Z276).
*   **5.3 Behavioral Safety Observations**
    *   Peer-to-peer coaching and leading indicator tracking.

## Phase 6: Emergency Response & Incident Management
*Regulatory Driver: WorkSafeBC OHS Reg 3.3(e), Part 31, BCER ERAA*

*   **6.1 Emergency Response Plan (ERP)**
    *   Must cover fire, LNG release, medical emergencies, natural disasters, and security threats (BCER & OHS Reg Part 31).
*   **6.2 First Aid Program**
    *   Facilities, attendants, and transport requirements based on Schedule 3-A.
*   **6.3 Incident Reporting & Investigation Program**
    *   Requirements for preliminary (48 hr) and full (30 day) investigations (WCA s. 68-73). Includes Root Cause Analysis methodology (e.g., TapRooT, 5-Why).
*   **6.4 Regulatory Notification Procedures**
    *   Who calls WorkSafeBC, BCER, and the RCMP, and when.

## Phase 7: Records, Review, and Continuous Improvement
*Regulatory Driver: WorkSafeBC OHS Reg 3.3(d), 3.3(f)*

*   **7.1 Records Management**
    *   Retention schedules for training, inspections, and investigations (OHS Reg 3.3(f)).
*   **7.2 Leading and Lagging Indicators (Safety Statistics)**
    *   Tracking TRIR, LTIR, near-misses, and corrective action closure rates.
*   **7.3 Annual Program Audit and Review**
    *   Mandatory management review of the safety system to ensure it is actually working (OHS Reg 3.3(d)).

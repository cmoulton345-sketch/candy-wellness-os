# Construction-to-Operations Transition Risk Register
**Project Phase:** Construction, Commissioning, Transition & Continuous Operations  
**Primary Location:** British Columbia, Canada (LNG, Squamish)  
**Governing Jurisdictions:** BC Energy Regulator (BCER), WorkSafeBC, Technical Safety BC, CSA Z276, CSA Z1002  
*Cross-Jurisdictional Note: Equivalent WorkSafeNB (New Brunswick) OHS Regulation 91-191 clauses are cross-referenced for completeness.*

---

## 1. Overview & Risk Matrix
This risk register identifies and prioritizes hazards associated with the transition from the construction phase, through commissioning, to early and continuous operations (CTO) of a new build LNG facility. Risk ratings are determined using a standard 5x5 Likelihood and Severity matrix:
*   **Likelihood (L):** 1 (Rare) to 5 (Almost Certain)
*   **Severity (S):** 1 (Negligible) to 5 (Catastrophic/Fatality)
*   **Risk Level (R):** Likelihood x Severity (Score 1-25)

## 5. Maintenance & Review Protocol
1. **Frequency:** This transition risk register must be reviewed monthly by the Joint Commissioning & Operations Leadership Team.
2. **Trigger Events:** A Management of Change (MOC) request, a high-potential (HiPo) near-miss, or a significant schedule deviation must trigger an immediate review of the affected risk items.
3. **Closing Risks:** Risks associated with pre-commissioning (e.g., pressure testing) may be closed out and archived once system turnover packets are signed off.

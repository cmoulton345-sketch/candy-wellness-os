Integrated Operational & Safety Management System

The 7 Pillars of safety and process controls embedded directly into LNG terminal operations.
01
Leadership & Governance
Operational accountability, executive safety commitment, and regulatory oversight structures.
02
Risk Assessment (HIRA & PHA)
Methodologies for analyzing process safety, identifying occupational hazards, and mapping envelopes.
03
Safe Systems of Work (Work Control)
Permit to Work systems, Lock-Out/Tag-Out boundaries, and marine zone safety controls.
04
Asset Integrity (SCE & CUI)
Maintenance cycles prioritizing Safety-Critical Elements and managing cryogenic corrosion.
05
Management of Change (MOC)
Safety reviews before any physical, operational, process system, or organization changes.
06
Operator Competency & Training
Control room simulator training, fatigue logging, and contractor safety vetting.
07
Incident Investigation & Learning
Structured root cause analysis, action item tracking, and safety sharing protocols.

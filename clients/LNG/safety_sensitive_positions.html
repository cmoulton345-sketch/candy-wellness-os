Risk ID Nomenclature & Legend

Reference guide mapping risk prefix categories back to the primary LNG Risk Registers
CRIT
SIMOPS & Commissioning: System bypasses, initial gas introduction, interface ignition sources.
LOTO
Lockout / Tagout: Energy isolation controls, boundary lockboxes, and handovers.
CSE
Confined Space Entry: Tank/vessel entry, blind lists, atmospheric testing, and sentry logs.
WAT
Over-Water Work: Jetty access, falls over marine environments, and personal flotation.
UTIL
Purging & Utilities: Nitrogen asphyxiation, high-voltage switching, and arc flash.
CHEM
Hazardous Chemicals: Ammonia leak detection, deluge systems, and chemical handling.
MAR
Marine Terminal: Loading arm cryogenic tests, ERS validation, and vessel berthing.
TS
Pressure Testing: Hydrotest boundary lines, transducer calibration, and line rupture.
ENV
Environmental Compliance: Acoustic limits, vessel speeds, flaring limits, MMO logs.
OPS
Commissioning & Ops: Thermal shock cool-down, CUI piping checks, document packages.
REG
Regulatory Approvals: BCER permit conditions, Federal/Provincial Leave to Open.
LIFT
SIMOPS Critical Lifting: Crane lifts, module rigging, and drop zones.
VEH
Mobile Equipment: Traffic management, speed control, pedestrian separation.
OPS-CRIT
Process Safety (Ops): BLEVE vapor clouds, RPT, tank rollover, alarm flooding.

Fatigue Risk Management System (FRMS)

Governing protocols to prevent fatigue-related errors in safety-sensitive positions

Hours of Service Limits

  • Max Shift Length: 12 hours (strict cap)
  • Min Rest Period: 11 hours between shifts
  • Consecutive Nights: Max 4 shifts
  • Consecutive Days: Max 6 shifts
  • Overtime Limit: Max 60 hours per week

Drug & Alcohol Policy

  • Zero Tolerance: 0.00% BAC / Drug testing
  • Pre-Employment: Mandatory for all SSP roles
  • Post-Incident: Must test within 2 hours
  • Medication Check: Declared to Occ Health Nurse

Cognitive Monitoring

  • FLHA Assessment: Sleep quality logged daily
  • CRO Rotations: Screen breaks every 4 hours
  • Face-to-Face Check: Mid-shift supervisor walk
  • Fatigue Reports: Immediate MOC trigger

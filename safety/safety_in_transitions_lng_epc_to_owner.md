# 🛡️ LNG FACILITY EH&S SAFETY IN TRANSITION PLAN (STP)
## EPC Contractor to Operating Owner Transition Governance

> **FACILITY TYPE:** Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) Export / Regasification Facility  
> **JURISDICTION:** British Columbia, Canada  
> **GOVERNING REGULATORS:** BC Energy Regulator (BCER), WorkSafeBC, Technical Safety BC (TSBC), BCEAO, Impact Assessment Agency of Canada (IAAC), Squamish Nation (SNEAA)  
> **PRIMARY CODES & STANDARDS:** CSA Z276, NFPA 59A, CSA Z662, WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation (Part 23 / Part 10 / Part 9 / Part 31), BC Energy Resource Activities Act (ERAA)  
> **MILESTONE FOCUS:** Pre-Commissioning to Fuel Gas Entry (Introduction of Hydrocarbons) to Commercial Operations

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

### 1.1 Purpose
This Safety in Transition Plan (STP) establishes the formal management system bridge, regulatory compliance mechanisms, and operational control transfer protocol as the LNG Facility transitions from the **Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) Contractor** to the **Operating Owner**. 

The transition from a construction site to a live hydrocarbon process facility represents the single highest risk phase in the asset lifecycle. This document mandates the precise bridging mechanisms required to safely manage **Simultaneous Operations (SIMOPS)**, transfer **Care, Custody, and Control (CCC)**, and enforce strict Process Safety Management (PSM) and Occupational Health & Safety (OHS) governance before, during, and after **Fuel Gas Entry (First Hydrocarbon Introduction)**.

### 1.2 Scope
This plan applies to all personnel, contractors, subcontractors, commissioning teams, and operations staff across all battery limits of the LNG Facility, including marine berthing, LNG storage tanks, pretreatment, liquefaction trains, flare systems, utilities, and fuel gas feed pipelines.

## 3. Four-Phase Transition Architecture

The transfer of EHS governance is structured into four distinct execution phases, ensuring zero ambiguity regarding who holds operational authority at any given moment:

```
  ┌───────────────────────┐      ┌───────────────────────┐      ┌───────────────────────┐      ┌───────────────────────┐
  │        PHASE I        │      │       PHASE II        │      │       PHASE III       │      │       PHASE IV        │
  │   EPC CONSTRUCT &     │ ───> │  PRE-FUEL GAS ENTRY   │ ───> │   FUEL GAS ENTRY      │ ───> │   HOT COMMISSIONING   │
  │   COLD COMMISSIONING  │      │     (SIMOPS & PSSR)   │      │  (HYDROCARBON MILESTONE)│      │  & OWNER OPERATIONS   │
  └───────────────────────┘      └───────────────────────┘      └───────────────────────┘      └───────────────────────┘
    EPC Prime Contractor           Shared SIMOPS Control           Owner Live Plant PTW            Owner Operational Care,
    Construction PTW               PSSR Gate Signed               Energized Boundary Demarcation  Custody & Control (CCC)
```

### Phase I: EPC Dominant Construction & Cold Commissioning
* **Primary Authority:** EPC Contractor (Designated Prime Contractor under WorkSafeBC OHS Reg 3.12).
* **Workplace Governance:** EPC Safety Management System (SMS) and Construction Permit to Work (PTW).
* **Activities:** Mechanical completion, hydrotesting, pneumatic testing, electrical loop checks, instrument calibration, cold nitrogen purging.
* **EHS Focus:** High-risk construction hazards (rigging/lifting, working at heights, excavation, energy isolation for hydrotesting).

### Phase II: Pre-Fuel Gas Entry & SIMOPS Governance
* **Primary Authority:** Joint EPC/Owner Operational Commissioning Committee (OCC).
* **Workplace Governance:** SIMOPS Protocol activated. Boundary demarcation established between "Greenfield Construction Zones" and "Brownfield Operational Commissioning Zones."
* **Activities:** Pre-Commissioning completion, Pre-Startup Safety Reviews (PSSRs), inert nitrogen drying and purging of fuel gas piping.
* **EHS Focus:** Strict physical segregation, barrier management, LOTO transfer preparation, and readiness of emergency shutdown (ESD) systems.

### Phase III: Fuel Gas Entry (The Hydrocarbon Milestone Gate)
* **Primary Authority:** **Operating Owner Operations Manager** (Care, Custody, and Control signed for Fuel Gas System).
* **Workplace Governance:** Owner Live Plant PTW System and Owner LOTO Standard enforced within fuel gas battery limits.
* **Activities:** Final PSSR sign-off, BCER authorization verification, removal of nitrogen blinds, controlled cracking of fuel gas tie-in valve, pressuring fuel gas headers, initial burner light-off.
* **EHS Focus:** 24/7 Fire & Gas detection online, ignition source control (Hot Work ban in gas-in zones), breathing air deployment, emergency response readiness.

### Phase IV: Hot Commissioning & Full Owner Operational Dominance
* **Primary Authority:** Operating Owner (Prime Contractor designation fully transferred).
* **Workplace Governance:** Owner Integrated Management System (IMS), ISO 45001 / ISO 14001 / CSA Z276 compliant.
* **Activities:** Gas turbine generators fired on fuel gas, liquefaction train chill-down, LNG production, tank filling, marine loading.
* **EHS Focus:** Process Safety Management (PSM), Management of Change (MOC), occupational hygiene, environmental emissions monitoring.

## 4. Program-by-Program Bridging Protocol (EPC → Owner)

As the facility reaches the **Fuel Gas Entry Milestone**, each key EH&S safety program must explicitly bridge from the EPC Contractor's construction procedures to the Owner's operational management system:

### 4.1 Permit to Work (PTW) & SIMOPS Bridging

```
                      TRANSITION OF PERMIT TO WORK GOVERNANCE
                      
  EPC Construction PTW Zone                         Owner Live Plant PTW Zone
┌───────────────────────────────┐                 ┌───────────────────────────────┐
│ - Mechanical Assembly         │  FENCE / BARRIER│ - Fuel Gas System (Energized) │
│ - Piping Tie-ins              │ <=============> │ - Nitrogen Purged Headers     │
│ - Construction Hot Work       │  RED LINE ZONE  │ - Owner Hot Work Ban          │
│ Governed by EPC PTW           │                 │ Governed by Owner Live PTW    │
└───────────────────────────────┘                 └───────────────────────────────┘
```

* **The Problem:** Construction contractors are accustomed to open construction permits (Hot Work, Grinding) across the site. Introducing fuel gas creates an immediate explosion hazard if construction hot work occurs near gas-in battery limits.
* **Bridging Protocol:**
  1. **Red Line Boundary Demarcation:** Prior to Fuel Gas Entry, the Owner Operations team draws physical "Red Lines" on plant plot plans and installs physical barricading (fencing, signage, access control turnstiles) around the Fuel Gas Skids, Gas Turbines, and Flare headers.
  2. **PTW Handover:** All EPC construction permits within the Red Line Boundary are formally **revoked and cancelled**.
  3. **Owner Live PTW Supremacy:** Any work inside the Red Line Boundary requires an **Owner Live Plant Permit to Work**, issued exclusively by the Owner Area Operating Authority (AOA).
  4. **Hot Work Restrictions (WorkSafeBC Part 23.9):** Hot work is strictly prohibited within 25 meters of any energized fuel gas component during initial gas entry. Any subsequent hot work requires continuous LEL gas monitoring and Owner Operations Manager approval.

### 4.2 Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) & Energy Isolation Transfer

* **Regulatory Basis:** WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation Part 10 (De-energization and Lockout) & CSA Z276 Section 10.
* **Bridging Protocol:**
  1. **Construction Lockout to Operations LOTO:** Construction locks used during hydrostatic testing and loop checks must be systematically removed under joint EPC/Owner supervision.
  2. **Key Box Transfer System (Red Key / Blue Key):**
     * **Owner Primary Isolation Lock (Red):** Applied by the Owner Authorized Isolator on all fuel gas boundary valves, blind flanges, and electrical breakers feeding fuel gas equipment.
     * **EPC Construction Lock (Blue):** Applied onto the Owner Lock Box if construction personnel need to complete non-gas physical work outside the hydrocarbon boundary.
  3. **Car-Seal & Positive Isolation Registry:** All boundary blinds and double-block-and-bleed (DBB) isolation valves for Fuel Gas Entry are logged in a master **Car-Seal Register**. Valves are car-sealed locked shut (CSC) or locked open (CSO) under Owner LOTO authority.

### 4.3 Process Safety Management (PSM), PSSR & Management of Change (MOC)

* **Regulatory Basis:** CSA Z276 Section 10 & 14, BCER LNGFR.
* **Bridging Protocol:**
  1. **PSSR (Pre-Startup Safety Review) Gate Execution:** Fuel Gas Entry cannot occur until a formal PSSR is completed for the specific system. The PSSR team (Lead Process Safety Engineer, Owner Operations Manager, EHS Manager, EPC Commissioning Lead) must physically inspect the field and sign off on 7 core criteria:
     * Construction and equipment conform to design specifications (CSA Z276 / ASME B31.3).
     * Process safety, hazard analysis (HAZOP/SIL) action items are 100% closed out.
     * Operating, maintenance, and emergency procedures are approved and in place.
     * Training of operating personnel is complete and verified.
     * Fire & Gas detection and ESD systems are fully commissioned and tested.
     * Environmental approvals (BCER / BCEAO) are active.
     * Management of Change (MOC) items generated during construction are resolved.
  2. **MOC Transition:** The EPC "Field Change Request" (FCR) system is frozen for gas-in systems. All future modifications must pass through the formal **Owner Operational MOC System**, requiring engineering review and risk assessment prior to physical modification.

### 4.4 Emergency Response Plan (ERP) Integration & ICS Bridge

* **Regulatory Basis:** BCER Emergency Management Regulation, WorkSafeBC OHS Reg Part 31, BCEAO EA Conditions.
* **Bridging Protocol:**
  1. **EPC Site ERP Suspension:** The EPC Construction ERP (focused on medical evacuation, trench rescue, and construction fires) is transitioned into an **Integrated Facility ERP** managed under the Incident Command System (ICS).
  2. **Fuel Gas Hazard Readiness:** 
     * Sirens and gas alarm tones (Wail = Evacuation, Intermittent = Gas Detection) are tested plant-wide.
     * Muster stations are relocated outside potential gas dispersion plumes.
     * 24/7 Emergency Response Team (ERT) equipped with breathing apparatus (SCBA), thermal imaging, and gas detectors is stationed on-site prior to cracking the fuel gas tie-in valve.
  3. **Mutual Aid & Municipal Notification:** Local Fire Departments (Squamish/District First Responders), BCER Emergency Response Line, and Squamish Nation Emergency Liaisons receive formal 72-hour written notice prior to Fuel Gas Entry.

### 4.5 Environmental Monitoring & Indigenous Collaboration

* **Regulatory Basis:** BCEAO EA Certificate, BC Environmental Management Act (EMA), Squamish Nation SNEAA.
* **Bridging Protocol:**
  1. **Air Quality & Startup Flaring Governance:** Fuel gas entry and line displacement require controlled venting/flaring. The Owner Environmental Lead coordinates with the EPC Commissioning team to ensure flaring volumes comply with BCER Flaring Regulations and BCEAO EA ambient air quality thresholds.
  2. **Indigenous Environmental Monitors (IEMs):** Squamish Nation IEMs are provided full access to the commissioning command center and field locations during Fuel Gas Entry to monitor noise, air emissions, and marine boundary conditions as mandated under SNEAA.

## 5. Milestone Focus: Fuel Gas Entry (First Gas In Execution Protocol)

The milestone of Fuel Gas Entry is the critical tipping point where the facility transforms from an inert piping assembly to a high-hazard live process plant. The following mandatory step-by-step checklist governs Fuel Gas Entry:

```markdown
### 📋 FUEL GAS ENTRY MANDATORY APPROVAL & EXECUTION GATE

#### Step 1: Pre-Condition Verification (72 Hours Prior)
- [ ] BCER Written Authorization to Introduce Hydrocarbons received and logged.
- [ ] TSBC Operating Permits active for all fuel gas pressure vessels and fuel piping.
- [ ] PSSR for Fuel Gas System signed off with ZERO Category A (blocking) punch list items.
- [ ] All construction personnel evacuated from the Fuel Gas Red Line Boundary; construction PTWs cancelled.
- [ ] Nitrogen drying and purging completed; oxygen content in fuel gas headers verified < 0.5% O2.

#### Step 2: Site Hardening & Boundary Control (24 Hours Prior)
- [ ] Red Line Physical Barricades secured; Security personnel stationed at access control gates.
- [ ] Ignition Source Exclusion Sweep completed: All non-explosion-proof electrical tools, vehicles, and cell phones removed from Gas-In zone.
- [ ] Fire & Gas (F&G) Detection System set to LIVE (24/7 automated monitoring with direct ESD trip logic).
- [ ] Windsocks inspected and illuminated; breathing air (SCBA) banks pressurized.

#### Step 3: Execution of Gas Entry (Day of Hydrocarbon Introduction)
- [ ] Operations Manager convenes Final Execution Briefing with Operations, EHS, EPC Commissioning, and Indigenous Monitors.
- [ ] Owner Area Operator removes Car-Seal CSC from Main Fuel Gas Supply Tie-In Valve.
- [ ] Owner Area Operator slowly cracks Fuel Gas Inlet Valve to pressurize fuel gas header at controlled rate (max 2 bar/min).
- [ ] Handheld LEL gas testing performed continuously at all flanged connections, valve packing, and instrument taps along the fuel gas route.
- [ ] System pressure stabilized; fuel gas samples drawn to confirm gas composition.

#### Step 4: Post-Gas Entry Governance
- [ ] Fuel Gas Zone officially declared a "LIVE BROWNFIELD HYDROCARBON ZONE."
- [ ] Plant Status Board updated; Owner Live PTW and LOTO systems permanently active.
- [ ] Written confirmation of successful Fuel Gas Entry dispatched to BCER, WorkSafeBC, and Squamish Nation.
```

## 6. Prime Contractor Designation & Regulatory Handoff

Under **WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation Section 3.12**, a construction site must have a single designated **Prime Contractor** responsible for workplace health and safety.

### 6.1 Formal Transfer Procedure
1. **Notice of Transfer:** 30 days prior to Fuel Gas Entry, the Owner submits a formal **Notice of Prime Contractor Transfer** to WorkSafeBC and the EPC Contractor.
2. **Geographic Split (Phase II SIMOPS):** During pre-commissioning, WorkSafeBC is notified of a geographic split:
   * **Zone A (Construction/Greenfield):** EPC Contractor remains Prime Contractor.
   * **Zone B (Fuel Gas & Utilities/Brownfield):** Operating Owner becomes Prime Contractor.
3. **Final Facility Transfer (Phase IV):** Upon completion of hot commissioning and commercial handover, EPC relinquishes Prime Contractor status entirely. The Operating Owner assumes 100% Prime Contractor responsibility across all land and marine battery limits.

2. JOE'S HEALTHCARE & DOCTOR STRATEGY: THE TWO OPTIONS

Managing Crohn's disease requires uninterrupted access to specialists, colonoscopy monitoring, and biologic medications (such as Inflectra, Remicade, or Stelara). Here is how both paths actually work:

OPTION A (RECOMMENDED FOR HEALTH & DOCTOR SECURITY):
Maintain NB Medicare as an Interprovincial Mobile Worker

Because your spouse, family home, and legal ties remain in New Brunswick, you qualify as a mobile worker temporarily stationed out-of-province for employment.

- NB Family Doctor: 100% Protected. You remain an active, funded patient on your NB family doctor's roster. You will never be de-rostered or lose your spot to the provincial waitlist.
- Crohn's Specialist (GI): 100% Protected. Your NB specialist remains your primary attending doctor for annual colonoscopies, blood monitoring, and renewals during trips home.
- Biologics & Prescriptions: Your new employer's private extended health plan covers your prescription drugs. Your drug's Patient Support Program (such as BioAdvance or AbbVie Care) coordinates medication delivery or infusion clinic appointments at a Vancouver clinic while your NB specialist remains the prescriber.
- Coverage in BC: Your NB Medicare card covers emergency, walk-in clinic, and hospital visits in BC under Canada's reciprocal medical agreement.
- Tax Alignment: Clean and audit-proof. You file as an NB resident (matching your family home and spouse), eliminating the risk of CRA clawing back out-of-province tax adjustments.

OPTION B (THE BC TAX SAVINGS ROUTE):
Switch to BC MSP (Medical Services Plan)

You establish formal provincial residency in BC to take advantage of lower provincial income tax rates.

- NB Family Doctor: HIGH RISK. When you register for BC MSP, NB Medicare is notified and cancels your NB card. With New Brunswick's severe doctor shortage, clinic managers routinely de-roster patients who hold out-of-province health cards. Once dropped, getting back on an NB family doctor roster is nearly impossible.
- Crohn's Specialist (GI): Can technically bill BC MSP under reciprocal billing when you are in NB, but cross-provincial telemedicine (phone or virtual visits) is restricted by medical licensing bodies.
- Biologics & Prescriptions: Requires transferring your biologic authorization to BC PharmaCare or BC private benefits, and establishing care with a Vancouver gastroenterologist or infusion center.
- CRA Tax Reality: Saves roughly $7,500 to $8,000 per year in provincial taxes on a $250k salary. However, CRA Income Tax Folio S5-F1-C1 classifies a spouse and family home as Primary Residential Ties. CRA may audit and deem you a factual NB resident, forcing you to repay the tax difference in the spring unless you carefully document your separate BC household.

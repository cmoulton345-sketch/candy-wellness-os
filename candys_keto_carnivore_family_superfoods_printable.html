  • ⚠️ 3-Day-Old Fridge Leftovers & 24-Hr Bone Broth: Histamine grows on cooked meat sitting in the fridge! Fix: Cook fresh or freeze extra cooked meat portions right away!

  • ⚠️ Fermented Foods & Vinegars: Sauerkraut, Kimchi, Kombucha, Soy Sauce, Balsamic Vinegar (Use Coconut Aminos or Apple Cider Vinegar/Olive Oil).
